Homestyle Chicken Noodle Soup
A comforting chicken noodle soup that's ready in under an hour. My family asks for it every time someone has a cold.

Prep Time: 15 mins
Cook Time: 35 mins
Total Time: 50 mins
Servings: 6

Ingredients
1 tablespoon olive oil
1 medium onion, diced
2 carrots, peeled and sliced
2 stalks celery, sliced
3 cloves garlic, minced
8 cups chicken broth
1 pound boneless skinless chicken breasts
1 teaspoon dried thyme
2 bay leaves
3 cups egg noodles
2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
salt and ground black pepper to taste

Directions
Step 1
Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion, carrots and celery; cook and stir until onion is translucent, about 5 minutes. Stir in garlic and cook for 1 minute more.
Step 2
Pour in chicken broth, add chicken breasts, thyme and bay leaves. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer until chicken is no longer pink in the center, about 20 minutes.
Step 3
Remove chicken to a cutting board and shred with two forks. Discard bay leaves.
Step 4
Return chicken to the pot, add egg noodles and cook until tender, 6 to 8 minutes. Stir in parsley and season with salt and pepper.
