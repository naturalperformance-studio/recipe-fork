Lemony Chicken and Rice Soup
Bright, brothy and gentle on the stomach — I lean on this one all winter. Leftovers thicken overnight, so add a splash of water when you reheat.

Prep Time: 10 mins
Cook Time: 40 mins
Total Time: 50 mins
Servings: 5

Ingredients
2 tablespoons butter
1 leek, white and light green parts, thinly sliced
1 large carrot, diced
1 celery stalk, diced
2 cloves garlic, chopped
7 cups chicken stock
3 bone-in chicken thighs, skin removed
½ cup long-grain white rice
1 teaspoon dried dill
1 lemon, juiced
2 tablespoons chopped fresh dill
salt and pepper to taste

Directions
Step 1
Melt butter in a Dutch oven over medium heat. Add leek, carrot and celery and cook, stirring now and then, until the leek is soft, about 6 minutes. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds.
Step 2
Pour in stock, add chicken thighs and dried dill. Bring to a simmer, cover partway and cook gently for 25 minutes.
Step 3
Lift out the chicken, pull the meat off the bones and tear it into bite-sized pieces.
Step 4
Stir rice into the pot and simmer until tender, 15 to 18 minutes. Return the chicken, add lemon juice and fresh dill, and season with salt and pepper.
