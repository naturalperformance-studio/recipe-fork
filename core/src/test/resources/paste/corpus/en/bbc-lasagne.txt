Easy lasagne
A classic Italian-style lasagne with a rich beef ragù and creamy white sauce – perfect for a family dinner.

Prep: 30 mins
Cook: 45 mins - 1 hr
Easy
Serves 6

Ingredients
1 tbsp olive oil
1 onion, finely chopped
2 garlic cloves, crushed
500g beef mince
2 x 400g cans chopped tomatoes
2 tbsp tomato purée
1 tsp dried oregano
50g butter
50g plain flour
600ml milk
250g dried lasagne sheets
100g cheddar, grated

Method
STEP 1
Heat the oil in a large pan. Add the onion and cook for 8-10 mins until softened. Stir in the garlic, then add the mince and cook until browned all over.
STEP 2
Tip in the tomatoes, tomato purée and oregano. Season, then simmer for 20 mins until thickened.
STEP 3
Meanwhile, melt the butter in a saucepan, stir in the flour and cook for 1 min. Gradually whisk in the milk and cook, stirring, until you have a smooth, thick sauce.
STEP 4
Heat oven to 200C/180C fan/gas 6. Spoon a layer of ragù into a baking dish, top with lasagne sheets, then some white sauce. Repeat the layers, finishing with white sauce, and scatter over the cheese.
STEP 5
Bake for 40-45 mins until golden and bubbling. Leave to stand for 10 mins before serving.
