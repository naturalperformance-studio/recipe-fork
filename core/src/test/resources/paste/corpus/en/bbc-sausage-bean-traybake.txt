Sausage, fennel & butter bean traybake
A one-tray supper with crisp-skinned sausages, soft fennel and creamy beans in a sticky tomato glaze – barely any washing up.

Prep: 15 mins
Cook: 50 mins - 1 hr
Easy
Serves 4

Ingredients
8 pork sausages
2 fennel bulbs, cut into wedges
1 red onion, cut into wedges
2 tbsp olive oil
1 tsp fennel seeds
400g can butter beans, drained
250g cherry tomatoes
1 tbsp red wine vinegar
1 tbsp honey
small bunch parsley, chopped

Method
STEP 1
Heat oven to 200C/180C fan/gas 6. Put the fennel and onion in a large roasting tin, toss with the oil and fennel seeds and season well. Roast for 20 mins.
STEP 2
Push the veg to the sides, add the sausages and roast for 15 mins more, until they start to brown.
STEP 3
Tip in the butter beans and tomatoes, drizzle over the vinegar and honey and give everything a shake. Return to the oven for 15-20 mins until the tomatoes burst and the sausages are cooked through.
STEP 4
Scatter with parsley and serve straight from the tin.
