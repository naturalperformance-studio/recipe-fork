## Salted Tahini Oat Cookies

Makes about 18 cookies

### Ingredients

* 1/2 cup (120 g) tahini, well stirred
* 1/3 cup maple syrup
* 3 tbsp coconut sugar
* 1 egg
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* 1 1/4 cups rolled oats
* 1/2 cup almond flour
* 1/2 tsp baking soda
* 1/4 tsp salt
* 1/3 cup dark chocolate chunks
* flaky salt, for topping

### Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. In a medium bowl, whisk the tahini, maple syrup, coconut sugar, egg and vanilla until glossy.
3. Stir in the oats, almond flour, baking soda and salt, then fold in the chocolate.
4. Let the dough sit for 10 minutes so the oats soak up some moisture.
5. Roll into balls the size of a walnut, flatten slightly on the sheet and sprinkle with flaky salt.
6. Bake for 11 to 13 minutes, until the edges are golden. Leave on the sheet for 10 minutes – they firm up as they cool.

### Notes

They keep for 5 days in a tin, and the dough balls freeze well.
