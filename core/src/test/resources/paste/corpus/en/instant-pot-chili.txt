Instant Pot Beef Chili

Prep 10 minutes · Cook 35 minutes · Serves 6

You'll need:
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 lbs ground beef
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 1 green bell pepper, chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 (15 oz) can kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup beef broth
- 1 tsp salt
- sour cream and shredded cheddar for serving

Instructions:
1. Set the Instant Pot to Sauté. Add the oil, then the ground beef, and cook for 5-6 minutes, breaking it up, until no longer pink. Drain excess fat.
2. Add the onion, bell pepper and garlic and sauté for 3 minutes more.
3. Stir in the chili powder, cumin and paprika and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
4. Add the beans, tomatoes, broth and salt. Stir well, scraping the bottom of the pot so you don't get a BURN notice.
5. Lock the lid, set the valve to Sealing and pressure cook on High for 15 minutes.
6. Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then do a quick release of the remaining pressure.
7. Serve topped with sour cream and cheddar.
