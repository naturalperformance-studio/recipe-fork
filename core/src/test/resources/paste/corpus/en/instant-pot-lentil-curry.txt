Instant Pot Red Lentil and Spinach Curry

Prep 10 minutes · Cook 25 minutes · Serves 4

You'll need:
- 2 tbsp coconut oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, grated
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- 1 1/2 cups red lentils, rinsed
- 1 (14 oz) can coconut milk
- 2 1/2 cups water
- 1 tsp salt
- 3 cups baby spinach
- juice of half a lime
- rice or naan for serving

Instructions:
1. Press Sauté and melt the coconut oil. Cook the onion for 4 minutes, stirring, until soft.
2. Add the garlic, ginger and spices and stir for 1 minute.
3. Add the lentils, coconut milk, water and salt and scrape the bottom well so nothing sticks.
4. Close the lid, turn the valve to Sealing and cook on Manual / High Pressure for 8 minutes.
5. Allow a natural release for 10 minutes, then release the rest of the pressure carefully.
6. Stir in the spinach until it wilts, add the lime juice and serve over rice.
