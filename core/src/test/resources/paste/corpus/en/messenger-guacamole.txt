ok here's the guac you liked on saturday 🥑

2 big avocados
1 lime
quarter of a red onion
6 cherry tomatoes
1 green chili
a handful of coriander
1/2 tsp salt

Chop the onion, tomatoes, chili and coriander as small as you can be bothered to.
Mash the avocados in a bowl with a fork, leave it a bit chunky, squeeze in the lime and add the salt.
Stir everything together and taste, it usually wants more lime.
Press cling film right onto the top and put it in the fridge for 20 min, it's better a bit cold!
