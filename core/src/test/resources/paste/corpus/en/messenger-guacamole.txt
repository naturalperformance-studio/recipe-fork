ok here's my guac recipe you asked for 🥑

3 avocados
1 lime
half a red onion
1 tomato
1 jalapeño
handful of cilantro
1/2 tsp salt

Chop the onion, tomato, jalapeño and cilantro really small.
Mash the avocados with a fork in a bowl, squeeze in the lime juice and add the salt.
Mix everything in and taste — more lime or salt if it needs it.
Cover with plastic touching the surface and chill for 30 min before serving, it gets better!
