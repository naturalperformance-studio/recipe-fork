Braised Chickpeas With Greens and Preserved Lemon
Yield: 4 servings
Total time: 45 minutes

Canned chickpeas turn silky after a slow braise in garlicky oil, and a little preserved lemon keeps the pot from tasting heavy. Spoon it over toast, rice or a pile of yogurt.

INGREDIENTS
¼ cup olive oil, plus more for drizzling
1 large shallot, finely chopped
4 garlic cloves, smashed
1 teaspoon ground coriander
½ teaspoon red-pepper flakes
2 (15-ounce) cans chickpeas, rinsed
1 ½ cups vegetable broth
1 bunch Swiss chard, stems sliced, leaves torn
1 small preserved lemon, rind only, finely chopped
½ teaspoon kosher salt, plus more to taste
Plain whole-milk yogurt, for serving

PREPARATION
Step 1
In a wide, heavy pot, warm the oil over medium. Add shallot and garlic and cook, stirring, until the shallot is translucent, 4 to 5 minutes. Stir in coriander and red-pepper flakes and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
Step 2
Add chickpeas, chard stems, broth and salt. Bring to a simmer, then lower the heat and cook, uncovered, until the liquid has reduced by about half and some chickpeas have begun to break down, 20 to 25 minutes.
Step 3
Stir in chard leaves and preserved lemon and cook until the leaves wilt, 3 minutes. Taste and add salt if needed.
Step 4
Serve with a spoonful of yogurt and a drizzle of oil.
