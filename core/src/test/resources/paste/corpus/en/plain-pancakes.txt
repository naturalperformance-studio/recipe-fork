Fluffy pancakes

1 1/2 cups flour
3 1/2 tsp baking powder
1 tbsp sugar
1/4 tsp salt
1 1/4 cups milk
1 egg
3 tbsp butter, melted

Sift the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt into a large bowl.
Make a well in the center and pour in the milk, egg and melted butter; mix until smooth.
Let the batter rest for 5 minutes.
Heat a lightly oiled frying pan over medium high heat. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter for each pancake.
Cook until bubbles appear on the surface, about 2 minutes, flip and cook for 1 minute more until golden.
