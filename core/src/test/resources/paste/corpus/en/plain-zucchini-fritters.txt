Zucchini and feta fritters

2 medium zucchini
1 tsp salt
2 spring onions
100 g feta
1 egg
5 tbsp flour
1/2 tsp baking powder
2 tbsp chopped mint
3 tbsp oil for frying

Grate the zucchini on the coarse side of a grater, mix with the salt and leave in a sieve for 15 minutes.
Squeeze out as much water as you can with your hands or a clean towel.
Slice the spring onions, crumble the feta and mix everything with the egg, flour, baking powder and mint.
Heat the oil in a frying pan and fry spoonfuls of the batter for 3 minutes on each side until deep golden.
Drain on paper towels and eat warm with a yogurt dip.
