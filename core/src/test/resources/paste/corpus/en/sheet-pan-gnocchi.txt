Sheet Pan Gnocchi with Peppers and Halloumi

Serves: 3
Prep time: 10 minutes
Cook time: 25 minutes

INGREDIENTS
1 lb shelf-stable potato gnocchi
2 bell peppers, cut into strips
1 small red onion, cut into wedges
1 cup cherry tomatoes
3 tbsp olive oil
1 tsp smoked paprika
1/2 tsp garlic powder
1/2 tsp salt
8 oz halloumi, cubed
2 tbsp basil pesto

DIRECTIONS
1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the gnocchi, peppers, onion and tomatoes with the oil, paprika, garlic powder and salt on a large sheet pan.
2. Roast for 15 minutes, then stir and scatter the halloumi over the top.
3. Roast for 10 to 12 minutes more, until the gnocchi are crisp and the halloumi is browned at the edges.
4. Spoon the pesto over everything and serve straight away.
