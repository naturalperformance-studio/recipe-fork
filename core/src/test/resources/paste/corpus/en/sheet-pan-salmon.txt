Sheet Pan Salmon with Potatoes and Green Beans

Serves: 4
Prep time: 10 minutes
Cook time: 30 minutes

INGREDIENTS
1 1/2 lb baby potatoes, halved
3 tbsp olive oil, divided
1 tsp salt, divided
1/2 tsp black pepper
4 salmon fillets (6 oz each)
12 oz green beans, trimmed
2 tbsp honey
1 tbsp Dijon mustard
2 garlic cloves, minced
1 lemon, sliced

DIRECTIONS
1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss the potatoes with 2 tbsp of the oil, half the salt and the pepper on a large sheet pan. Roast for 15 minutes.
2. Meanwhile whisk the honey, mustard, garlic and the rest of the oil in a small bowl.
3. Push the potatoes to the sides, add the green beans and lay the salmon in the middle. Brush the salmon with the honey mustard glaze, sprinkle everything with the remaining salt and top with lemon slices.
4. Roast for 12 to 15 minutes more, until the salmon flakes easily with a fork.
