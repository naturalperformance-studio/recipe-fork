Brown Butter Banana Bread
This brown butter banana bread is moist, tender and packed with flavor. The perfect way to use up overripe bananas!
Print Recipe Pin Recipe
Prep Time 15 mins
Cook Time 1 hr
Cooling 20 mins
Total Time 1 hr 35 mins
Course Breakfast, Snack
Cuisine American
Servings 10 slices

Equipment
▢ 9x5 inch loaf pan
▢ Mixing bowl

Ingredients
For the bread
▢ ½ cup unsalted butter
▢ 3 ripe bananas mashed (about 1 ½ cups)
▢ ¾ cup brown sugar packed
▢ 2 large eggs room temperature
▢ 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
▢ 1 ¾ cups all-purpose flour
▢ 1 teaspoon baking soda
▢ ½ teaspoon salt
▢ ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
For the topping
▢ 2 tablespoons turbinado sugar
▢ ⅓ cup chopped walnuts optional

Instructions
Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9x5 inch loaf pan and line it with parchment paper.
Melt the butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Keep cooking, swirling the pan, for 5-7 minutes until it smells nutty and turns golden brown. Pour into a large bowl and let cool for 10 minutes.
Whisk the mashed bananas, brown sugar, eggs and vanilla into the browned butter.
In another bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, salt and cinnamon. Fold the dry ingredients into the wet ones until just combined.
Pour the batter into the prepared pan, sprinkle with the walnuts and turbinado sugar.
Bake for 55-60 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Cool in the pan for 20 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack.

Notes
Banana bread keeps at room temperature, wrapped, for up to 3 days.
