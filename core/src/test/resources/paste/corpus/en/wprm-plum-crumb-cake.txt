Cardamom Plum Crumb Cake
A soft buttermilk cake under a layer of tart plums and a crunchy cardamom crumb. We make it every September when the neighbours' tree drops more plums than anyone can eat.
Print Recipe Pin Recipe
Prep Time 25 mins
Cook Time 50 mins
Total Time 1 hr 15 mins
Course Dessert
Cuisine Scandinavian-ish
Servings 12 squares

Equipment
▢ 8x8 inch square baking pan
▢ Stand mixer

Ingredients
For the crumb
▢ ⅔ cup all-purpose flour
▢ ⅓ cup light brown sugar
▢ ¾ teaspoon ground cardamom
▢ 5 tablespoons cold butter cubed
For the cake
▢ 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
▢ 1 ½ teaspoons baking powder
▢ ¼ teaspoon baking soda
▢ ¼ teaspoon fine salt
▢ 6 tablespoons butter softened
▢ ½ cup granulated sugar
▢ 1 large egg
▢ ⅔ cup buttermilk
▢ 1 teaspoon orange zest
▢ 6 plums pitted and cut into eighths

Instructions
Heat the oven to 350°F and butter the pan, then line the bottom with a square of parchment.
Make the crumb: rub the flour, brown sugar, cardamom and cold butter together with your fingertips until it forms pea-sized clumps. Put it in the fridge while you make the cake.
Whisk the flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt in a bowl.
In the stand mixer, beat the softened butter and sugar for 3 minutes until pale. Beat in the egg and orange zest.
Add the dry ingredients in two parts, alternating with the buttermilk, mixing on low just until no flour shows.
Spread the batter in the pan, press the plum pieces into it skin side up and scatter the crumb over everything.
Bake for 45-50 minutes, until the crumb is golden and a skewer comes out with only plum juice on it. Let it cool in the pan for 30 minutes before cutting.

Notes
Any stone fruit works; apricots need about 5 minutes less.
