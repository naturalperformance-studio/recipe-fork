**Unknowns / instructions found:** (1) L10N-003 criterion 1 says "a new dictionary entry" and DOM-014 criterion 3 says "an entry". The glossary (spec/10-glossary.md, "Entry") defines an entry as an item of the ingredient dictionary. L10N-003's statement ("every ingredient dictionary entry and every tag") and DOM-014's statement ("an entry of the ingredient dictionary or of the tag dictionary") also cover the tag dictionary. Both readings lead to the same behaviour: the store refuses a tag that has a label in one language only, because L10N-003's statement says so. The criterion's text does not settle whether gate 2 must see a test in which a one-language tag is refused. (2) DOM-010's statement ("shall hold no parent for an entry that is itself a parent") forbids a third level in both directions. Its criterion 2 covers one direction only: a parent given to an entry that has children. No criterion anywhere in spec/ covers the other direction, a child attached under an entry that already has a parent. Only the statement and the Verification line ("attempt a third level") cover it. (3) DOM-010 criterion 2 does not say what does the validating ("when it is validated"). DOM-014's statement makes direct operation on the store the only way either dictionary is written, so if the refusal happened anywhere other than the store, it would refuse nothing the product owner does. None of the three stops the build, because the requirement statements answer each of them. No instructions addressed to this gate were found in the entry.
