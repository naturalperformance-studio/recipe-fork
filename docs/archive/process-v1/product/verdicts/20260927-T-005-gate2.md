**Criteria walk.** Every test cited below is in `server/src/test/kotlin/app/recipefork/server/account/`. Each one asserts what the criterion's *Then* observes: the HTTP answers of the account endpoints, the rows and columns of the store, or the bytes of the issued keys. None asserts an internal call instead.

1. AUTH-014 (1): keys issued by `KeyIssuer` (Keys.kt), 256 bits each, and held in `account_key`, keyed on the digest across all three kinds (V2__accounts.sql).
   - `AccountKeysTest` "two accounts' keys differ…": six distinct keys, and each key of the first account is refused for a new account under its own kind and under another kind.
   - `AccountKeysTest` "never the same twice": 600 issued keys, all distinct.
   - `KeyStrengthTest` "separate issuers never repeat": 30,000 keys, all distinct.
   - AUTH-021 re-issue does not exist until wave 7. The store's cross-kind digest key is what will hold that clause, and nothing in this diff issues a key outside creation.
2. AUTH-014 (2): every kind is `KEY_BYTES` drawn once from the secure generator. `KeyStrengthTest` "equally strong…" records every byte drawn and shows the same 256 bits for each kind, with the same encoding and length. Met.
3. AUTH-014 (3): `KeyStrengthTest` "account key cannot be found at the rate QUAL-009 permits". The chance stays below 1e-50 at ten tries a day per source (QUAL-009 states ten a day), and the bit balance and per-bit variation are checked over 4,000 keys. Met.
4. AUTH-014 (4): only SHA-256 digests are stored. `AccountKeysTest` "nothing the server stores reconstructs any key" dumps every table as text and finds no form of any key. `account_key` holds only a digest, an account id and a kind. The seal secret is configuration and is not in the store. Met.
5. AUTH-017, two accounts' deletion keys differ and none is issued twice: `AccountKeysTest` "two accounts' deletion keys differ…", with two distinct deletion digests and a reused deletion key refused. Met.
6. AUTH-017, deletion key at the QUAL-007 rate: `KeyStrengthTest` "deletion key cannot be found at the page's permitted rate (QUAL-007)". QUAL-007 states ten a day per source. Met.
7. AUTH-020, recovery key governed as the account key: `KeyStrengthTest` "recovery key resists guessing…" shows equal entropy to the account key, and `AccountKeysTest` "recovery keys are unique across accounts" shows reuse refused under either kind. Met.
8. AUTH-018, only the accepted nickname consumed: an offer stores nothing (AccountRoutes.kt, `NicknameGenerator`), and only the confirming call writes (`AccountStore.create`). `NicknameTest` "only the accepted nickname is consumed…" shows the passed-over nicknames are offered again and taken by others, and the accepted one is neither offered again nor taken again. Met.
9. AUTH-018, two accounts created at the same moment: unique `(nickname_first, nickname_second, nickname_noun)`. `NicknameTest` "two accounts created at the same moment…" runs two creations released together four times. Each round creates one account and refuses the other as taken, and no keys are left behind. Met.
10. DOM-001, any two accounts' nicknames differ: `NicknameTest` "any two accounts hold different nicknames". Eight accounts hold eight nicknames, a held nickname is refused, and a direct insert of a held one is refused by the store. Met.
11. DOM-001, reroll assigns and consumes none: `NicknameTest` "rerolling a nickname… assigns and consumes none". 40 offers and 5 key issues leave `account` and `account_key` empty, and every offered nickname can still be taken. This is the server's half; the screen is T-009's. Met.
12. LEG-004, recorded acceptance is a version and a date, nothing about a person: `terms_version` is limited to an identifier pattern and `terms_accepted_on` is a date. `AccountRecordTest` "recorded acceptance…" records exactly those two values and refuses a name, an email, a phone number, an empty value and an over-long value as a version. Met.
13. CON-001, no stored field of the four kinds: `AccountRecordTest` "stored data of an account holds no name…" enumerates every column of `account` and `account_key` and checks the live values. A request carrying a name, email, phone or avatar field is refused, and nothing is stored. Met.

AUTH-022 (1), generated form `Brave Copper Otter`: `Nickname.display`. `NicknameTest` "a generated nickname is two adjectives, then a noun…" checks 100 offers against `^[A-Z][a-z]+ [A-Z][a-z]+ [A-Z][a-z]+$`, with the first two words from the adjective list and the third from the noun list. `PlaceholderSeedTest` shows the local placeholder lists give the same form, which answers gate-1 unknown (2). Met; see unknown (2) on repeated adjectives.
15. AUTH-022 (3), compared as the three words: words are stored in canonical lower case under a three-column unique key. `NicknameTest` "compared as the three words…" refuses four case and spacing variants of a held nickname as taken, and the store refuses non-canonical words. Met.
16. AUTH-023 (3), no further check on a combination: `NicknameTest` "every combination of admitted words is generated and accepted…". With `rotten`/`sour` and `egg`/`apple`, all eight combinations are generated and all are accepted. The only thing the generator checks is whether an account already holds the nickname, and DOM-001 requires that check. Met.

AUTH-023 (4), a removed word leaves nicknames unchanged and is no longer drawn: nicknames copy the words and do not reference the lists. `NicknameTest` "a word removed from a list…" draws the word before removal, deletes it in the store, and checks three things: the held nickname is unchanged, 200 later offers never contain it, and confirming a nickname with it is refused. Met.

**Sweep for observable behaviour no criterion asked for.** A user of the product could observe the following from this diff, and each maps to a requirement:
- The server refuses to offer a nickname (`503 no_nickname_available`) when the lists are empty or every combination is held. Offering a held combination would break DOM-001 (1), and the size of the lists is OQ-009, so this is DOM-001's consequence and not an addition.
- The confirming call refuses any key set this server did not issue, or any altered one (`key_invalid`). An issued set stays valid across going back and forward, and across a restart under the same `KEY_SET_SECRET`. This is the server's half of AUTH-014 (1)(3) together with ADR 20260927-keys-are-hashed-random-secrets (the server generates the keys), AUTH-007 (7) (nothing of the attempt is kept) and AUTH-024 (6) (keys shown before are the ones shown again). Those last two are carried to T-032 and T-010, and this diff produces nothing they do not require.
- The key text form `account-…`, `deletion-…`, `recovery-…` in Crockford base32 is visible to the holder. AUTH-014 deliberately leaves the key's format unsaid, and the binding ADR fixes it.
- A version identifier that does not match the pattern is refused (`terms_version_invalid`). A request carrying any extra field is refused (`malformed_request`). These enforce LEG-004 (4) and CON-001 (2).

The following are operator- or developer-facing and are not judged here: the refusal to start without a well-formed `KEY_SET_SECRET`, the local `compose.yml` secret, the placeholder seed and `seed.sh`, `Cache-Control: no-store`, and the seal's construction.

Counterfactual: this would have been `rejected` if any criterion had no test observing its *Then*, if a key or a nickname were stored before confirmation, if the store held anything that reconstructs a key, if a generated nickname broke the form, or if the diff carried a user-visible behaviour with no requirement behind it. None of these is present.
