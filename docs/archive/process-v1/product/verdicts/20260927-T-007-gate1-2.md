**Failed check:** C3 — on the trigger the task carries, the task describes less than the requirement states.
T-007 carries FLOW-034 (1): "Given an account that has marked recipes and owns recipes, when it opens the section, then both views are reachable from it and the switch between them is visible without scrolling". So T-007 builds the favourites section, both of its views and the switch. It also carries FLOW-034 (5), which keeps a chosen view. It sends FLOW-034 (6) and (7) to wave 3. Those two criteria state what the same trigger, opening the section, produces in the cases every wave-1 device is in:
- (6) "Given a session in which the reader has not chosen a view, when they open the section, then it opens on the view of marked recipes, whatever the account holds."
- (7) "Given a device holding no account, when the section is opened, then it shows the view of marked recipes alone and no switch; the own-recipes view and the switch appear once the device holds an account."

Deferring these two criteria does not defer the behaviour. From the day T-007 lands, the section opens on some view and is shown on devices with no account. Every client device is such a device until T-010. The developer receives only this entry's slice (TL-008), so the developer would choose both things again. These are the two decisions SD-004 raised and the user settled on 2026-09-27. An implementation that opens on the own-recipes view, or that shows the switch on a device with no account, meets every criterion in this entry. It still contradicts live FLOW-034 (6) and (7) and the permissions table of ACT-001 (5). Gate 2 walks the entry's criteria and could not reject it on them.

Reading under which the entry would pass: the backlog lets a requirement's criteria be split between tasks, and wave 3 is where marking recipes (FLOW-009) arrives. That reading does not hold here. Criteria (6) and (7) are not about marked recipes existing. They are about how the section T-007 builds opens, and about who sees the switch T-007 builds. Both are observable as soon as T-007 is on `develop`.

How the entry is corrected, whether by carrying FLOW-034 (6) and (7) in this task or by slicing the section differently, is the Team Lead's decision. No spec defect is owed: `spec/` states the behaviour, and the task omits it.
