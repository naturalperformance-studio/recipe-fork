(2) FLOW-022 (3) names no route by which a free-text ingredient is replaced by an entry; the criterion is observable as stated and any route that achieves it passes. (3) The divisibility question of FLOW-022 (5) and FLOW-001 (3). The ADR `spec/decisions/20260920-divisibility-belongs-to-the-ingredient.md` keeps it a question ("that question stays") and rejects a row toggle, so it is not a fourth field of the row (FLOW-023 (1) at gate 2). (4) FLOW-057 (2) opens with "the same dictionary"; its referent is FLOW-057 (1), carried by T-031 — a dictionary holding `молоко`. The criterion is copied verbatim as C5 requires; the developer's slice needs FLOW-057 (1) beside it to read it. (5) FLOW-057's statement offers free text "whenever it is not exactly the label of an offered entry"; nothing states what the field shows for empty or whitespace-only input, and no criterion carried here or in T-031 observes it. Not a defect: the task can be built and gated without it.
