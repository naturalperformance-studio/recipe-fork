**What stops it now.** T-014 is the task that puts a duration field in the editor:
FLOW-027 (1) — "when the author attaches a timer and enters 30 minutes" — (4) — "a
duration is the only thing required of them" — and (11) — "Given a duration the
author has not entered, when the recipe is saved, then the timer is not stored". What
that field accepts is stated nowhere in `spec/`: the domain model types `duration` as
"duration" with an empty notes column; the glossary says only "a duration attached
inside one step"; DOM-012 bounds the timer's name and not its duration; no ADR under
`spec/decisions/` and no entry of `spec/open-questions.md` addresses it. Whether the
author enters minutes only, or hours, minutes and seconds; whether 0 is a duration
"entered" (and so stored) or the absence FLOW-027 (11) drops; whether a ceiling
exists — each is visible on the editor and each is decided by whoever writes the
field. Gate 2 could not tell a right field from a wrong one (TEAM-008). This is the
same class of gap SD-013 recorded for the authored serving count, which the product
owner answered with DOM-015. The reading under which the task would be accepted —
"duration" is a duration, so any is admissible — does not hold without deciding the
product: it admits a zero-length timer and says nothing about the unit the author
types in.

A second item, of the ambiguity class, is recorded in the same entry rather than
left for a later round: GUIDE-007 (4)'s trigger, "when the author takes their next
action in the editor", is defined nowhere (not in GUIDE-005, the glossary's "Hint",
or the ADR of 2026-09-27, which repeats "the author's next action"). A build that
keeps the statement through a scroll and one that removes it on a scroll are each
right under one reading. The reading also decides whether a screen-reader user can
hear the statement at all: if moving the reader's focus onto it is an action, it is
gone when reached. The gate does not choose either reading.
