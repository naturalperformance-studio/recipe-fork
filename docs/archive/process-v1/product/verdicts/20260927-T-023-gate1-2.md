**Failed check:** C3 — the task builds a timer control on the cooking card and does not
carry the criteria that state that control's size.
FLOW-013 (7): "Given a running timer, when the reader cancels it, then nothing announces
itself afterwards." Its trigger is an act of the reader, so the task that carries it
builds the control that performs it. T-022's accepted gate 1 records (unknown 4) that a
cancel control built in T-022 would be an addition there, since T-022 does not carry
(7); the entry now lists `app/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/card/**` and its
strings for that reason. The cancel control is therefore T-023's, and it is one of "the
timer controls" of the cooking card.

A11Y-003 (2): "Given the cooking card, when its controls are measured — the timer
controls and the serving control — then each is at least 56 by 56 dp." A11Y-007 (2):
"Given the cooking card, when its controls are measured, then A11Y-003's larger figure
holds." T-023 carries A11Y-003 (1) alone — 48 by 48 dp — and does not name A11Y-007. Its
carried-elsewhere line sends A11Y-003 (2) to T-018, and T-022 also carries it; both are
gated at gate 2 before the cancel control exists, so neither can measure it. As sliced,
a cancel control of 48 by 48 dp meets every criterion at T-023's gate 2 and is measured
against 56 by no task at any gate. What the task gates is narrower than what the
requirements state for the behaviour it builds.

The reading under which the task would pass — that A11Y-003 (2) as carried by T-018 or
T-022 covers the card's timer controls in general — does not hold: a criterion is walked
against the diff of the task that carries it, and neither diff holds this control. The
reading that cancelling is offered off the card removes the finding only by adding a
surface no requirement names (unknown 1).
