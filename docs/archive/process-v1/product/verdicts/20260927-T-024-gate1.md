**Failed check:** C3 — the task is narrower than the requirements state for the controls it adds to the cooking card.
FLOW-021 (1–3) makes the reader, on starting a running timer again, "told it is running and ...
asked whether to restart it", then "confirms" or "declines". That question is answered with
interactive elements, and T-024 is the task that adds them; it raises them on the cooking card,
from a timer control, about a timer on that card. For those elements the entry carries only
A11Y-003 (1), the 48 by 48 dp rule for any screen. The specification sets a larger figure there:
A11Y-003's statement — "at least 48 by 48 density-independent pixels, and at least 56 by 56 on the
cooking card"; A11Y-003 (2) — "Given the cooking card, when its controls are measured — the timer
controls and the serving control — then each is at least 56 by 56 dp."; A11Y-007's statement —
"shall give its controls the larger targets of A11Y-003"; A11Y-007 (2) — "Given the cooking card,
when its controls are measured, then A11Y-003's larger figure holds." T-024 names neither A11Y-003 (2)
nor A11Y-007 (2). T-022, which carries both, precedes T-024 and cannot measure controls that do not
exist yet at its gate 2; the coverage map places neither anywhere else. As sliced, confirm and
decline buttons of 48 dp meet every criterion T-024 carries and fail A11Y-003 (2) unobserved.

The reading under which the task would pass — that the restart question is a surface of its own,
not part of the cooking card, so only the 48 dp rule applies — does not hold: A11Y-003 binds by place
("on the cooking card"), not by kind of widget; the card is one scrolling screen (introduction of
`spec/70-flows/30-cooking.md`), and the question exists only there, raised by and answering for a timer
control; no requirement exempts a question shown over the card; and A11Y-007's source — a card used
"at arm's length ... by someone whose hands are wet or covered in flour" — is exactly the situation in
which the reader answers it. Choosing the narrower reading would be the gate narrowing a requirement.
The criteria to add already exist in `spec/` and are copied verbatim like the rest (C5); no new
requirement is needed.
