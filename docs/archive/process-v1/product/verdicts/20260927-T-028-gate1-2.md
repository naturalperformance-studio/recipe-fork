**Failed check:** C3 — the task's sending is wider than `spec/` states for a muted
account, which wave 1 creates and lets write.
The task carries FLOW-002 (4) verbatim — "Given changes made offline, when a connection
returns, then they are sent to the server without the owner asking for it" — with no
account kind excepted, and builds the outbox that does the sending. `spec/` withholds
exactly this from a muted account: AUTH-004's statement ("A muted account shall send no
recipe to the server") and its criterion (4) ("recipes can be written, edited and
deleted, they are held on the device alone and nothing of them reaches the server").
A muted account exists in wave 1: T-006 creates one when no verdict can be obtained,
the editor opens for any account from the own-recipes view (T-008), and the write half
of AUTH-004 (5) is being settled into T-026, on which this task depends. The entry's
second note concedes the gap: "Until then no wave-1 criterion checks that this task's
outbox holds a muted account's recipes back."
The consequence is that gate 2 of this task cannot tell a right outbox from a wrong
one. An outbox that sends a muted account's recipes meets every criterion the entry
carries and contradicts a live requirement. An outbox that holds them back, which is
what AUTH-004 states, fails FLOW-002 (4) as carried when the walk takes a muted
account's offline change, or shows behaviour no carried criterion asks for. Either
way the decision falls to the developer where no gate can check it (TEAM-008), and
the correction arrives in wave 6 as a change to this task's files.
The counterfactual reading under which the entry passes — that splitting AUTH-004 (4)
to wave 6 is ordinary slicing and FLOW-002 (4) is faithful for the owner of a full
account — does not hold, because the entry does not limit FLOW-002 (4) to a full
account and gate 2 walks each carried criterion as written.
What clears it is the Team Lead's to choose, and it is slicing, not a product
decision: the entry names AUTH-004 and carries the observation that a muted account's
recipes do not reach the server, verbatim, or the sending of this task is otherwise
placed where that observation is carried with it.
