the telling to "when the owner opens the application on device B". The task's own
FLOW-002 (4) sends changes by itself whenever a connection returns, and the binding ADR

stated nowhere. (2) FLOW-005 (4) marks a fully accepted recipe "when it is displayed" and
names no surface; (1) names the own-recipes list and (2) the editor only for unaccepted
changes, while an own recipe is also displayed on its card (FLOW-029, FLOW-053) and, once
published, in the catalogue. The task's expected files reach the list and the editor and
not the card, which is the narrow reading chosen silently; the spec does not say it is
the right one. Both are product decisions the developer would otherwise make where gate 2
could not tell. Not `rejected`: the task copies the requirements as they are, and a
corrected task would read the same.
