**Failed check:** C3 — the cut the task builds is not the cut its requirement states.

GUIDE-006 (1) takes its *Then* from another requirement: "what is shown is the text the
notification will carry at the authored serving count, cut exactly as FLOW-016 cuts it, with
the ellipsis". GUIDE-006 (4) does the same with "(FLOW-016)". The rule of that cut is stated
in FLOW-016 alone, in three criteria:
- (3): "the first 60 characters of its step's text, cut at a word boundary, with an ellipsis
  where the text was longer";
- (4): a step of 60 characters or fewer is announced whole, "and nothing marks it as cut";
- (8): a reference in those 60 characters "reads there exactly as it reads on the card, at the
  scale the reader is using (FLOW-032)".

T-034 does not name FLOW-016. The developer agent receives only the requirements its task
names (TL-008). What it would receive instead is the entry's Note: "The cut FLOW-016 makes —
the first 60 characters at a word boundary, with an ellipsis — is built here in `:core`
`timer` … T-023 builds the notification from the same function." That paraphrase differs from
FLOW-016 in two ways:
- It drops (4). As written, it puts an ellipsis on every derived name. That is the case
  GUIDE-006 (6) exercises: a 55-character step opening with a reference is shown, and FLOW-016
  (4) does not cut it.
- It drops (8). It says nothing about how a reference inside the 60 characters is rendered
  before they are counted and cut, although GUIDE-006 (1), (2) and (6) are about exactly that
  step. The rendering (8) points to is FLOW-032's, on the card. Its criteria (1)–(4), including
  (2), "the value in the reference equals the value the ingredient list shows", are carried by
  T-021. T-034 does not depend on T-021, and T-021 is ordered after it.

The Note also says this task builds the function T-023 will announce with. The rule FLOW-016
states would then be fixed in code in a task that carries none of FLOW-016's criteria. T-023,
which carries FLOW-016 (3)–(8), would later be gated against a function that already exists.
The display T-034 would ship follows a narrower rule than the one its own criterion points to.

The reading under which the entry would pass: "(FLOW-016)" is only a cross-reference, and the
Note says enough. That reading does not hold. The Note is the Team Lead's wording, not the
specification's, and it states a different rule for steps of 60 characters or fewer. C5 forbids
exactly that kind of paraphrase for criteria, because the developer cannot tell it from the
requirement. The rule also cannot arrive from earlier in the chain. T-014, whose FLOW-027 (5)
shows a derived name, does not name FLOW-016 either. That entry is stopped by SD-030, and the
Note places the cut here, not there.

The specification is not at fault. FLOW-016 at a given serving count reads determinately
(SD-015 found as much and is resolved), and GUIDE-006 now fixes that count as the authored
one. Two ways back to gate 1, and the Team Lead chooses between them:
- the entry names FLOW-016 and carries the criteria that fix the cut, with the order that puts
  FLOW-032's card rendering on `develop` before this task;
- the cut is built in a task that carries those criteria, and T-034 follows it.
