The computation starts from the exact factor, never from a rounded value, and no count is too large. At the authored count, the authored value is shown with no derived marker. The six statements describe the same behaviour on the same trigger. The question that stopped the third gate is now closed. By DOM-017, an authored quantity is always a grid value. SCALE-001's note and DOM-017 (7) state that at a ratio of 1 the grid value and the authored value are therefore the same. The one exception is DOM-018, which is placed with wave 4. The domain model states that the display follows the grid, "not by the unit the author chose", so an `exact` value entered in another unit of its dimension is also unambiguous. Every arithmetic criterion was recomputed against the grid:
  - 500 × 6/4 = 750 g.
  - 7 × 0.6 = 4.2, shown as 4 g.
  - 3 × 0.1 = 0.3 of an indivisible piece, held at the floor of 1.
  - 0.05 teaspoon, held at the floor of ¼.
  - 1 5⁄12 is the exact midpoint of 1⅓ and 1½, so it is shown as 1½.
