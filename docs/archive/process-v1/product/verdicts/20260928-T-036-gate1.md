**Unknowns / instructions found:** (1) The Note says "In this wave the pool holds recipes only: favourites arrive in wave 3." The gate reads it as a statement of what the running product can put into the pool in this wave. It does not read it as a limit on the move, and the same reading was applied to T-007's Note (`20260928-T-007-gate1.md`, unknown (2)). Two carried criteria mention more than recipes. AUTH-026 (1) has the Given "a pool holding recipes and favourites". AUTH-026 (2) has the Then "everything in the pool is that account's". So at gate 2, the evidence for (1) must be a test whose pool holds recipes and favourites. The evidence for (2) must be a test that shows every kind of row the pool's book holds at the merge moving into the account. It is not enough to show only the recipes moving. A move that handles recipes alone does not meet those criteria as worded. The question that would have been asked: is this the Team Lead's intended reading of the Note? (2) This is recorded for the Team Lead, not as a finding. It is about later coverage. AUTH-026 (1) and (2) are carried only here, and the backlog does not carry them again later. The coverage tables of waves 3 and 7 name no AUTH-026 criterion. Two parts of those criteria have no trigger in this task's product. One is the "signs in" half of (1), because signing in with a key is AUTH-009 in wave 7. The other is favourites and blocked authors in the move of (2), because they arrive in wave 3 and with ABUSE-005. Unless a later task carries those criteria again, nothing will observe those two parts when they arrive. (3) This is recorded for the Team Lead, not as a finding. In wave 1b the product has no way to return to "no account active". Continuing with no account is AUTH-011 (5), which is placed in wave 7. So the second half of AUTH-026 (3) and of FLOW-051 (6) cannot be reached in the running product until wave 7: "the pool is shown again whenever no account is active". A declined pool stays on the device but is not visible until then. At gate 2, the only possible evidence is a test that makes no account active and observes the pool. (4) The menu action in AUTH-026 (6) is built by T-037, under AUTH-026 (4). Until then, the half of (6) that says the action is not offered holds trivially. T-037 carries AUTH-026 (4–5) but not (6). So once the action exists, no carried criterion checks that it is absent when the pool is empty. This is recorded for the Team Lead. (5) An account "offered once" is the title's promise and AUTH-026's statement. The criteria that observe it, (4) and (5), are in T-037. This is not a C3 failure. In this task's product, the only time an account becomes active is its creation, and that is the trigger of (1) and (6). The minimal implementation shows the offer there and nowhere else. An offer shown on any other trigger, such as a relaunch, would be behaviour that no carried criterion asks for, and the gate-2 sweep rejects that. (6) No instruction addressed to the gate was found in the backlog entry (docs/architecture/backlog.md, lines 1548–1586).
