**Failed check:** C3. None of the entry's criteria can be triggered through the product
on T-037's branch, and the route that the Note names needs behaviour this task carries
elsewhere.
Every behavioural criterion in the entry has a *Given* or *When* that needs a second
account on the device, or an account becoming active again after another one was
active:
- AUTH-011 (1–4) need two accounts signed in and a switch between them.
- AUTH-012 (3) needs "the active account changes".
- AUTH-026 (4) needs an account that "becomes active again later". AUTH-026 (5) needs
  "a second account that becomes active for the first time".
- AUTH-026 (6) needs "any account becomes active". Here it is the menu action's
  absence, so that also needs a later activation.

The Note (line 1578) says: "Two accounts reach one device in this wave by creating both
on it (a device has three slots, AUTH-002)". In `spec/` @ 21b80b2, creation is offered
only to "a person without an account" (AUTH-001 statement,
spec/45-auth/10-creating-an-account.md lines 16–20). The book's own offer of creation is
also given "Given no account" (FLOW-051 (7)). While account A is active, the person is
not without an account, and nothing in `spec/` offers creation from there. AUTH-011 (5)
is the one behaviour in `spec/` that leads from an active account back to no account
active: "Given the account list, when it is opened, then it also offers to continue with
no account, which shows the pool, and to sign in with another key (AUTH-009)."
(spec/45-auth/25-accounts-on-a-device.md, AUTH-011). The entry carries that criterion
to wave 7 in full (line 1579, and the wave-7 table, AUTH-011 | 5). No task in waves 1
and 1b carries it.

So at T-037's merge, a second account cannot come into being through behaviour that
`spec/` states and that this task or an earlier one carries. Gate 2 would then have
three options, and each one fails:
- The branch offers "continue with no account" without carrying AUTH-011 (5). The
  gate-2 sweep finds observable behaviour that no criterion of this task asks for.
- The branch offers a different route to a second account, such as a "create another
  account" action in the list. That behaviour maps to no requirement (C4 arriving late).
- The branch offers no route, and tests seed two accounts directly. Those tests observe
  a state that a user of this build cannot reach. The gate has held that such a test is
  not evidence for the criterion (docs/product/verdicts/20260928-T-009-gate1.md, on
  LEG-004 (1); docs/product/verdicts/20260928-T-008-gate1.md).

This is not a spec defect. `spec/` states the route in full: the account list offers to
continue with no account, and creation is offered to a person with no account. Only the
placement in the backlog leaves the route out of the task that needs it. Where AUTH-011
(5) goes, including the "sign in with another key" half that the entry ties to wave 7,
is slicing, and slicing is the Team Lead's. The gate names no form for it.

The same gap was recorded against T-036 as an unknown
(docs/product/verdicts/20260928-T-036-gate1.md, unknown (3)). There it touched only
half of one criterion, and a test could make no account active. Here it touches every
behavioural criterion of the task, and the task's own Note depends on it.

Counterfactual: T-037 would pass if its branch let a person who has account A active
reach creation of account B through behaviour that `spec/` states and that this task or
an earlier one carries. It would also pass if the entry's criteria could be observed
without a second account or a second activation. Neither is true, for the reasons above.
