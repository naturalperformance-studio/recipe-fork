**Where the behaviour is produced.** In `core/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/core/scaling/QuantityText.kt`, `QuantityText.of` writes a `DisplayedQuantity` in Russian or English:
- An `exact` value is taken to whole base units and written in g/ml below 1000, in kg/l from 1000, and in t/m³ from 1 000 000, to three places with trailing zeros dropped.
- A `household`, `taste_measured` or `piece` value is written as a whole number, a vulgar fraction from ¼ ⅓ ½ ⅔ ¾, or a whole number followed by one of them.
- Whole parts are grouped by three digits with U+00A0 in Russian and a comma in English. The decimal mark is a comma in Russian and a point in English.
- The label is the DOM-016 name in the interface language.
