**Walk, criterion 1**
- *Where it is produced.* `AccessibilityChecks.assertTouchTargets` (app/src/test/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/ui/AccessibilityChecks.kt) now reads the node's laid-out size (`node.size`), not `touchBoundsInRoot`. It reads the unmerged tree and selects every node that has an action the reader performs, or that is toggleable. It fails when the width or the height is under 48 dp. "Whatever the element looks like" is therefore measured on the layout, which Compose does not widen.
- *Tests that observe the Then.* There are two kinds.
  - On the screens: `LibraryScreenTest.everyInteractiveElementIsLargeEnoughAndAnnouncesWhatItDoes` and its Russian counterpart cover both library views, with a recipe present.
  - `EditorLanguageAndAccessibilityTest.inEnglish` and `inRussian` (lines 132–187) cover:
    - the empty own-recipes view
    - the editor on a new recipe with the tag offers
    - a tag chosen with a search that finds nothing
    - the language cleared
    - the frozen editor
  - Each of these states calls the check, which asserts at least 48 by 48 dp on every interactive element. These are the two screens that exist (AppNavHost.kt has no interactive element).
  - In AccessibilityChecksTest.kt, self-tests show that the assertion can fail on what the Then forbids:
    - a 24 dp clickable fails although Compose widens its touch bounds to 48 dp
    - 200 by 40 fails (height)
    - 40 by 200 fails (width, added by 164e4c3)
    - 48 by 48 passes
    - small elements nested in merging parents fail, as do small elements acted on only through custom actions, expand, dismiss or an IME action
- *The screens as laid out (gate-1 unknown (a)).* Every interactive element on both screens measures at least 48 by 48 dp:
  - The library tabs are `weight(1f)` and `heightIn(min = 48.dp)`.
  - "Write a recipe" is a Material `Button` with `heightIn(min = 48.dp)`.
  - `ActionText` (back, clear, remove) is `heightIn(48)` and `widthIn(48)`.
  - The language control is at least 96 by 56.
  - The offered tags fill the width and are `heightIn(48)`.
  - The text fields are `OutlinedTextField`s that fill the width and are at least 56 high.
  - Library entries are not clickable.
  - No element was found under 48 dp, so nothing was left as an open item.
