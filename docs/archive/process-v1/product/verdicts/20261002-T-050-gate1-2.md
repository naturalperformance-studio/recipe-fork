**C1.** The entry names A11Y-003.

**C2.** A11Y-003 is in `spec/200-accessibility.md`. It is not in `## Retired IDs`, and it has no `**Blocked by:**` line.

**C3.** The Note has the check select a scrolling container and measure it "across its scroll direction only". It measures a disabled control at its enabled size, and it leaves out text whose only actions are focus, selection or copying while selecting a link inside text. This is A11Y-003's definition, (1) as now worded, and (5)–(7). Since 71ea18a the two readings of SD-047 no longer split, and the entry's "only" is the requirement.

**C4.** Behaviours described, and the requirement behind each:
- a scrolling container selected and measured across its scroll direction only — A11Y-003 (1, 5), definition
- a disabled control measured at its enabled size, unchanged on enabling — A11Y-003 (6), definition
- text that only takes focus, selection or copying not measured; a copy control and a link inside text measured — A11Y-003 (7), definition
- the changed check run by the library and editor tests, with findings returned as an open item — process, not observable behaviour of the product
No bullet is left without a requirement.

**Failed check:** C5 — criterion 1 of the entry differs from the specification's wording.
The entry: "Given a container that scrolls, when it is measured across the direction it scrolls in, then it is at least 48 dp — a strip that scrolls sideways at least 48 dp tall, a list that scrolls down at least 48 dp wide."
`spec/200-accessibility.md`, A11Y-003 (5), at 71ea18a: "Given a container that scrolls, when it is measured across the direction it scrolls in, then it is at least 48 dp — a strip that scrolls sideways at least 48 dp tall, a list that scrolls down at least 48 dp wide — and its length along that direction is not measured."
The missing clause is the answer to SD-047. It is the half of the criterion that a self-test of "across the scroll direction only" asserts. Without it, the developer agent's slice of `spec/` and its task statement disagree on the one point the re-gate was for (TL-008).

Counterfactual: the entry would pass with A11Y-003 (5) recopied as it now stands. Criteria 2 and 3 match `spec/` word for word.
