# v0.1 — A recipe you can cook from, and its fork
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-02 · **Shipped:** —

Goal, scenarios and out-of-scope below were approved by the user on 2026-10-02.

## Goal
On the phone, without any server or account, the user writes a full recipe, opens it, changes the serving count and sees quantities recalculate, and forks it into an editable copy that names its parent.

## What exists at the start
On `develop` @ 6f9b218: the recipe book (own recipes and favourites lists); the editor with title, language and tags only; the scaling engine in `:core` (`ScaledRecipe`, `Scaling`, `RoundingGrid`, `QuantityText`) with no screen using it; the book database already stores a recipe's ingredients, steps and description in full (`RecipeRepository.save`/`get`), but nothing writes them; a server with accounts and dictionaries that the app does not call yet. A saved recipe cannot be opened, and "new recipe" is offered only while the book is empty. The shipped ingredient dictionary holds five entries and carries no divisibility.

## Demo scenarios
1. New recipe → add ingredients, some picked from the dictionary and one typed as free text, each with a quantity and unit → add three steps as plain text → back to the book: the recipe is in the list.
2. Close and reopen the app: the recipe and everything in it is still there.
3. Tap the recipe in the list → its card opens with ingredients and steps, at its own serving count.
4. On the card, change the serving count (e.g. 4 → 6, then 4 → 1): quantities recalculate and read sensibly (no 0.333 g of salt, no 7 eggs from 4.67).
5. On the card, fork → an editable copy opens in the editor; change an ingredient and save → both recipes are in the book, the original unchanged, and the fork's card says which recipe it came from.
6. Open your own recipe from the list → it opens as its card; the edit action in the card's top bar opens it in the editor (FLOW-029).

## Out of scope
- Timers, quantity references inside steps, reordering by drag, limits marking (T-014, T-015, T-016, T-017, T-022–T-025, T-033, T-034 in the archived backlog).
- Fixing an ingredient on the card, the rounding-direction note (T-019, T-020) — the engine supports them; the card does not show them yet.
- Server, sending, publication, accounts, moderation, catalogue, ratings, media.
- Accessibility and localisation sweeps (archived T-043, T-044 follow-ups, T-045, T-050); ordinary good practice inside each task is enough.

- The unit field offers exactly the twelve units of DOM-016, named in the interface language (DOM-016 (1), FLOW-023 (10)); the unit type stored follows from the unit (DOM-016 (2)).
- Choosing "to taste" withdraws the quantity field, keeps the unit field showing "to taste", and stores no quantity; choosing another unit afterwards brings the quantity field back empty (FLOW-023 (8, 9), DOM-002).
- In this task the name is typed as free text; adding it shows the warning of FLOW-022 stating both that filters will not find the recipe by this ingredient and that publication makes it permanent (FLOW-022 (2)).
- A `piece` row whose name is free text asks the author for divisibility (FLOW-022 (5)).
- A typed quantity reads a comma or a point as the decimal mark and refuses a digit grouping separator, with a message saying a number is written without separators (DOM-019 (1–5)); every example of DOM-019 (1–5) is a `:core` test.
- The quantity field holds only a value on its unit type's grid (DOM-017 (1–5)), displayed as the grid writes it (`0,5 ч. л.` → ½); every example of DOM-017 (1–4) is a `:core` test, for both a divisible and an indivisible piece.
- A value not held stays in the field and the row is marked with an error naming the values the field accepts; changing a row's unit or divisibility so that its value leaves the grid marks it the same way (DOM-017 (6)).
- A row can be deleted.
- Everything entered — the serving count and every row, including an incomplete or off-grid one — is in the book after leaving the editor and after the book is reopened from disk (FLOW-024 (6)).
- A frozen recipe shows these parts read-only, as the editor already does for the title and tags.

### T-055 — The serving count control on the card
**Status:** todo
**Serves:** scenario 4 · **Core:** yes · **Depends on:** T-054
**Expected files:** `core/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/core/scaling/RequestedServings.kt` (new) and its test; `app/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/card/*`; `strings_card.xml` (both); tests under `app/src/test/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/card/`.
**Criteria:**
- The card opens at the authored serving count with every quantity as authored (FLOW-017 (2)).
- A − and a + action walk the requested count through ¼, ⅓, ½, ⅔, ¾, 1, 2, 3 … and no other value can be reached; at ¼ the − action does nothing; there is no ceiling (SCALE-010 (1), SCALE-011 (1–3)). The walk is a `:core` function with its own tests.
- Every change recomputes every quantity through `ScaledRecipe` from the exact factor (SCALE-001, SCALE-003). Tests on a recipe for 4: 500 g at 6 servings shows 750 g; 3 indivisible eggs at 1 serving shows 1 egg; 1 tsp at 1 serving shows ¼ tsp; at no count does a line show zero (SCALE-005).
- The requested count is shown in the form requested — fraction or whole number — and never marked approximate (SCALE-011 (4)).
- Returning to the authored count shows the authored values exactly (FLOW-017 (5)).
- A recipe with no serving count shows no control and its quantities as authored; an incomplete or off-grid row is shown unscaled and does not stop the other rows from scaling.
- Each time the card is opened it starts at the authored count (decision log).
- Both actions are at least 48 dp and say to a screen reader what they do.

### T-056 — Fork from the card
**Status:** todo
**Serves:** scenario 5 · **Core:** yes · **Depends on:** T-055
**Expected files:** `core/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/core/recipe/Fork.kt` (new) and its test; `app/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/card/*`, `app/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/library/LibraryScreen.kt` / `LibrarySession.kt` if navigation needs it; `strings_card.xml` (both); tests under `app/src/test/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/card/`.
**Criteria:**
- The card's top bar offers a fork action on every recipe of the book, editable or frozen.
- Forking creates a new recipe: a new identity, state `local`, never sent, its parent the forked recipe's identity, in the active book (the pool when no account is active), created and changed now, not published, not a replacement (FORK-001 (7)).
- The fork's title, tags, language, serving count, ingredients (entry, qualifier, free-text name, unit type, unit, quantity, piece weight, divisibility, order) and steps (text, timer, references resolving within the fork) equal the parent's (FORK-001 (1–5)); its description and media equal the parent's too, since it is the owner's own fork (FORK-007 (1–3)). A `:core` test compares field by field on a recipe covering all five unit types, a named and an unnamed timer, and a step reference.
- After forking, the editor opens on the fork; an edit to the fork leaves the parent unchanged (FORK-007 (4)); both are in the own-recipes view afterwards, and after the book is reopened from disk.
- The fork's card names its parent by title in a provenance area; tapping it opens the parent's card while the book holds the parent; a parent the book does not hold is named as unavailable and cannot be opened (FORK-003 (3)).
- Exactly one parent is named, even for a fork of a fork, and no card lists descendants (FORK-004 (1, 3)).

## Decision log
- 2026-10-02 `decided` A fork in v0.1 is a local copy on the device with a parent link; the server-side fork of spec/65-forking comes with the server increment, and migrating local forks then is an accepted cost of about one task (the user).
- 2026-10-02 `decided` The specification is a vision, not a contract; MVP-001 is superseded by increments (the user).
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` The fork's card names its parent as serverless mode does (FORK-003 (3), FLOW-065): by title, opening it while held; spec/ FORK-005 hides a never-published parent, departed from because nothing can be published in v0.1 and scenario 5 asks for the name. FORK-005 applies again once there is a server.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` Every recipe in the pool is the user's own, so a fork carries description and media too (FORK-007); spec/ said nothing about the pool.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` Forking again always makes a new fork; the "you already hold an unchanged fork" offer of FLOW-015 is left for later.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` A `piece` ingredient always asks for divisibility, even when picked from the dictionary, because the shipped entries and the dictionary contract carry no divisibility yet; spec/ has the entry supply it (FLOW-001).
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` No qualifier tail on a picked entry (FLOW-023 (4)): a row is an entry or free text. Added later in one task if needed.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` Steps and ingredients are appended at the end and deleted; no insertion between rows (goes with FLOW-025, out of scope).
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` "New recipe" stays offered in the own-recipes view when the book is not empty; spec/ names the offer only for an empty book (FLOW-042).
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` Leaving the editor shows the recipe's card, and back from the card returns to the book — FLOW-029's "saving shows the reader's view".
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` The serving count is changed by − / + through ¼ ⅓ ½ ⅔ ¾ 1 2 3 …, with no typed count; the card opens at the authored count every time — no usual-count setting and no memory of the last count within a session (spec/ FLOW-017 keeps it for the session; departed from as the simplest first card).
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` A recipe with no serving count shows no count control on its card.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` The editor's fork marking (FLOW-030), the minimum/maximum warnings (FLOW-028) and the proportions warning (SCALE-009) are not in v0.1.
