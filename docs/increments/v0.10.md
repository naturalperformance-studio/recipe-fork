## Goal
The editor tells the author what a recipe needs, what a reader will see and what a fork takes — without ever blocking a keystroke.

## What exists at the start
On `develop` @ 92ee0d8 (v0.9 at demo, unanswered). The editor has the compact step and ingredient blocks, the insert-ingredient offer (whole or part), timers with a derived name (`core/timer/TimerName.kt`), GUIDE-007 (a second timer explains itself), a shared snackbar for statements (`EditorStatements`). Forking always makes a new fork (`card/Forking.kt`). Nothing warns about minimums or limits; there is no checklist; nothing says what a fork carries.

## Demo scenarios
1. Leave an empty ingredient list for the steps → the list is marked as needing at least one ingredient; add one → the mark goes. Type a step past 2 000 characters and leave it → marked over its limit, the text kept whole. Nothing is ever refused.
2. Begin the first step of a recipe that has ingredients → a statement says quantities can be inserted as references (by @ or the step's icon) and follow the servings, typed numbers do not.
3. Leave a long step carrying an unnamed timer → it shows what the notification will say (cut at 60 with "…") and offers to name the timer there.
4. The editor's top has a "Checklist" control → seven pieces of advice, read-only; closing returns to the same place. The editor also says which parts a fork takes (title, tags, servings, ingredients, steps) and which stay (description, photos — except in your own fork).
5. Fork a recipe twice without changing the first fork → "You already have a fork of this recipe" with Open it / Make another.
