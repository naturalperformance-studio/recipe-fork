# v0.11 — A card for busy hands, in your language
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-03 · **Shipped:** —

Planned by the lead under the user's standing instruction (v0.9 decision log): v0.10's build was delivered at 08:33 and no answer came within 20 minutes. From `serverless-roadmap.md`, increment B.

## Goal
The card is easy to use with wet or busy hands, the app speaks the language the reader picks, the book finds recipes by their language and description, and nothing in the serverless build mentions a server.

## What exists at the start
On `develop` @ 67cd86d (v0.10 at demo, unanswered). The card's controls (− / +, timer start/cancel/stop, fix, fork, edit) are 48 dp; step text is `bodyLarge` like everything else. The interface language follows the device; there is no way to change it in the app. The book searches title and ingredients, with ingredient and tag filters; a recipe declares a language (L10N-004) but nothing filters by it. Own entries in the serverless build still show sending marks ("Not sent: kept on this device alone…"). The card shows Loading / Missing only. Theme colours are Material defaults, never measured for contrast.

## Demo scenarios
1. On the card every control — servings − / +, a timer's start / cancel / stop, fix, fork, edit, back — is at least 56 dp; a step's text reads larger than the list around it.
2. In the book's ⋮ menu choose "Language" → Русский / English / As on the device → the whole app switches at once and keeps the choice after a restart.
3. In the book, filter by a recipe's language (e.g. only recipes written in English); search also finds a word from a recipe's description.
4. In the serverless build no own entry says "Not sent" or anything about sending.
5. A card whose recipe cannot be read from the device says so and offers "Try again".

## Out of scope
- A third language; a dark theme (only contrast of the existing theme is checked).
