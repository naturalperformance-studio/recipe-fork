# v0.12 — A dictionary that knows divisibility and comparison
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-03 · **Shipped:** —

Planned by the lead under the user's standing instruction (v0.9 decision log): v0.11's build was delivered at 09:49 and no answer came within 20 minutes. From `serverless-roadmap.md`, increment C.

## Goal
The dictionary knows whether an ingredient can be split and how much of it counts as how much: the editor stops asking about divisibility for dictionary ingredients, a dictionary ingredient can carry a few words of its own ("крылышки острые"), the filter orders recipes by how much of an ingredient they really hold, and the shipped dictionary is read once rather than on every screen.

## What exists at the start
On `develop` @ 1d95abf (v0.11 at demo, unanswered). Dictionary entries carry labels and a parent only; `AppDatabase` is version 1. Every `piece` row asks the author whether the ingredient divides (a departure listed in `spec/00-index.md`, FLOW-001 (2)); the row's own `divisible` drives scaling. Typing after a picked entry turns the row into free text (v0.1 "no qualifier tail"). The ingredient filter orders by one measure only (v0.8, no factors). Replacing the dictionary deletes entries, so a dropped one would lose its name. `DictionarySeeding.seedIfNeeded()` parses the whole asset on every catalog call (about three per editor open).

## Demo scenarios
Entry values shipped for the demo (ids unchanged, none removed): курица divisible, factors g 1 · pc 1500; крылышки not divisible, g 1 · pc 80; мясо, фарш, говядина g 1.
1. In the editor add крылышки in шт: no divisibility question; 1½ is marked as whole pieces only. Add курица in шт: 1½ is accepted. Add "яйцо" as free text in шт: the question is still asked.
2. Pick крылышки, keep typing " острые": the field reads "крылышки острые"; the card shows "крылышки, острые"; search "остр" finds the recipe and the курица filter still finds it.
3. Three recipes for 4 servings — A: 8 шт крылышки, B: 500 g курица, C: 1 шт курица. Filter by курица: C, then A, then B. A recipe with курица "по вкусу" only comes last.
4. On A's card change servings to 3: the wings stay whole pieces.
5. The app opens the editor and cards as before; v0.12.md records the bench at the caps (50 ingredients, 50 steps, 5 000-entry dictionary) and the parse-once check.
