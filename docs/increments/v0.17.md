# v0.17 — Cooking with busy hands
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-04 · **Shipped:** —

Goal, scenarios and out-of-scope below were approved by the user on 2026-10-04. The user chose to go on with the UX audit (`ux-roadmap.md` U2) and to leave handing the build to people for later. Development not started at the user's word: planned only.

## Goal
Cooking from a recipe with dirty hands is comfortable: running timers are always in sight, the buttons are large and answer with a vibration, and steps and ingredients read at a distance.

## What exists at the start
On `develop` @ 570a500 (v0.16 shipped). Timer controls in `timer/StepTimer.kt` are text links (`TimerAction`) inside each step, so a running timer is out of sight once its step is scrolled past (`StepTimer.kt:232-296`). No haptics anywhere. Fixing an amount takes a second tap and draws its own unit boxes (`card/FixAmount.kt`, `UnitField`). Steps (`card/StepsList.kt`, `card/StepText.kt`) have no number badges; ingredient quantities (`card/IngredientsList.kt`, `card/IngredientLine.kt`) are not aligned.

## Demo scenarios
1. Start a timer in step 2, scroll to step 6 → a bar pinned at the bottom shows the running timer and the time left; tapping it brings back its step; pause and stop work from the bar.
2. Timer controls are large buttons with icons (56 dp), not links; start, pause and stop answer with a short vibration.
3. Change servings with −/+ → each step gives a light tick.
4. Fixing an amount takes one tap on the quantity: the field opens with the keyboard ready; the unit is chosen from a standard drop-down.
5. Steps have a number in a circle, larger text and more space between them; your place is easy to find by eye.
6. Ingredient quantities are aligned in a column, the names beside them.
7. In the editor, a row being dragged ticks at each new position.

## Out of scope
- Keeping the screen on while a timer runs (audit 9): FLOW-014 is the owner's decision — the screen sleeps after 15 minutes untouched and the timer still announces itself.
- Ingredient groups ("Dough" / "Glaze"): needs a data model (`later.md`).
- Colours and the type scale (F1); the colour parts of audit 7 and 8.
- A step-by-step cooking mode, ticking off ingredients or steps (product questions in `ux-roadmap.md`).
- The editor's own UX (U3), beyond the drag tick; filters, media, import and export (U4).

## Tasks
Order: T-137 and T-139 start together; T-138 after T-137; T-140 after T-139.

Standing note for every task: interface strings in English and Russian; tests wait for conditions, never on timing; touch targets ≥ 48 dp (56 dp on the card) and ContrastTest stays green; existing behaviour covered by tests stays (adapt tests' structure, not their meaning); Material 3 components from the version catalog, no new libraries; colours from today's theme. Haptics through Compose `LocalHapticFeedback` (or the view's `performHapticFeedback`) behind one small seam tests can observe; they follow the system's touch-feedback setting. Gradle: the shared `~/.gradle`, never a per-run `GRADLE_USER_HOME` (`00-index.md`).

### T-137 — Timer buttons and haptics
**Status:** todo
**Serves:** 2, 3, 7 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** —
**Expected files:** `timer/StepTimer.kt` (controls), a haptics seam (new, e.g. `ui/Haptics.kt`), `card/CardScreen.kt` (the servings −/+ only), the editor's drag-to-reorder (`editor/EditorScreen.kt` or `editor/RecipeEditor.kt`, the drag only), `strings_timer.xml`, tests.
**Criteria:**
- A step's timer controls (start, pause, resume, stop/reset, whatever exists today) are M3 tonal (or filled) icon buttons with a text label or content description and tooltip, at least 56 dp tall on the card; behaviour unchanged (FLOW-013).
- Starting, pausing, resuming and stopping a timer each give a confirm haptic.
- Each change of servings by −/+ gives a light tick haptic; a change refused at the range's end gives none (or a reject haptic).
- In the editor, a dragged row gives a tick each time it passes to a new position, not on every pixel.
- Tests assert each haptic through the seam (which kind, how many).

### T-138 — A docked bar of running timers
**Status:** todo
**Serves:** 1 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** T-137
**Expected files:** `card/CardScreen.kt` (the Scaffold's bottom bar), `timer/StepTimer.kt` or a new `timer/RunningTimersBar.kt`, `card/StepsList.kt` (scrolling to a step), `strings_timer.xml`, tests.
**Criteria:**
- While any timer of this recipe is running or paused and its step is not on screen, a bar pinned at the bottom of the card shows it: name (or the step's number), time left, pause/resume and stop; several timers are listed, the soonest first, the bar never taller than about a third of the screen (it scrolls inside).
- Tapping a timer in the bar scrolls the card to its step.
- The bar disappears when no timer is running or paused, or when every running timer's step is on screen; content gets bottom padding so the bar covers nothing.
- The bar's buttons use T-137's buttons and haptics; a timer that rings shows as ringing in the bar with its stop.

### T-139 — Fixing an amount in one tap
**Status:** todo
**Serves:** 4 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** —
**Expected files:** `card/FixAmount.kt`, `card/IngredientLine.kt` (the tap target only), `strings_card.xml`, tests.
**Criteria:**

### T-140 — Steps and ingredients that read at a distance
**Status:** todo
**Serves:** 5, 6 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** T-139
**Expected files:** `card/StepsList.kt`, `card/StepText.kt`, `card/IngredientsList.kt`, `card/IngredientLine.kt` (layout), tests.
**Criteria:**
- Each step starts with its number in a filled circle (decorative to accessibility; the step's announced text still says its number once); step text one size up from today (a material type style, not a hard-coded size), with clear spacing between steps.
- Ingredient quantities (with unit) sit in one right-aligned column whose width fits the longest quantity, names in a column beside it; at large font scale or narrow width the line wraps rather than truncating the name.
- References, timers and media in steps look and work as today; A11Y large-text tests stay green.
