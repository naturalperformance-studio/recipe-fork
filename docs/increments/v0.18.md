# v0.18 — Writing a recipe fast
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-04 · **Shipped:** —

Goal, scenarios and out-of-scope below were approved by the user on 2026-10-04: U3 of `ux-roadmap.md` plus the two issues from the v0.17 demo. Development starts in a new session at the user's word.

## Goal
Typing a recipe on the phone takes no extra taps and nothing is lost by a slip, and the small cooking issues found at the v0.17 demo are fixed.

## What exists at the start
On `develop` @ 0d93077 (v0.17 shipped). The haptics seam is `ui/Haptics.kt` (`Haptic { CONFIRM, TICK }`, `LocalHaptics`); `TICK` maps to `HapticFeedbackType.SegmentTick`, which the owner's phone does not play. A step's timer sits right under its text (`card/StepsList.kt`, `StepIndent`). The editor lives in `editor/` (`RecipeEditor.kt`, `IngredientsSection.kt`, `StepsSection.kt`, `StepTextField.kt`, `Reorder.kt`, `EditorScreen.kt`).

## Demo scenarios
1. On the card, a step's timer stands clearly apart from the step's text.
2. Servings −/+ and dragging a row in the editor are felt as a vibration on the owner's phone.
3. In a new recipe, the title, steps and ingredient names start with a capital letter by themselves; the keyboard's Next goes title → … → ingredient name → quantity → unit → the next row; a new row is focused at once.
4. Removing an ingredient or a step shows "Undo" at the bottom; Undo brings the row back with its photo or clip.
5. Tags are chips with a cross, with suggestion chips beside them; the recipe's language is a segmented [RU | EN] switch.
6. The editor has a top app bar; saving says "Saved" for a moment; errors show inside their fields; the free-text warning does not pop up mid-typing.

## Out of scope
- Media, filters, import and export (U4); the settings screen and the theme (U5, F1).
- The permanent fork note and the references hint pushing the form down (audit 17) — next editor pass.
- Ingredient groups (`later.md`); a timer pause (v0.17 decision log).
- The v0.17 review edges (step numbering of hidden steps in the timer bar; media indent at 2× font).
