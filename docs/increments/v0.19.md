## Goal
The cover, import and export, filters and deleting take one or two taps and show what is happening, and nothing on the card or in the editor jumps when a hint appears.

## Demo scenarios
1. An empty cover shows a placeholder; tapping it opens a bottom sheet "Camera / Gallery", as step media do; the crop screen after the camera has "Cancel".
2. Under the book's search, a row of quick filter chips filters on one tap; "All filters" opens one bottom sheet instead of today's dialogs.
3. An empty book and an empty search result show an icon, a headline and one action.
4. Importing a file shows progress; afterwards a snackbar offers "Open" and the list scrolls to the imported recipe; exporting shows progress too.
5. A recipe can be deleted from the card's menu as well as from the list's ⋮; the confirming button is marked as destructive.
6. In the editor, the fork note and the references hint no longer push the form down (audit 17).
7. On the card, changing servings to and from the authored count does not move the ingredient list: a status line always stands above it, saying "As the author wrote it" or "Recalculated · ↑ rounded up · ↓ rounded down", its height that of the taller of the two at the font scale in force.

## Out of scope
- The theme, colours and the settings screen (v0.20); M3 component sweep and motion (v0.21).
- A whole-book backup; Undo instead of a confirmation for deleting a recipe (a product question in `ux-roadmap.md`).
- The proportions warning (SCALE-009) keeps appearing and disappearing as today; it shows only when quantities hit their floors.
