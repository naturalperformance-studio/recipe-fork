# v0.2 — Cooking from the card
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-02 · **Shipped:** —

Goal, scenarios and out-of-scope below were approved by the user on 2026-10-02.

## Goal
Cook from the card on the phone: timers on steps, a screen that stays on, quantities that say they were recalculated, and scaling from what the cook actually has of one ingredient.

## What exists at the start
On `develop` @ 0c2f9ac (v0.1 shipped): the editor writes title, language, tags, serving count, ingredient rows (dictionary or free text, quantity on its grid, unit), steps and description; the card shows the recipe, scales it with − / + through `ScaledRecipe` (`card/IngredientLine.kt`, `core/scaling/RequestedServings.kt`), forks it and names the parent. `Step.timer` (`Timer(duration, label)`) exists in the model and is stored (`step.timer_minutes`, `step.timer_label`), but nothing writes or shows it. `ScaledRecipe` already supports fixing and releasing an ingredient (SCALE-002, -006, -016, -017) and `Scaling.display` returns the rounding direction; the card shows neither. The app holds no notification permission and schedules nothing.

## Demo scenarios
1. In the editor, give a step a 20-minute timer → on the card the step has a timer control → start it, leave the app → when it ends a notification arrives naming the recipe and the step; tapping it opens the card at that step.
2. Start two timers on different steps: both run, each shown on its own step with its remaining time and named by its step.
3. Open a card and leave it untouched for a minute: the screen stays on.
4. Change the serving count: every quantity is marked as recalculated, and a rounded one says which way it was rounded ("exact value was lower").
5. Tap an ingredient → enter "I have 300 g" → the whole recipe recalculates for that amount and the serving count is shown as approximate (≈).
6. Scale down until two ingredients sit at their minimum: a warning says the proportions no longer hold.

## Out of scope
- Quantity references inside steps (FLOW-031, FLOW-032, SCALE-007) — a derived timer name uses the step's plain text.
- Inserting between and reordering steps or ingredients (FLOW-025).
- The usual serving count setting (FLOW-017), restoring the scroll position (FLOW-012).
- Server, sending, publication, catalogue, ratings, media.
- Accessibility and localisation sweeps; ordinary good practice inside each task is enough.

## Tasks

### T-057 — A timer on a step in the editor
**Status:** done
**Serves:** scenarios 1, 2 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** —
**Expected files:** `core/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/core/timer/TimerName.kt` (new) and its test; `app/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/editor/StepsSection.kt`, `RecipeEditor.kt`; `strings_editor.xml` (both); tests under `app/src/test/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/editor/`.
**Criteria:**
- Each editable step offers attaching a timer; its duration is entered as hours (0–99) and whole minutes (0–59), nothing else is required, and no seconds are offered; the field cannot hold more than 99 h 59 min (FLOW-027 (1, 4, 12, 14)).
- A duration of 0 h 0 min, or none entered, stores no timer, and nothing is refused (FLOW-027 (11, 13)).
- On a step that already carries a timer the control stays; choosing it creates no second timer and states that a step carries one timer and that two waits in sequence are two steps (FLOW-027 (2), GUIDE-007).
- A timer with no name of its own shows the name it will announce itself by, derived from the step; the author can correct it, after which it stays as written whatever happens to the step's text; clearing it makes it follow the step again (FLOW-027 (5–7), FLOW-016 (6, 7)).
- The derived name is the step's first 60 characters cut at a word boundary with an ellipsis where the text was longer, and the whole text with no mark where it was 60 characters or fewer (FLOW-016 (3, 4)); a `:core` function with its own tests.
- A name over 60 characters is kept in full and not refused while typing.
- Deleting a step deletes its timer; the timer and its name survive leaving the editor and reopening the book from disk.
- A frozen recipe shows a step's timer read-only.
**Notes:** Passed review on the first round; merged. A review nit — the derived name can cut a word at a hyphen or apostrophe ("кое…" from "кое-где") — is folded into T-058 as a criterion.

**Serves:** scenarios 4, 6 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** —

- Markers and directions are words or symbols with a text alternative, never colour alone, and are read by a screen reader.
- The card's ingredient list and step list move into their own files (`IngredientsList.kt`, `StepsList.kt`), so that T-058 and T-062 change disjoint files; `CardScreen.kt` only composes them.
**Notes:** Passed review on the first round; merged. Added `heldAtFloor` to `DisplayedQuantity` and `holdsProportions` to `ScaledRecipe` in `:core`. The full suite after the merge exposed an intermittent Compose test-runtime threading failure in unrelated tests; split out as T-063.

**Expected files:** `app/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/timer/*` (new: the running timers, held across process death); `app/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/card/StepsList.kt`; `AppContainer.kt`; `strings_timer.xml` (new, both); tests under `app/src/test/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/timer/` and `.../card/`.
**Criteria:**

- A running timer can be cancelled; it then never ends or announces (FLOW-013 (7)).
- Starting a timer that is running asks whether to restart it: confirm restarts from the full duration, decline keeps it; other timers are untouched; a timer that has ended starts again with no question (FLOW-021).
- A timer that ends while the card is open is shown as finished on its step.
- Time is read from a clock the tests control; no test waits real minutes.
- (Folded in from T-057's review) The derived name does not cut inside a word at a hyphen, an apostrophe or a combining mark.
**Notes:** Passed review on the first round; merged. Review nits that bite only once timers announce — a timer keyed by step position, start/cancel in the card's scope, the running check reading a not-yet-loaded snapshot — are folded into T-059 as criteria.

- (From T-058's review) A start tapped before held timers load, or a quick second tap, still asks before restarting a running timer.

### T-062 — Scale from what you have of one ingredient
**Status:** done
**Serves:** scenario 5 · **Core:** yes · **Depends on:** T-061
**Expected files:** `app/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/card/IngredientsList.kt`, `IngredientLine.kt`, `CardScreen.kt` (where the scale state lives), new `card/FixAmount.kt`; `core/.../scaling/*` only if `ScaledRecipe` needs a missing piece; `strings_card.xml` (both); tests under `app/src/test/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/card/` and `core/src/test/...`.
**Criteria:**
- Tapping an ingredient that carries a quantity offers entering the amount the reader has; a "to taste" row or an incomplete/off-grid row offers nothing.
- The amount is read as the quantity field reads it — comma or point, no separators — and accepted only on its unit type's grid, in a unit the author's unit reads in without conversion (gram ↔ kilogram, not gram ↔ cup) (SCALE-018, DOM-019, DOM-017); otherwise the entry says what it accepts.
- A fixed amount sets the factor for the whole recipe; the fixed line is marked as fixed and the serving count is shown as approximate (≈), as a fraction below one (SCALE-002, SCALE-008). Test: a recipe for 4 with 500 g chicken, fixing 300 g, shows 300 g and ≈2 servings; every other line follows the same exact factor (SCALE-003).
- Fixing a second ingredient makes the latest set the factor; an earlier fixed one whose need now exceeds what was fixed shows the shortfall (SCALE-006).
- Changing the serving count releases every fixing and shows the count asked for, not approximate (SCALE-016); releasing the last fixing returns to the count in force before the first (SCALE-017).
- The derived markers and directions of T-061 apply to every line while a fixing sets the scale.
**Notes:** Passed review with the mutation check on the first round (every criterion's tests fail when its behaviour is removed). The lead changed the Russian release label to «Снять фиксацию» after a review nit; that commit goes to a fresh reviewer before the merge. That review found the longer label squeezed «Пересчитать» mid-word on a 360dp phone (and even the old layout broke at font scale 2.0); fix round 1 relaid the actions with a measuring test. Re-review passed; merged (the lead resolved a one-line conflict in `CardScreen.kt` with T-058's `StepsList(recipe.steps, recipe.id)`).

## Decision log
- 2026-10-02 `decided` v0.2 is the cooking card — timers, screen awake, derived markers, fixing an ingredient — on the device alone; the server increment waits (the user).
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` A derived timer name uses the step's plain text; ingredient references in steps are out of scope, so FLOW-016 (8) has nothing to render yet.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` How a timer is made to fire with the app closed (exact alarm or foreground service) is chosen by the T-059 developer for reliability on API 29–37 without asking the user for special access beyond notifications; the Play Store policy for that mechanism is checked before any store release, not now.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` A recalculated quantity carries a small line under it, in words only: "recalculated" or "recalculated, exact value was lower / higher" (RU «пересчитано…»); spec/ fixes the meaning, not the form.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` The proportions warning sits at the top of the Ingredients section and is announced politely by the screen reader.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` "Held at its floor" means the exact scaled value is below the floor; a value landing on the floor exactly, or rounded down onto it, does not count towards the warning.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` Rows the card shows unscaled (to taste, incomplete, off-grid) carry no marker at any count and do not count towards the warning.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` Returning to the app while a card is shown counts as a touch and starts a fresh 15 minutes of keeping the screen on.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` Any press anywhere on the card, including the start of a scroll, counts as a touch for keeping the screen on.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` A step's timer is removed by emptying its duration or entering 0 h 0 min; there is no separate remove control.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` The timer's name field is pre-filled with the derived name, labelled "Timer's name", with the helper "Taken from the step. Change it to name the timer yourself."; any edit, even retyping the derived text, makes it the author's own name.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` The derived name folds line breaks and runs of spaces into one space, counts emoji as one character, cuts a single word longer than 60 hard at 60, and puts "…" after the 60 characters.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` The one-timer-per-step statement reads "A step has one timer. For two waits one after another, split this step into two steps, each with its own timer." and disappears on the next tap or edit, not on a scroll.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` A frozen recipe shows "Timer: 1 h 5 min" and "Timer's name: …" as text.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` A name over 60 characters is not marked when the author leaves the field; FLOW-028 is not part of this increment.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` Under a step with a timer sits one row: the timer's name, then its duration before it starts, the countdown while it runs ("9:59", hours only from an hour up), or "Finished" once it ends; beside a running timer "Cancel", beside a finished one "Clear".
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` A finished timer stays marked finished on its step, even after reopening the card, until it is started again or cleared.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` The restart question is a dialog — "“<name>” has <left> left. Restart it from <duration>?" — with Restart and Keep running; tapping outside it keeps the timer running.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` A running timer belongs to its step's position: if the author moves steps while it runs, it stays at that position.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` Running timers are held on the device in a small database of their own, keyed by book, recipe and step position with their end time; the timer's name is not stored but derived when shown or announced.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` Fixing an amount opens inline under the tapped row: a field, a unit choice among the units the author's unit reads in, and Recalculate / Cancel (and Release on a fixed row); it applies on Recalculate, not per keystroke, and a refused amount shows why as you type, reusing the editor's quantity messages.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` While a fixing is in force, − / + step from the shown count (from ≈2, + gives 3) and release every fixing.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` A fixed row reads "fixed: the amount you have"; one that falls short reads "fixed: you have 300 g, the recipe now needs 500 g"; the count reads "Servings: ≈2".
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` A recipe with no serving count can still be fixed and shows no count; fixings reset when the recipe's ingredients change.
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` The Russian label of the action that releases a fixed ingredient is «Снять фиксацию», not «Отменить», so it is not read as a second "cancel" beside «Закрыть».
- 2026-10-02 `assumed` In the fixing control, Release sits on its own line; Cancel and Recalculate wrap as whole buttons, and Recalculate is a filled primary button — so every label stays whole on a narrow phone at a large font.
