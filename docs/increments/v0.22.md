# v0.22 — Nothing sticks together, ticks are felt, the tails are picked up
**Status:** planning
**Planned:** 2026-10-04 · **Shipped:** —

Goal, scenarios and out-of-scope approved by the user on 2026-10-04 after touching the v0.21 build (with v0.23 and v0.24). Development starts at the user's word.

## Goal
On the card and in the book nothing sticks together, the owner's phone feels the ticks, and the tails left by v0.18–v0.21 are picked up.

## Demo scenarios
1. On the card, − and + are one segmented button [ − | + ]; each half is 56 dp, is announced and works as today.
2. In the book, search and sort share the first line; the row of filter chips sits below with a clear gap (about 12 dp); nothing touches the field at any font size.
3. With the system's touch feedback on, servings −/+ and dragging a row give a short tick and the timer buttons a click on the owner's phone (checked on the device over USB); with it off, nothing vibrates.
4. A recipe exported and sent through Telegram opens again: the file's name is ASCII, its extension kept.
5. After the camera, the crop's "Cancel · Retake · Done" fit one line in Russian at 2× font on a 320 dp screen.

## Out of scope
- New recipe capabilities, undo of deleting, "done" marks (v0.23); several timers in a step, an animated image as a clip (v0.24).
- Handing the build to people (`later.md`).

## Tasks
Order: T-160, T-161, T-162 and T-163 are independent (three at a time); T-164 and T-165 after them.
