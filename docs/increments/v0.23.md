## Goal
While cooking the owner marks what is done and can take back a deleted recipe; while writing, ingredients fall into groups and any line can carry a short note.

## Demo scenarios
1. On the card, tapping an ingredient or a step marks it done (struck through, read as "done"); the marks stay after going to the book, switching apps or restarting; "Clear marks" on the card removes them. Exporting or forking the recipe carries no marks.
2. Deleting a recipe (from the list or the card) removes it at once and shows "Recipe deleted · Undo"; Undo brings it back with its photos and clips; once the snackbar goes, the deletion is final and the media are deleted.
3. In the editor, "Add group" puts a named header ("Dough", "Glaze") among the ingredients; rows below it belong to it; groups can be renamed, reordered with their rows and removed (rows stay, ungrouped). The card shows the groups as subheadings; scaling, references and filters work as before.
4. Any ingredient line can carry a short note ("finely chopped", "room temperature"), shown after it on the card in a muted style; a free-text ingredient can carry one too.
5. A recipe with groups and notes exported and imported again keeps them.
6. In the editor, many tags wrapping onto several lines keep a gap between the lines (about 8 dp, as between tags); the offered tags too.

## Out of scope
- Several timers in a step, an animated image as a clip (v0.24).
- Groups of steps; notes that scale or are searched.
- Marks shared between devices.

### T-171 — Groups in the editor and on the card
**Status:** done
**Serves:** 3 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** T-170

**Criteria:**

- Undo of removing a group restores it, like removing a row today.
- Inserting, moving or removing an ingredient row keeps every heading over the rows it had (the headings' positions shift with the rows); no edit can leave a heading past the last row but one just added; an exported grouped recipe always passes the importer's checks (a test).

## Decision log
- 2026-10-04 `decided` "Done" marks live on the device per recipe until "Clear marks" (F: the user, recommended option); never in the recipe, its file or a fork — spec/'s "nothing records progress" holds for the recipe itself.
- 2026-10-04 `decided` Deleting a recipe offers Undo instead of being immediate and final — departs from spec/ FLOW-053 (2), to fold back with `/req-forge --sync` (the user).
- 2026-10-04 `assumed` Groups are ingredient-only headers; a group changes nothing in scaling, references or filters. The recipe document gains groups and notes — a new `recipe.json` version; files from older versions still import (no group, no note).
- 2026-10-04 `assumed` A note is free text up to 60 characters on any ingredient line, not searched, not scaled.
- 2026-10-09 `decided` The editor's wrapped tag lines get a vertical gap — added to this increment (the user).
- 2026-10-09 `assumed` On the card a tap on an ingredient line marks it done; fixing an amount, which a tap on the line opened before, now opens from the amount itself (its own touch target). The spec gives the tap to the mark (FLOW-068).
- 2026-10-09 `assumed` Done marks are kept in the device's preferences per book and recipe; a mark on a row whose content changed in the editor is dropped.
- 2026-10-09 `assumed` A group name is 1–40 characters; groups and notes are a new `recipe.json` version (2) and a book-database migration (3→4).
- 2026-10-09 `assumed` Deleting no longer asks first: the Undo notice replaces the confirmation dialog, for own and found recipes alike.
- 2026-10-09 `assumed` Removing a group heading leaves its rows in place: they join the group above, or no group if it was the first. DOM-022 says "outside any group"; that holds for the first group, and for a later one would mean moving the rows above the first heading, which reorders the recipe — departure to fold back with `/req-forge --sync`.
- 2026-10-09 `assumed` A heading may stand after the last ingredient (a group just added, with no rows yet). A version-1 file that happens to carry groups or notes is read rather than refused.
- 2026-10-09 `assumed` A done line or step is struck through in the theme's muted colour; the screen reader says "Done" only when marked; every ingredient line and step is at least 56 dp tall now that each is a card control.
- 2026-10-09 `assumed` "Clear marks" clears at once, without asking and without Undo; on a recipe whose ⋮ had nothing else, the ⋮ shows only while there are marks.
- 2026-10-09 `assumed` In the editor the note field ("How to prepare") sits on its own line under the name, above quantity and unit, without a forced capital; on the card the note follows the name after " — " in the muted colour and is read with the line.
- 2026-10-09 `assumed` When every amount in a list is short, the amount column widens a little (44 dp) so that the amount's touch target never covers a name.
- 2026-10-09 `assumed` The notice reads "Recipe deleted · Undo" / "Recipe removed · Undo" (Russian «Рецепт удалён» / «Рецепт убран» · «Вернуть», the editor's word), lasts 10 s and can be swiped away; turning the phone shows it again. Opening a card, the editor or the settings ends it, and the deletion becomes final.
- 2026-10-09 `assumed` A pending deletion is held in memory only: if the app's process dies while the notice shows, the deletion is final and the media sweep clears the files later.
- 2026-10-09 `assumed` A new heading shows "e.g. Dough" until named and is never saved blank; leaving a name blank removes a never-named heading, or restores the old name. A group left with no rows by a move or a removal loses its heading too (Undo brings both back).
- 2026-10-09 `assumed` A row's Move up/down at the edge of a group crosses the heading (leaves the group, or joins the next one); Insert above/below keeps the new row in the menu row's group; a group moves only past another group, never above the ungrouped rows; "Add group above" is offered on a row's menu unless the row already starts a group.
- 2026-10-09 `assumed` On the card a group is a small heading (screen-reader heading) over its rows; a heading with no rows is not shown.
- 2026-10-09 `assumed` If Undo cannot bring the recipe back (a rare clash of its media with another recipe), the deletion becomes final and the book says "Couldn't bring the recipe back".
