**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-04 · **Shipped:** —

## Goal
A step can hold more than one timer, and a GIF or animated WebP attached to a step plays like a clip.

## Demo scenarios
1. In the editor, a step offers "Add timer" more than once; each timer has its own name and duration; they can be removed one by one.
2. On the card, each timer of a step starts, runs and rings on its own, with its own notification; the running-timers bar lists them all; a recipe file keeps them.
3. Attaching a GIF or an animated WebP to a step turns it into a silent clip that plays on the card like any clip; a still image stays a photo.

## Out of scope
- Timers outside steps; a timer that depends on another.
- Animated covers.

### T-177 — An animated image becomes a silent clip (spike and converter)
**Status:** running
**Serves:** 3 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** —
**Expected files:** new files in `app/media/` (an animated-image detector, duration reader and converter implementing `ClipConverter`), `AppContainer.kt` (wiring only), an androidTest beside `VideoConversionTest`, unit tests.
**Criteria:**
- A GIF or animated WebP is detected as animated (a still GIF/WebP/PNG/JPEG is not); its duration is the sum of its frame delays.
- It converts to an MP4 within the clip limits (≤ 15 s, ≤ 6 MB, ≤ 1280 px longer edge, no audio track) that the existing card player plays — proven on the emulator (`scripts/device-test.sh`) with a GIF and an animated WebP fixture; an animation over 15 s is refused as too long like a clip.
- No new library, or the lead approves one (APK growth ≤ 1 MB). If no route fits the limits, the task reports that and stops — the feature is then dropped and said so.
