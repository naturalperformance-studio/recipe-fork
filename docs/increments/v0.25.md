# v0.25 — Ready to put on Google Play
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-09 · **Shipped:** —

Planned by the lead while the owner slept, under the owner's standing instruction (2026-10-09): when the iterations run out and the owner has not come back, the last iteration prepares publishing on Google Play — "a simple icon, all the tests needed and screenshots". Goal, scenarios and scope are `assumed`; the owner re-decides at the demo. Nothing is published and no account, key or payment is created: those are the owner's (see Forks).

## Goal
Everything Google Play asks of the app itself is ready: an icon, a signed-ready app bundle with a real version, store screenshots in English and Russian, the listing and policy texts drafted, and a test pass that shows the build is fit to hand to strangers.

## Demo scenarios
1. On the phone the app shows its own icon (also as a themed icon on Android 13+) and the name "Recipe Fork" on the launcher.
2. `scripts/play-bundle.sh` builds `build/play/recipe-fork-<version>.aab` (serverless, minified) with the version of the increment; given the upload key's path and passwords in `~/Projects/.env` it is signed with it, without them it says what is missing and stops.
3. `build/play/screenshots/` holds phone screenshots (1080×1920) of the book, a recipe card with a running timer, the editor with groups, and the settings, in English and in Russian, plus the 512×512 icon and the 1024×500 feature graphic.
4. `docs/play/` holds the listing texts (EN, RU), the Data safety answers, a privacy policy draft and the owner's checklist of what only they can do.
5. The pre-launch test pass is green: every main screen passes the accessibility checks in both languages and both themes, and the minified release build passes the smoke test on the emulator.

## Out of scope
- Creating the Play developer account (a fee), the upload key, the store listing itself, and publishing — the owner's (F-1).
- The standard (server) build on Play; a server deployment; sign-in.
- A whole-book backup and restore (`later.md`, "Handing the build to people") — the owner's call whether it must come before strangers use it (F-1).
- The vibration on the owner's phone (postponed by the owner).

### T-180 — The app's icon
**Status:** review
**Serves:** 1, 3 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** —
**Expected files:** `app/src/main/res/mipmap-anydpi-v26/`, `res/drawable/` (vector foreground, monochrome), `res/values/` (icon background colour from the theme file), `AndroidManifest.xml` (`android:icon`, `android:roundIcon`), a 512×512 PNG export for the store, tests.
**Criteria:**
- An adaptive icon: a simple vector foreground (a fork crossing a recipe card or a leaf — simple, legible at 48 dp), a solid background from the theme's primary container, and a monochrome layer for themed icons (Android 13+); the foreground keeps inside the 66 dp safe zone.
- A 512×512 PNG of the icon for the store, generated from the same vector (a Gradle task or a test that renders it), under `build/play/`.
- A test asserts the manifest's icon and round icon resolve to the adaptive icon with a monochrome layer.

## Forks
### F-1 — What must exist before strangers install it (open)
Before the first upload: (a) the owner creates the Play developer account (one-off fee) and the upload key (Play App Signing keeps the app's own key; the upload key can be reset through Play support, so it is less final than an APK key); (b) whether a whole-book backup/restore must come first — in the serverless build a phone lost or reset loses the book. Recommendation: internal testing first with the current build, backup as the next increment before a public release. Nothing in this increment waits on it.
