# v0.26 — The editor and the card, with less in the way
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-09 · **Shipped:** —

Planned with the owner on 2026-10-09 after v0.23–v0.25 shipped ("Push, all ok, overall"; for the next iterations: Done in the editor, once the recipe is saved, closes the editor). The rest is the interface findings the store screenshots showed (`later.md`). Goal, scenarios and scope approved by the owner ("Ок").

## Goal
Done in the editor saves and closes it, and the card and the editor show more of the recipe and less that gets in the way.

## Demo scenarios
1. In the editor, Done saves the recipe and closes the editor, back to where it was opened from (the card, or the book for a new recipe); a row removal still offering Undo becomes final, as when leaving.
2. On the card, a cooking screen (360×640 dp) shows at least six ingredient lines; every line is still a 56 dp target that marks it done.
3. The editor's top bar shows the recipe's title (or "New recipe" while it has none).
4. The card's title in its top bar is not cut by its action icons on a 360 dp screen for a title of up to ~30 characters.
5. A step's ingredient reference never breaks between its number and its unit («4½ ст. л.», «1,5 кг»).
6. The editor's tag chips are drawn 32 dp tall (48 dp touch); "Clear saved media" says plainly what it clears in the serverless build; a timer's remove button and its "Timer N removed" notice give the same N.

## Out of scope
- A whole-book backup (F-1 of v0.25, open); the vibration on the owner's phone (postponed); publishing.
- Redesigning the card's layout beyond the line heights.

## Tasks
Order: T-187, T-188 and T-191 first (three at a time); T-190 and T-192 after (T-190 after T-188: both in the card; T-192 after T-187: both in the editor).

Standing note for every task: load the `android-m3-ui` skill and follow `docs/architecture/ui-conventions.md`; interface strings in English and Russian; tests wait for conditions, never on timing; touch targets ≥ 48 dp (56 dp on the card) and ContrastTest green; the pre-launch sweep (`ui/PreLaunchSweepTest`) stays green; existing behaviour covered by tests stays; no new libraries.
