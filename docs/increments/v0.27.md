# v0.27 — Reading and cooking are two tabs; the recipe names its equipment
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-09 · **Shipped:** —

Planned with the owner on 2026-10-09 during the v0.26 demo: the big cooking interface is uncomfortable when not cooking; the owner wants the card split into "View" and "Cooking", always visible, like the book's two tabs, switched by swipe too ("check it against Material 3"); asked the lead for further ideas for both modes and added a list of the equipment a recipe needs. Scope approved by the owner ("Да, давай так"). The next iteration is likely about the editor (the owner).

## Goal
The recipe card has two tabs — View, compact and for deciding, and Cooking, large and for busy hands — and a recipe lists the equipment it needs.

## Demo scenarios
1. Under the card's top bar sit two tabs, "View | Cooking" / «Просмотр | Готовка», always visible; a tap or a horizontal swipe switches them; each keeps its own scroll; the card opens on the last tab chosen, and a timer's notification opens it on Cooking.
2. View: compact ingredient and step lines (normal height), done marks shown but not toggled, timers shown as their durations without Start, servings adjustable; Fork as the floating button; Edit and ⋮ (Delete) in the bar; under the title a summary line ("9 ingredients · 4 steps · timers ≈ 1 h 10 min") and, for a fork, where it came from.
3. Cooking: no floating button, no Edit, no cover or description; the bar holds Back and the title, its ⋮ only Clear marks; 56 dp lines that mark done, fixing an amount, timers that start; larger step text; the first unmarked step is highlighted and the card scrolls to the next one after a step is marked; once every ingredient is marked the list folds into one line ("All gathered · show"); the screen stays on while the Cooking tab is open.
4. In the editor a recipe gets an "Equipment" section — short lines ("Oven", "Frying pan 26 cm"), up to 15; the card shows them above the ingredients (marked off like ingredients on Cooking); a recipe file carries them, older files open without.

## Out of scope
- A landscape layout for a phone on a stand or a tablet (`later.md`).
- Filtering the book by equipment ("without an oven"); a dictionary of equipment (`later.md`).
- Teaching the `/recipe-file` skill the new field (outside the repository; the lead notes it).
- The rest of the editor's rework (the next iteration).

- 2026-10-09 `decided` Cooking has nothing that distracts from cooking — no Fork, no Edit (the owner).
- 2026-10-09 `decided` A recipe lists the equipment it needs (the owner).
- 2026-10-09 `assumed` From the lead's ideas, taken with the owner's agreement: the screen stays on while Cooking is open; the current step is highlighted with a scroll to the next; ingredients fold when all are marked; larger step text on Cooking; a summary line and the provenance on View. A landscape layout is left for later.
