# v0.28 — My kitchen: the equipment you have, and what a recipe still needs
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-10 · **Shipped:** —

Planned with the owner on 2026-10-10 after v0.26 and v0.27 shipped ("Всё супер, пушь"). The owner's words: an equipment dictionary like the ingredients' one, so that a user fills in once what their kitchen has and at once sees what a recipe lacks, and can hide the recipes they have no equipment for. Then the editor's ingredients and steps ("the interface teems with buttons and fields") and the description (each step gets an optional author's part; the main description shows on the card before the ingredients) — both v0.29. Goal, scenarios and scope approved by the owner ("Да, так").

## Goal
You mark the equipment your kitchen has; a recipe's card shows what you lack, and the book can hide the recipes you have nothing to cook with.

## Demo scenarios
1. Settings → "My kitchen" / «Моя кухня»: the equipment dictionary in groups (stovetop cookware, oven and baking, appliances, knives and tools, …) with a search; you tick what you have; the ticks stay.
2. In the editor's "Equipment" section, typing a line offers dictionary entries in Russian and English, with a qualifier after the entry ("Frying pan" + "26 cm"), like an ingredient; free text still goes and is marked as not from the dictionary.
3. On the card, on View and on Cooking, an item your kitchen lacks is marked "not in your kitchen"; with "My kitchen" empty nothing is marked.
4. In the book a filter "Enough equipment" / «Хватает посуды» hides the recipes that need something your kitchen lacks.
5. Recipes and files written before: an equipment line whose text is an entry's name finds its entry by itself; anything else stays free text. Export and import carry the entry.

## Out of scope
- Counts of equipment ("two frying pans"); a shopping list of what is missing.
- The equipment dictionary on a server (it is kept in the repository like the ingredients', the recorded serverless departure).
- The editor's rework and the description / author's notes on steps (v0.29).
- Teaching the `/recipe-file` skill the new file format (outside the repository; the lead notes it).

## Tasks
