# v0.29 — The editor reads like the recipe; each step can carry the author's note
**Status:** shipped
**Planned:** 2026-10-10 · **Shipped:** 2026-10-11

Planned by the lead on 2026-10-10 while the owner slept (the owner's standing request until they wake: after a build, wait 10–15 minutes for feedback, then plan and run the next increment). Its content is the owner's own of 2026-10-10: the editor's ingredients and steps "teem with buttons and fields — something has to be done"; the description stays, each step gets an optional author's part beside its required text, and the main description shows on the card before the ingredients. The way the editor is decluttered (rows that read as text, a sheet to edit one) is the lead's direction, offered to the owner on 2026-10-10 without objection; goal, scenarios and scope are `assumed` and the owner re-decides them at the demo.

## Goal
Writing a recipe shows the recipe itself — each ingredient and step reads as finished text and opens for editing on a tap — and a step can carry the author's own note beside the method.

## Demo scenarios
1. Open a recipe in the editor: each ingredient reads as one line, as on the card ("500 g beets — peeled"); a step reads as its text with its references, its timers ("Simmer · 40 min") and a small picture of its photo or clip; no fields, no icon rows. Rows still drag by their handle.
2. Tap an ingredient: a sheet rises with its name (with the dictionary's offers), note, amount and unit (and divisibility where it applies), and its actions — move up/down, insert above/below, start a group, remove (with Undo). Close it and the row reads the new line. "Add ingredient" opens the same sheet on a new row; closing it empty adds nothing.
3. Tap a step: a sheet holds its text (with "Insert ingredient"), its author's note, its timers, its photo or clip, and its actions. "Add step" opens it on a new step.
4. A step's author's note («Бабушка всегда добавляла щепотку сахара») shows on the card under the step's text, muted, on View and on Cooking. Forking someone else's recipe leaves the notes behind, as it leaves the description; forking your own keeps them.
5. On the card's View tab the description stands right under the title block, before the equipment and the ingredients; in the editor its field sits under the title and tags.
6. On a recipe's View tab, tap an item marked "Not in your kitchen": My kitchen opens at that item; tick it, press Back, and you are back on the same recipe where you were, the mark gone.

## Out of scope
- Rewriting how a step's text references ingredients (the reference spans and the part dialog stay as they are, inside the sheet).
- Any new field beyond the author's note; voice input; templates.
- The equipment section of the editor (already short lines) and the cover, tags and servings controls.
- A landscape or tablet layout of the editor.
