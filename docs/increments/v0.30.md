# v0.30 — A fresh audit of the whole interface, and the fixes it calls for
**Status:** shipped
**Planned:** 2026-10-10 · **Shipped:** 2026-10-11

Asked by the owner on 2026-10-10 while v0.29 was finishing: "the next iteration is a full new audit of all the UI/UX and fixes for the problems found"; start right after v0.29 is sent, the owner will look at both builds on coming home. The audit's findings decide the tasks; the lead slices the most important ones (at most eight) into this increment and lists the rest in `ux-roadmap.md`. Scope and the fixes chosen are `assumed`; the owner re-decides at the demo.

## Goal
Every screen of the app follows current Material 3 practice and reads well in both languages, both themes and at large text — the problems a fresh audit finds most costly are fixed.

## Demo scenarios
1. Walk the book, a recipe's View and Cooking tabs, the editor with its sheets, the settings, My kitchen, the filters, the crop and the dialogs, in light and dark, English and Russian: the fixes listed in this file's tasks are visible where they say.
2. At the largest font size and on a narrow phone nothing is cut, overlapped or out of reach on the screens the fixes touched.

## Out of scope
- New features (the recipe catalogue on GitHub Pages, a recipe's source plate — `later.md`).
- Findings not chosen for this increment — they go to `ux-roadmap.md` with their weight.
- A landscape or tablet layout.

## Audit
Two fresh auditors on 2026-10-10 (develop 0997b29, the v0.29 build): A — the book, the card, My kitchen, the settings; B — the editor and its sheets, the crop, the dialogs. Both rendered the screens themselves (Robolectric) in EN light, EN dark, RU, and at 2× text on a 320 dp phone, besides the store shots. Light and dark contrast held everywhere rendered. Of the 2026-10-04 audit (`ux-roadmap.md`), items 1–3, 5 (book, card), 6–18, 20–25, 27–30, 32–34 are fixed; still open: 4 (the book's header), 5 (the editor's Done and the Add buttons out of thumb reach), 19 (limit marks as separate rows), 26 and 31 in part.

High:
- The part dialog on a `piece` row whose halving is unanswered never lets Compose go idle (3 of 3 runs) — likely an endless recomposition; and such a part would be refused as "whole number of grams" (`IngredientShare.kt` ~L42).
- At 2× text on 320 dp: the editor's title is squeezed beside the language switch («Название» one letter per line); sheet labels and texts break mid-word ("Quantit/y", «Минут/ы», «Изменить ингредиен/т»); the running-timers bar squeezes a timer's name to one letter per line; the book's RU tabs break mid-word ("Отмеченн/ые"); the book's search label wraps and is clipped under the tabs.
- The book's empty and nothing-found states: the extended FAB covers their only way out at 2× text.

Medium (chosen): an ingredient row with no amount reads "g Grains of paradise"; "to taste salt" (also in the store shot); picking a dictionary offer drops the keyboard and breaks the Next chain; Next walks the optional note before the amount; the unit menu in domain order (tsp, tbsp, cup, pinch below the fold); a new row's sheet titled "Edit ingredient" with half its height in actions; the two editor sheets differ from each other and from the source sheets (titles, removal wording, handle); "Insert ingredient" is a bare "@" icon; the empty 4:3 cover pushes the ingredients two screens down; the book menu's Delete is not in the error colour; done marks stay from last time with "Clear marks" only on Cooking and no Undo; the Fork FAB covers the servings control; Cooking's current-step highlight leaves its timers outside and a running timer looks like an idle one; the usual-servings dialog's hand-built −/+ and "Not set" where Cancel belongs; My kitchen reachable only from Settings, one flat list of ~190 items, a group tick of 25 items with no Undo; the book's "Marked" tab holds only imported files in this build; RU says «Посуда и техника», «оборудование» and «посуды» for one thing.

Medium and low not taken here go to `ux-roadmap.md` (2026-10-10 audit).

## Tasks
Order: T-215 and T-219 first; then T-216 and T-220; then T-218 (after T-216) and T-221 (after T-219, T-220); T-217 last (after T-218, T-219). At most two at once.

Standing note for every task: load the `android-m3-ui` skill and follow `docs/architecture/ui-conventions.md`; interface strings in English and Russian; tests wait for conditions, never on timing; 2× text checks of sheets and dialogs use `@Config(fontScale = 2f)`; touch targets ≥ 48 dp (56 dp on the card's Cooking tab); ContrastTest and `ui/PreLaunchSweepTest` green; existing behaviour covered by tests stays unless the task changes it on purpose (then the test is rewritten, never deleted without a replacement); no new libraries. Gradle: the shared `~/.gradle`; never `./gradlew --stop`; narrow `--tests` while iterating, the full `testAll` once; no emulator. Stage explicit paths only.

### T-215 — The part dialog on a piece row
**Status:** done
**Serves:** 1 · **Core:** yes · **Depends on:** —
**Expected files:** `editor/StepTextField.kt` (the part dialog), `core/recipe/IngredientShare.kt`, tests.
**Criteria:**

**Notes:** Cause: not piece rows — any dialog holding a text field at the platform's default width never settled at 360 dp under the tests (its window measured the content at 320 and 360 dp alternately); the part dialog now spans the screen with a 16 dp gutter (on a wide screen it still wraps its content, 328 dp). Review 1 (core; the hang reproduced on a gram row with the width reverted; the core rules mutated): pass; merged 2026-10-10. Not taken: no test that the dialog closes when its row stops offering a part; `NotOffered` falls into the dialog's `else`. No other dialog in the app holds a text field.
