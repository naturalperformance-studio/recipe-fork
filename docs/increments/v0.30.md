# v0.30 — A fresh audit of the whole interface, and the fixes it calls for
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-10 · **Shipped:** —

Asked by the owner on 2026-10-10 while v0.29 was finishing: "the next iteration is a full new audit of all the UI/UX and fixes for the problems found"; start right after v0.29 is sent, the owner will look at both builds on coming home. The audit's findings decide the tasks; the lead slices the most important ones (at most eight) into this increment and lists the rest in `ux-roadmap.md`. Scope and the fixes chosen are `assumed`; the owner re-decides at the demo.

## Goal
Every screen of the app follows current Material 3 practice and reads well in both languages, both themes and at large text — the problems a fresh audit finds most costly are fixed.

## Demo scenarios
1. Walk the book, a recipe's View and Cooking tabs, the editor with its sheets, the settings, My kitchen, the filters, the crop and the dialogs, in light and dark, English and Russian: the fixes listed in this file's tasks are visible where they say.
2. At the largest font size and on a narrow phone nothing is cut, overlapped or out of reach on the screens the fixes touched.

## Out of scope
- New features (the recipe catalogue on GitHub Pages, a recipe's source plate — `later.md`).
- Findings not chosen for this increment — they go to `ux-roadmap.md` with their weight.
- A landscape or tablet layout.
