# v0.31 — Largest text holds, and the Cooking tab follows the cook
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-11 · **Shipped:** —

From the owner's notes on build a713487 (v0.30's Demo feedback, items 1–5), planned with the owner on 2026-10-11.

## Goal
At the largest font the book and the card stay on screen, and on the Cooking tab done steps fold away like a stepper while running and finished timers each gather into one line.

## Demo scenarios
1. With the system font at its largest, open the book → the import button is whole on screen and reads "Import" / «Импорт»; the book's tab titles are all drawn at one size.
2. Open a recipe's card → the servings −/+ buttons are visibly smaller; they still answer a tap anywhere in their 48 dp (View) or 56 dp (Cooking) target.
3. On Cooking, the steps are a vertical M3-style stepper (a numbered circle on a connecting line); marking a step done strikes it through, puts a check in its circle and folds it to one line; the current step is unfolded and highlighted; tapping a folded step unmarks it and it unfolds.
4. Start three timers and scroll away from their steps → the bar at the bottom shows one line: the timer that ends first, its countdown and "+2 more"; tapping the line opens the list, where each timer can be cancelled on its own.
5. Let two timers end → one line "2 timers finished" with one "Stop" that silences them all; the line also opens the list, where each finished timer can be stopped on its own.

## Out of scope
- The system notifications of timers: they stay one per timer.
- Any forward/back control between steps: the current step stays derived from the done marks (FLOW-070).
- The stepper on the View tab: View reads the recipe whole, as now.
- The rest of `ux-roadmap.md` (the book's header, the editor's Done out of thumb reach, …).
- New features in `later.md` (the catalogue on GitHub Pages, a recipe's source plate).
