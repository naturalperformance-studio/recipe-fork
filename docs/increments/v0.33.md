# v0.33 — A recipe and the shopping list on paper or as a PDF
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-12 · **Shipped:** —

Chosen by the owner on 2026-10-12 after v0.32 shipped. Builds FLOW-075 (`spec/70-flows/75-shopping-and-printing.md`), with two choices the owner made at planning: besides the system's print dialog (whose "Save as PDF" is the system's), a "Share as PDF" item hands a PDF file straight to the share sheet; and the shopping list prints and shares the same way.

## Goal
From a recipe's card or the shopping list, the reader prints it or sends it as a PDF — the recipe as its View tab reads at the serving count shown, the list as it stands — with no network involved.

## Demo scenarios
1. Open a recipe, set 4 servings, ⋮ → "Print" → the system's print dialog shows a preview: cover photo, title, its summary, description, equipment, "Ingredients · 4 servings" with the four-serving quantities, numbered steps with their timers and notes; "Save as PDF" in that dialog saves it.
2. Same card, ⋮ → "Share as PDF" → the share sheet offers Telegram, mail, Files…; the file sent opens as a PDF named after the recipe as written ("Лимонный кекс.pdf") with the same pages.
3. A long recipe runs onto a second page: no line is cut between pages, a heading never ends a page, and each page's footer reads the title and "2 / 3".
4. Russian interface → the pages are in Russian; airplane mode on → printing and sharing work the same.
5. The shopping list's ⋮ → "Print" / "Share as PDF" → a page "Shopping list" with its recipes and their servings, the lines to buy each with an empty box to tick by pen, approximate lines with their note, own lines, and what is already "In the basket" at the end, ticked and struck through.

## Out of scope
- Several recipes in one document (the owner chose the recipe and the list).
- Layout choices (font size, columns, leaving out the photo); the page size of a shared PDF is A4.
- Sharing the list as plain text; any service of the product's own.

### T-234 — Pages drawn from blocks
**Status:** running
**Serves:** 1–5 (the drawing every document uses) · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** —
**Expected files:** `app/…/print/` (new: the blocks, the page layout, the PDF writer), tests in `app/src/test` and, if the PDF itself cannot be drawn on the JVM, `app/src/androidTest`.
**Criteria:**
- A document is a title and a list of blocks: a heading, a paragraph, a muted paragraph, a bulleted line, a numbered line (its number hanging), a box line (empty or ticked, ticked ones struck through) with an optional muted second line, and a picture (a local image file). Text blocks wrap to the page's width; text is drawn with the system's sans font and holds Cyrillic.
- Pages: a page size in points (A4 595 × 842 for a shared file; a print job passes its own) with 15 mm margins; blocks laid out top to bottom; a line of text is never cut across pages; a heading is kept with the first line of the block after it; a block longer than a page breaks between its lines; a picture is scaled to fit the text width and at most a third of the page's height, keeping its proportions, and moves whole to the next page if it does not fit; a picture that cannot be read is left out.
- Each page's footer, muted: the document's title (cut with "…" to one line) and "n / m".
- The writer writes the pages as a PDF to a stream and tells how many pages it wrote. Nothing reaches the network.
- Tested: the page breaks (by measured line heights on the JVM where the platform measures text there), the heading kept with what follows, the picture's scale and its move, the footer's numbers; a PDF written from a two-page document holds two pages (read back with `PdfRenderer` — on the device if the JVM cannot).

### T-235 — A recipe as a document
**Status:** planned
**Serves:** 1, 3, 4 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** T-234
**Expected files:** `app/…/print/` (the recipe's document), `strings_print.xml` (en, ru), tests in `app/src/test`.
**Criteria:**
- From a recipe, its held scale (the count and fixings the card shows) and the interface language, the document holds what the View tab shows, in its order: the cover photo where the device holds it, the title (or the untitled wording), the summary line, a stated author and the provenance line where the card shows them, the description, "Equipment" with each item as the card names it, "Ingredients · 4 servings" (the card's count wording, "≈" included; no count where the card shows none) with the ingredient groups as headings and each line as the card writes it, then "Steps", numbered, each step's text with its ingredient references as the card renders them, its timers ("⏱ 10 min") and the author's note muted under it.
- Not on the page: done marks, the kitchen's "you lack" marks, step videos, anything interactive.
- Tested: a recipe scaled to four servings yields the four-serving lines (FLOW-075 (1)); groups, references, timers and notes appear as described; Russian yields Russian wording.

### T-237 — Print and Share as PDF from the card and the list
**Status:** planned
**Serves:** 1, 2, 4, 5 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** T-235, T-236
**Expected files:** `card/CardScreen.kt`, `shopping/ShoppingScreen.kt`, `app/…/print/` (the print adapter, the shared file), `res/xml/file_paths.xml` if a directory is added, strings, tests.
**Criteria:**
- The card's ⋮ (View and Cooking) gains "Print" and "Share as PDF" after the shopping item; the list's ⋮ gains the same two items while the list has lines.
- "Print" hands the document to the system's print service (`PrintManager`) under the recipe's or the list's title; the pages are laid out at the media size the dialog asks for and rewritten when it changes.
- "Share as PDF" writes an A4 PDF under the cache through the existing `FileProvider` (only the latest such file is kept), named by the recipe's title as written (characters a file name cannot hold replaced, blank → the untitled wording), `.pdf`; the list's file is named by the localized "Shopping list", and opens the share sheet with it (`application/pdf`); a failure to write says so in a notice and changes nothing.
- The recipe prints at the count and fixings the card shows at that moment (FLOW-075 (1)); nothing is sent by the product itself (FLOW-075 (2)).
- Tested: the menu items and their order; the share intent's type, name and readable file; the print call's title (the print service itself is the platform's).

## Decision log
- 2026-10-12 `decided` v0.33 is printing and PDF (FLOW-075); the PDF comes both through the system's print dialog and as a "Share as PDF" file for the share sheet; the shopping list prints and shares too, several recipes in one document do not (the owner, at planning).
- 2026-10-12 `assumed` The pages are drawn by the app (`PdfDocument`) rather than through a WebView, so that the same drawing serves the print job and the shared file with no hidden-API tricks and no network; a shared PDF is A4 with 15 mm margins, one column, the system sans font; the interface language decides the page's language, as on the card; the cover photo at most a third of a page; footer "title · n / m".
- 2026-10-12 `assumed` The printed recipe is the View tab without its interactive parts: no done marks, no "you lack", no videos; the list prints whole, what is to buy first with empty boxes, the basket last, ticked and struck through.
- 2026-10-12 `decided` A shared PDF keeps the recipe's own title as its name, Cyrillic included — the ASCII export name (v0.22) answered Telegram losing the unknown `.recipefork` extension, which a `.pdf` does not risk (the owner, after planning); checked in Telegram at the demo.
