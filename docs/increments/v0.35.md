# v0.35 — Metric or US units, chosen once, shown everywhere
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-12 · **Shipped:** —

Chosen by the owner on 2026-10-12 after v0.34 shipped, over a bug-fix increment, collections and the Google Play preparation. Builds H-003 (`analytics/hypotheses.md`, adopted 2026-10-10; no requirements exist yet). The owner's direction at planning: one unit setting for the whole app; the editor offers the chosen system's units and recipes show amounts in it, converting where a recipe was written in the other — never both systems at once; and the full kitchen conversion: between mass and volume too, through the dictionary's measures, marked approximate. Departures for `/req-forge --sync`: SCALE-018 and `spec/decisions/20260927-stated-quantities-lie-on-the-grid.md` (a cup has no single volume; mass never converts to volume) and DOM-011 (comparison factors never yield a displayed quantity) — the card now shows "≈" amounts derived from an entry's measures, as the shopping list already does (v0.32).

## Goal
A reader in the US sees "flour ≈ 2 cups, butter 4 oz, milk 1 cup"; a reader with kitchen scales sees "мука ≈ 260 г, масло 113 г, молоко 240 мл" — the same recipe, in the system they chose, on the card, in Cooking, in the shopping list and on paper; authors write in their own system.

## Demo scenarios
1. Settings → "Units": Metric / US. A phone set to the US region starts on US, any other on Metric.
2. Metric: a recipe written in cups and ounces reads in grams and millilitres — "flour ≈ 260 g" (from 2 cups), "butter 113 g" (from 4 oz), "milk ≈ 250 ml" (from 1 cup of milk, by the dictionary's cup); teaspoons, tablespoons, pinches and pieces stay as written.
3. US: a recipe written in grams and millilitres reads in US units — "flour ≈ 2 cups" (from 260 g), "beef 17½ oz" (from 500 g, nothing in cups), "milk 1 cup" (from 250 ml), "oil 1 tbsp" (from 15 ml).
4. Scaling, fixing an amount, step references, the shopping list and the printed page all follow the chosen system; a converted line through the dictionary is marked "≈".
5. The editor offers the chosen system's units — US: oz, lb, fl oz, cup, tbsp, tsp, pinch, pc; Metric: g, kg, ml, l, cup, tbsp, tsp, pinch, pc; a row already written in the other system keeps its unit as written.

## Out of scope
- Temperatures (°C/°F) and lengths (a 24 cm tin) in step text or equipment.
- Imperial (UK) units; per-recipe overrides of the setting.
- Converting a recipe's stored amounts: the author's numbers stay as written; only the display converts.

## Rules decided at planning (`assumed` unless marked)
- Units of each system. Shared by both: piece, pinch, to taste, tsp, tbsp (always shown as written). Metric: g, kg, t, ml, l, m³. US: oz (28.349523125 g), lb (16 oz), fl oz (29.5735295625 ml), and the cup when converting into US (a US cup, 8 fl oz). A recipe may hold units of either system.
- Into Metric: oz, lb → g/kg exactly; fl oz → ml exactly; cup → grams through the entry's grams-per-cup measure, or millilitres where the entry has a grams-per-ml measure (a liquid), "≈"; a cup with no measure for its entry (or free text) stays a cup.
- Into US: g, kg, t → cups (kitchen fractions) where the entry has a cup measure and the amount is at least ¼ cup, else tbsp/tsp where it has those measures, "≈"; otherwise oz, on ½ oz up to 2 lb (written in lb only when a whole ¼ lb, "1½ lb"), then lb on ¼ lb, exactly. ml, l → cups / tbsp / tsp by the US cup (exact volume, rounded on the kitchen grid); oz/lb/fl oz and cups stay.
- The amount is scaled first, in the unit it was written in, then converted, then rounded once on the target unit's grid (SCALE-003 holds: rounding only on display). Exact conversions round silently like scaled amounts; conversions through the dictionary carry "≈".
- Fixing an amount on a converted line: the reader types it in the shown unit; it is converted back (exactly, or by the same measure) to fix the scale.
- The editor offers the chosen system's units plus the shared ones; a row in the other system keeps its unit, shown as written.
