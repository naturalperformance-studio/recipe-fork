# v0.36 — Collections of recipes
**Status:** shipped
**Planned:** 2026-10-12 · **Shipped:** 2026-10-13

Chosen by the owner on 2026-10-12 after v0.35 shipped, over a bug-fix increment, manual editing of the shopping list and the Google Play preparation. The owner's idea (later.md, 2026-10-12): authors group their recipes into collections and share a whole collection; the book's Imported view gains a Collections section, own recipes can be sorted into collections too; e.g. a "New Year's menu" goes into the shopping list in one action. Decided with the owner at planning (2026-10-12): one concept named **Collection** («Коллекция») — "New Year's menu" is just a collection's name; a collection that came in a file is **not editable**, like a found recipe; **a recipe may be in several collections**; importing the same collection again **adds what is missing** (recipes already held are untouched; the name and order come from the file). New domain concept with no requirements yet — departures for `/req-forge --sync`: collections, their file, and FLOW-064's import of one recipe per file.

**Handoff:** the owner asked to start development in a fresh session ("Готовь скоуп, стартанем разработку в свежей сессии"). Nothing of v0.36 is built; develop = master + docs (v0.35 shipped as a9d721a). Run `/team-lead`, which picks this file up; start with T-252.

## Goal
The reader gathers recipes — their own and found ones — into named collections, opens a collection to cook from it, puts a whole collection into the shopping list at once, and hands a collection to someone as one file that arrives in their book as a collection.

## Demo scenarios
1. On a card, ⋮ → "Add to collection" → a sheet lists the reader's collections with checkboxes and "New collection…"; tick "New Year's menu" and "Salads" → the recipe is in both.
2. The book's "My recipes" view shows a Collections row above the recipes (each collection with its name and count); tap "New Year's menu" → its screen lists its recipes in order; tap one → its card. The collection's ⋮: Rename, Share, Add all to shopping list, Delete (the recipes stay in the book); a recipe's ⋮ in it: Remove from collection; long-press drag reorders.
3. ⋮ → "Add all to shopping list" → every recipe of the collection is in the list at the serving count its card would open with; a notice with "Open".
4. ⋮ → "Share" → the share sheet with "New Year's menu.recipefork-collection" (the collection and every recipe in it, own and found, with their photos and clips).
5. A friend taps that file in Telegram → the app imports it: its recipes arrive as found recipes (those already in the book are not doubled), and "Imported" shows a Collections section with "New Year's menu" — readable, shoppable, deletable, not editable. The author sends an updated file → importing it again adds the new recipes to the book and the collection, keeps the others, and takes the file's name and order.
6. Deleting a recipe from the book takes it out of every collection; deleting a collection leaves its recipes in the book. A saved book (v0.34) carries the collections, and restoring it brings them back.

## Out of scope
- Collections on the server, or a catalogue of collections.
- A collection's own servings per recipe ("menu for 8") — the list takes each recipe's usual count; changing it there is as today.
- Nested collections; a cover picture for a collection; sorting other than the order the reader sets.
- Editing an imported collection (decided: not editable).

## Rules decided at planning (`assumed` unless marked)
- A collection has an identity (UUID), a name, an order of recipe ids, an origin (own or imported), and, when imported, the name of its stated author if the file gives one. One book holds its collections; they are book data, not device settings.
- Own collections hold any recipe of the book, own or found. Imported collections are frozen: the reader may open, shop, share onward and delete them; renaming, reordering and removing recipes are not offered.
- Deleting a recipe removes it from every collection. Deleting a collection never deletes a recipe.
- The collection file: extension `.recipefork-collection`, a zip holding `collection.json` (a format version, the collection's id, name, stated author, the ordered recipe ids) and each recipe as the recipe file holds it (document + media) under its own folder; built from the recipe file's code, written with the rules a recipe export has (FLOW-063: a found recipe keeps its stated author and parent).
- Importing a collection: each recipe is imported as `.recipefork` is today (found, frozen; skipped if the book already holds it, by id); the collection is created as imported, or, if the book already holds a collection of that id, gets the file's name and order and the recipes it lacks. The reader's own collection of the same id (they shared it and re-import it) is not touched beyond adding nothing — import into an own collection is refused with a notice ("You already have this collection").
- "Add all to shopping list": each recipe at the count its card would open with; a recipe already in the list keeps its count; one notice "Added N recipes" with "Open".
- The saved book file carries collections (they live in the book's database); restore Add adds the collections and memberships the book lacks; restore Replace makes them the file's.
