# v0.37 — Acting on several recipes from the list
**Status:** demo
**Planned:** 2026-10-13 · **Shipped:** —

Scope written by the owner on 2026-10-13 after v0.36 shipped: select recipes in the book's list and then add them to a collection, add them to the shopping list, export them to PDF or print them — none of it requiring a collection — or delete them; every one of these actions also from a single entry's ⋮ in the list; and print and PDF for a collection. Decided with the owner at planning (2026-10-13): the book keeps its two views ("My recipes" and "Imported"); selection lives within the view it was started in — a merged list was left for later. No requirements exist for selection — departures for `/req-forge --sync`: selection in the book and its bulk actions, a printed or PDF document of several recipes and of a collection (FLOW-075 prints one recipe).

## Goal
The reader selects several recipes in the book and, in one action, adds them to a collection or the shopping list, prints them, saves them as one PDF, or deletes them; each of these is in a single entry's ⋮ too, and a collection prints and saves as PDF.

## Demo scenarios
1. **Selection.** A long press on a recipe in "My recipes" selects it; the top bar shows "1 selected"; taps add or take off recipes; the bar's ⋮ has "Select all"; ✕ or Back leaves selection. The same in "Imported".
2. **Into a collection.** Select 3 → "Add to collection" → a sheet of the reader's collections and "New collection…"; choosing one puts all three in it, and a notice says "3 recipes added to “…”". Recipes already in it are not doubled.
3. **Into the shopping list.** Select → "Add to shopping list" → each is on the list once, at its usual servings, as "Add all to shopping list" of a collection does; a notice with "Open".
4. **PDF and print.** Select → "Save as PDF" → one file: a contents page, then each recipe from a new page. "Print" hands the same document to the system's printing.
5. **Deletion.** Select several own recipes → "Delete" → they leave at once, asking nothing; a notice "4 recipes deleted" with "Undo", which brings them all back with their collections. In "Imported" the action is "Remove".
6. **One at a time.** A recipe's ⋮ in the list has "Add to collection", "Add to shopping list", "Print", "Save as PDF" and "Delete" ("Remove"); the file export stays where it was.
7. **A collection.** The collection's ⋮ has "Print" and "Save as PDF": a first page with the collection's name and its recipes in order, then each recipe from a new page.

## Out of scope
- Selection inside a collection's screen; removing several recipes from a collection at once.
- Selection across both views at once — it lives in the view it was started in.
- Exporting several recipes as one `.recipefork` file (a collection's Share does that).
- Choosing servings or scale when adding several recipes to the shopping list.
- A merged "My recipes + Imported" list (owner, 2026-10-13: later, if needed).

## Rules decided at planning (`assumed` unless marked)
- Selection starts with a long press on an entry and ends with ✕, Back, a finished action (every action but "Select all" ends it), or switching view. While it lasts a tap toggles an entry instead of opening it; the top app bar is replaced by a contextual bar: ✕, "N selected", the actions as icons where they fit and the rest in its ⋮. Filters and search stay as they are; "Select all" selects every entry the list shows under the current filters.
- An entry that cannot take an action is still selectable; the action applies to those it can and the notice counts only them (e.g. an own recipe whose state forbids deletion (ST-010) in the standard build is kept and not counted). An action that applies to none of the selection is not offered.
- Bulk deletion is one undoable step: one notice, one Undo putting every recipe back with its collection memberships; the leaving becomes final when the notice goes, as one recipe's does (FLOW-053).
- "Add to collection" for several recipes adds to one chosen collection (no checkboxes: membership differs per recipe); "New collection…" creates it and adds them. Own collections only, as on the card.
- "Add to shopping list" for several recipes: each at the count its card would open with; one already on the list keeps its count (as a collection's "Add all"); for a single entry from the list, the same as the card's item does at the opening count.
- The document of several recipes (and of a collection): a first page with the title ("Recipes" or the collection's name) and the numbered list of the recipes' titles, then each recipe as one recipe prints today (FLOW-075), each starting a new page; one PDF named after the title; one print job. A single recipe from the list prints as from its card.
- Print and PDF are offered in both builds, as on the card.

### T-266 — A long document holds its pictures a page at a time
**Status:** done
**Serves:** 4, 7 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** T-262
**Expected files:** `print/PrintPages.kt` (and `PagesPrintAdapter.kt` / `PdfShare.kt` only if they hold pictures), tests.
**Criteria:**
- Laying a document out reads only each picture's size; a picture is decoded when the page that holds it is drawn, at no more pixels than its frame needs at print resolution, and released once that page is drawn — so a document's memory does not grow with its number of pictures.
- A picture that cannot be read is still left out as today; one that fails to decode at drawing leaves its space empty rather than failing the document.
- Single-recipe print and PDF look as before (same pages, same placements).
- Tested: a document of many pictures holds no decoded picture after layout and at most one page's worth while writing (a counting decoder seam), the downscaling, the unreadable cases, and the existing print tests unchanged.
**Notes:** The developer found the platform `PdfDocument` keeps each finished page's pixels until `writeTo`, so page-at-a-time decoding alone does not bound a PDF's memory; the lead added a per-document pixel budget (L2) in the same run. Passed review first time (recycling after `finishPage` checked against AOSP: the recorded page holds its own reference). Review nits: a single recipe with ~28+ full-width photos is scaled slightly by the budget; past several hundred pictures the 300 px floor lets memory grow again (~200 KB each); print preview decodes a page's pictures on each `onWrite`; `PdfWrittenOnDeviceTest` exercises recycle-then-write on a device (T-265's run).
