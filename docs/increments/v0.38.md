# v0.38 — A fresh Material 3 audit, its fixes, and the bugs found on the phone
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-13 · **Shipped:** —

Asked by the owner on 2026-10-13 after v0.37 shipped: run the interface audit again, because new elements have appeared since 2026-10-10 (collections, the shopping list, printing, backup, units, selection), to see how far the app follows Material 3; then an iteration that fixes, first, the interface and, second, the bugs found so far. The audit's findings and `later.md`'s bugs decide the tasks; the lead chose the most costly ones (eight tasks); the rest go to `ux-roadmap.md`. Which fixes were chosen is `assumed`; the owner re-decides at the demo.

## Goal
Every screen — the new ones included — holds at the largest text and reads the same way in both languages, the book and the card behave as Material 3 apps do, and the bugs seen on the owner's phone are fixed.

## Demo scenarios
1. **Largest text.** At the largest font size on a narrow phone: the book, the shopping list, a collection, the card, the settings and its dialogs, the photo source sheet — no word broken in the middle, no title cut or spilling under the bar, no button covering the first line, a notice's "Undo" on its own line.
2. **The book.** The first start opens on "My recipes"; search and filter chips scroll away with the list and come back on a scroll up; while recipes are selected, a swipe or a tab does not drop the selection; a notice ends when the view changes; Undo of a deleted top recipe shows it back in view; the selection bar is tinted and a long press gives a tick.
3. **Shopping list and collections.** Each line has a checkbox; "Add item" shrinks on scroll; the titles fit on one line; the empty states say what to do now; the collection sheets have a drag handle and the selection's sheet says how many recipes it adds.
4. **The card and its timers.** A timer without a name is called briefly, not by the whole step; cancelling a running timer gives "Undo" that brings back the same end time; the Fork button and the tabs behave at large text and when scrolled.
5. **Printing and menus.** Print or Share as PDF from the card, a collection or the shopping list shows "Preparing the document" while it is made; the recipe's file action has one name everywhere, and the ⋮ menus list their items in one order.
6. **The editor.** A field over its limit turns red with the message and a counter under it; a tag is removed only by its ×; a new step's field says what goes there; a recipe that cannot be changed shows no empty headings and says what to do; typing "chick" offers "chicken" first; "Insert ingredient" lists each ingredient once.
7. **Bugs from the phone.** A 15-second clip from the camera is accepted; a small GIF is accepted on the owner's phone; the device tests run on a phone set to Russian.

## Out of scope
- The timer that did not ring with the app closed on the owner's phone (`later.md`, T-243) — it needs the phone attached to diagnose; picked up when it is.
- The editor's Done and Add buttons out of thumb reach, the equipment rows as a third editing pattern, the crop's controls and colours, the checklist as a sheet, the file-message dialogs, the unit menu, the settings' section headers, My kitchen's indent, Cooking's two touch areas on an ingredient line — to `ux-roadmap.md` (audit of 2026-10-13).
- New features (the catalogue, a recipe's source plate, merging shopping lines by hand).
- A landscape or tablet layout.

## Audit
Two fresh auditors on 2026-10-13 (develop a078ef5, the v0.37 build): A — the book with its selection, collections, the shopping list, the card; B — the editor and its sheets, the crop, the settings with backup, units and My kitchen, dialogs and import. Both rendered their screens (Robolectric) in EN light, EN dark, RU, and at 2× text on a 320 dp phone. Dark contrast held everywhere rendered. Of the 2026-10-10 audit, still open: the book's header, the editor's Done/Add reach, limit marks as separate rows, equipment rows, the free-text mark per row, offers' order, the frozen recipe, the tag chip, the unnamed timer, the crop, Cooking's two targets, the Undo snackbar across tabs.

High:
- The book at 2× on 320 dp: the title bar, tabs, search and chips take about half the screen; no recipe title readable on the first screen; 42% stays pinned after scrolling.
- The shopping list's and a collection's bar title wrap to two lines at 2× and spill under the bar, overlapping the list.
- Words broken mid-word at 2×: "Serving/s" in the usual-servings dialog; «Сфотографирова/ть» in the source sheet, «Необязатель/но» on the cover tile, «перетаскивани/я» in Settings.

Medium (chosen): the shopping list's "Add item" never shrinks and covers the first line; shopping lines show no checkbox; print and PDF show no progress from the card, a collection or the shopping list; Undo notices at 2× squeeze the text beside the action; a swipe or a tab drops a selection; the collection's "Share" beside "Share as PDF" while the entry calls the file "Export"; "Written here" in EN vs «Мои рецепты» in RU; the RU imported tab cut at 2×; a running timer cancelled with no Undo; the unnamed timer repeats the whole step; limit marks outside the fields; the tag chip removes on any tap without Undo; the new step's field has no label; the frozen recipe's empty heading and dead end; offers put leaves before "chicken"; the insert menu lists each ingredient twice.

Not taken: see Out of scope; the rest are low and go to `ux-roadmap.md`.

## Rules decided at planning (`assumed` unless marked)
- At large text (font scale ≥ 1.5) a notice puts its action on its own line; an extended FAB starts collapsed to its icon and extends only at the top of the list.
- The book's first start (no view remembered) opens on "My recipes"; the English own view is named "My recipes", as in Russian.
- While selection lasts, the view does not change: the pager does not swipe, the tabs, search, chips and the collections row do not respond; ✕ and Back end it as before.
- The recipe's file action is called "Export" / «Экспорт» everywhere (an entry, a collection); the ⋮ order everywhere is: Add to collection, Add to shopping list, Print, Share as PDF, Export, then Delete / Remove.
- A timer without a name is called by the step's first words, cut at a word to about 24 characters with "…"; the notification uses the same short name.
- A field over its limit shows the message in the field's supporting text, in the error colour, with a counter; nothing else changes about when the mark appears (FLOW-028).
- A tag is removed only from its ×; the rest of the chip does nothing.

## Tasks
Order: T-267 and T-271 together; then T-268 and T-272; then T-270 and T-273; then T-274; T-269 last (after T-267, T-268, T-270). At most two developer agents at once.

Standing note for every task: load the `android-m3-ui` skill for any screen change and follow `docs/architecture/ui-conventions.md`; interface strings in English and Russian; tests wait for conditions, never on timing; 2× text checks use `@Config(fontScale = 2f)` on a 320 dp-wide qualifier; touch targets ≥ 48 dp (56 dp on Cooking); ContrastTest and `ui/PreLaunchSweepTest` green; existing behaviour covered by tests stays unless the task changes it on purpose (then the test is rewritten, never deleted without a replacement); no new libraries. Gradle: every command as `systemd-run --user --scope --quiet -p MemoryMax=10G gradle-queue ./gradlew …` (00-index.md), a device script as `systemd-run --user --scope --quiet -p MemoryMax=12G sg kvm -c 'gradle-queue scripts/device-test.sh'`; the shared `~/.gradle`; never `--stop`; never `adb kill-server`; narrow `--tests` while iterating, the full `assembleAll testAll` once on the final code (and report totals from the result XMLs); no emulator unless the task says so. Stage explicit paths only.
