# v0.39 — A recipe from pasted text
**Status:** demo
**Planned:** 2026-10-15 · **Shipped:** —

Asked by the owner on 2026-10-15: a static (deterministic, no language model) parser that turns a recipe written as plain text into a recipe of the app's model — ingredients, equipment, timers, steps and the rest. Planned while v0.38 waits in `demo`; this increment builds on `develop` as it stands.

## Goal
The owner pastes or shares the text of a recipe in Russian or English and gets an editable recipe of their own with dictionary-bound ingredients and quantities, equipment, steps whose quantities are references, and timers — fixing by hand only what the parser could not read — while the original text stays one tap away and easy to copy.

## Demo scenarios
1. **Paste.** My recipes → New recipe → Paste text → paste "Бургерный соус на йогурте" (from the owner's `Cooking/рецепты`) → the editor opens on a new recipe of the owner's: title, servings, nine ingredients; "½ зубчика чеснока" is garlic ½ pc with the qualifier, "щепотка соли" is salt 1 pinch, "3–5 г мёда" is honey 5 g; lines the dictionary knows are bound to it.
2. **Steps.** "Добавить 100 г сахара" becomes a reference to the sugar row that scales with servings; "варить 35–40 минут" gives a 40-minute timer, "1 ч 30 мин" a 1:30 timer.
3. **Equipment and groups.** Equipment named in the text (a frying pan, the oven) appears in the equipment list; sub-headings like "Тесто" / "Начинка" become ingredient groups.
4. **Share from another app.** Select a recipe's text in a browser or Telegram → Share → recipe-fork → the same result as pasting.
5. **English.** "2 cups flour, 1 tbsp butter … bake for 25 min" reads the same way.
6. **The original text.** On the card of a recipe made from text, "Original text" opens the text exactly as pasted, with one Copy action.
7. **No recipe.** Text with no recipe in it gives a plain message; nothing is created.

## Out of scope
- Fetching a page by URL, photos and OCR, languages other than Russian and English, a language model.
- Tags, a cover, the source's notes split into step notes (they go to the description).
- Converting US units to metric (oz and lb exist in the model as they are).
- The server-side social import of `spec/68-import` (a language model, posts, moderation) — untouched.
- A command-line tool for `/recipe-file` over the same parser — a later task once the parser lives in `:core`.

### T-284 — The parser's weak spots the corpus shows
**Status:** done
**Serves:** scenarios 1, 3, 5 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** T-281
**Expected files:** `core/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/core/paste/{TextLayout,PasteLines,IngredientLine,UnitWords}.kt`, their tests, the corpus test's floors.
**Criteria:**
- The eda.ru shape — a name on one line and its amount on the next ("Говядина на кости" / "800 г") — reads as one ingredient line; its servings ("Порции" / "8") are read.
- Count words for whole things read as pieces with the word as qualifier when the dictionary has no entry for them: "1 головка чеснока", "1 банка фасоли", "1 пакетик ванилина", "2 мультистакана риса" (the multicooker cup as a cup).
- "мука — 4 ст. л. + для обваливания" reads the quantity; the rest goes to the note.
- "Makes 24 cookies" stays no servings (a count of pieces), but servings stated inside a title ("… (порций 8)") are read.
- The owner's "Яблочный порошок из кожуры": its equipment lines (аэрогриль, кофемолка, сито) are equipment, not ingredients.
- A quantity after a comma clause reads: "гауда, тёртая — 40 г" → 40 g, note "тёртая"; "красный лук — половина луковицы, примерно 100 г" reads a quantity.
- Two amounts joined by "+" are read: "20 г укропа + 15 г петрушки" → two rows; "1 яйцо + 1 желток" → egg 1 with the rest in the note (or two rows when both bind); "30 г томатной пасты + 60 мл воды" → two rows.
- A label before a colon is a group, not part of the name: "Крем: 200 г мацони", "Пропитка: 60 мл …"; "~120 г густого соуса: «…»" reads 120 g.
- Units "мультистакан", "головка", "стручок", "пучок", "веточка" are read.
- A bare duration word is a timer: "прогреть минуту" → 1 min, "минимум час" → 60 min, "a minute", "an hour" — not "на минуту меньше".
- The corpus floors are raised to what the corpus then measures, rounded down.
- The corpus test can also run, opt-in, over an outside directory of texts (a system property naming it; skipped when unset) and writes the measures and report for it; nothing from that directory is committed.
**Notes:** Fix round 1: rules stopped at the corpus's own forms (a plus between two amounts of one name, eda.ru amounts, genitive number words, a container before a two-word name, bare hour words). Fix round 2 failed on "for an hour before …" and compound numbers in words; the lead fixed them (ac6b510, bca981b) after fresh reviews. Corpus: quantity read 99.3 %, bound 95.9 %, timed 99.6 % (floors 0.99 / 0.95 / 0.99). Held-out site set (206 recipes, not tuned to): 92.4 % / 93.5 % / 100 %.
