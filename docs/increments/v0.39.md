# v0.39 — A recipe from pasted text
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-15 · **Shipped:** —

Asked by the owner on 2026-10-15: a static (deterministic, no language model) parser that turns a recipe written as plain text into a recipe of the app's model — ingredients, equipment, timers, steps and the rest. Planned while v0.38 waits in `demo`; this increment builds on `develop` as it stands.

## Goal
The owner pastes or shares the text of a recipe in Russian or English and gets an editable recipe of their own with dictionary-bound ingredients and quantities, equipment, steps whose quantities are references, and timers — fixing by hand only what the parser could not read — while the original text stays one tap away and easy to copy.

## Demo scenarios
1. **Paste.** My recipes → New recipe → Paste text → paste "Бургерный соус на йогурте" (from the owner's `Cooking/рецепты`) → the editor opens on a new recipe of the owner's: title, servings, nine ingredients; "½ зубчика чеснока" is garlic ½ pc with the qualifier, "щепотка соли" is salt 1 pinch, "3–5 г мёда" is honey 5 g; lines the dictionary knows are bound to it.
2. **Steps.** "Добавить 100 г сахара" becomes a reference to the sugar row that scales with servings; "варить 35–40 минут" gives a 40-minute timer, "1 ч 30 мин" a 1:30 timer.
3. **Equipment and groups.** Equipment named in the text (a frying pan, the oven) appears in the equipment list; sub-headings like "Тесто" / "Начинка" become ingredient groups.
4. **Share from another app.** Select a recipe's text in a browser or Telegram → Share → recipe-fork → the same result as pasting.
5. **English.** "2 cups flour, 1 tbsp butter … bake for 25 min" reads the same way.
6. **The original text.** On the card of a recipe made from text, "Original text" opens the text exactly as pasted, with one Copy action.
7. **No recipe.** Text with no recipe in it gives a plain message; nothing is created.

## Out of scope
- Fetching a page by URL, photos and OCR, languages other than Russian and English, a language model.
- Tags, a cover, the source's notes split into step notes (they go to the description).
- Converting US units to metric (oz and lb exist in the model as they are).
- The server-side social import of `spec/68-import` (a language model, posts, moderation) — untouched.
- A command-line tool for `/recipe-file` over the same parser — a later task once the parser lives in `:core`.

### T-276 — The layout of a recipe text
**Status:** running
**Serves:** scenarios 1, 3, 5, 7 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** —
**Expected files:** `core/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/core/paste/TextLayout.kt` (new) and its tests.
**Criteria:**
- Reads plain text and Markdown (headings, bullets, numbered lists, bold) into: title, servings, description lines, ingredient lines with their group headings, step lines, note lines — and the recipe's language (ru / en by script and words).
- Ingredient and step sections are found by headings ("Ингредиенты", "Вам понадобится", "Приготовление", "Способ приготовления", "Ingredients", "Method", "Directions", "Instructions", …) and, without headings, by shape: a run of short lines starting with a quantity is ingredients, numbered or long lines after it are steps.
- Servings from "на 4 порции", "Порций: 4", "Serves 4", "Yield: 4 servings"; a count of pieces ("12 сырников") is not servings.
- A step's leading bold title ("**Крахмал.** Размешать…") stays part of the step text.
- A text with no ingredient lines or no step lines is "no recipe".
**Notes:** Fix round 1 (2026-10-15): before review the lead sent it back to read Markdown ingredient tables (the owner's Лимонные полоски); review then found a stack overflow on long rule lines, bare "STEP N" lines kept as steps, and a sentence-ended ingredient line ending the run. Sweep over the owner's 44 recipes: 43 read; the 44th is a shopping list.

### T-277 — An ingredient line
**Status:** todo
**Serves:** scenarios 1, 5 · **Core:** yes · **Depends on:** T-275, T-276
**Expected files:** `core/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/core/paste/IngredientLine.kt`, `core/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/core/paste/UnitWords.kt` (new) and their tests.
**Criteria:**
- Quantities: whole and decimal (`,` and `.`), `1/2`, `1 1/2`, `½`, `1½`, ranges `3–5` / `3-5` / `от 3 до 5` (the larger), words for small numbers ("пол", "половина", "один", "half", "a", "an").
- Units in Russian and English with abbreviations, dots and inflection (г, гр, грамм(ов|а), кг, мл, л, ст. л., столовая ложка, ч. л., стакан(а|ов), щепотка, шт, штук(и), g, kg, ml, tbsp, tablespoon(s), tsp, cup(s), pinch, oz, lb, …), mapped to the model's units; "по вкусу" / "to taste" is to-taste; a clove ("зубчик", "clove") is pieces with the qualifier when the dictionary has no entry for it.
- The name is bound through T-275; what follows the name and qualifies *what it is* ("9%", "без кожи") is the qualifier; preparation ("натереть", "мелко рубленного", "комнатной температуры", "finely chopped", a trailing comma clause) goes to the row's note within 60 characters; a parenthesis goes to the note or is dropped when too long (the original text keeps it).
- A quantity off its unit's grid snaps as `/recipe-file` does (⅛ tsp → 1 pinch, 2.5 g → 3 g); a line that cannot be read as a quantity keeps its text in `enteredQuantity` and the row is a free-text or bound row "as written", never lost.
- Divisibility of pieces comes from the entry (`effectiveDivisible`).
- Every row built passes the model's invariants; a unit test per line shape above.

### T-278 — Steps: references, timers, equipment; the assembled recipe
**Status:** todo
**Serves:** scenarios 1–3, 5, 7 · **Core:** yes · **Depends on:** T-277
**Expected files:** `core/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/core/paste/StepLine.kt`, `core/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/core/paste/RecipeFromText.kt` (new) and their tests.
**Criteria:**
- A quantity with a unit before an ingredient's name in a step ("100 г сахара") becomes a reference to that row at that offset, with a share when it is part of the row's amount, and the quantity's text is removed so the reference reads in its place; spacing follows `ReferenceSpacing`.
- An ingredient named in a step for the first time without a quantity gets a reference before its name when the row has a quantity.
- Durations ("20 минут", "1 ч 30 мин", "полчаса", "35–40 минут" → 40, "25 min", "1 hour", "1½ hours") become timers on the step (whole minutes, 1 min – 99 h 59 min, at most five, label empty); the text keeps its words.
- Equipment named in the steps or an equipment section is bound to the equipment dictionary (inflected: "на сковороде" → frying pan, "в духовке" → oven), each entry once, at most 15.
- `RecipeFromText.read(text, dictionaries, at, owner)` returns a LOCAL recipe or "no recipe"; over-limit content is cut to the limits (50 ingredients, 50 steps, lengths); every recipe it returns passes `RecipeFileCheck` when written as a document.
