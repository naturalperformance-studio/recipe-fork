# v0.40 — Approximate nutrition of a dish
**Status:** shipped
**Planned:** 2026-10-22 · **Shipped:** 2026-10-22

Asked by the owner on 2026-10-22: give the dictionary's ingredients calories, protein, fat and carbohydrates, and work out roughly what a dish holds. The spec says nothing about nutrition; this increment is a departure for `/req-forge --sync` (a new capability, not a contradiction of a goal or non-goal).

## Goal
On a recipe's card the reader sees roughly how many calories, and how much protein, fat and carbohydrate, the dish holds — per serving and for the whole dish — following the servings as they change, with the rows that could not be counted named.

## Demo scenarios
1. **The section.** Open a recipe whose ingredients are dictionary entries in grams → between the ingredients and the steps, a section «Пищевая ценность, примерно»: kcal, protein, fat and carbohydrates, per serving.
2. **Servings.** Switch to «Всё блюдо», then change servings 4 → 8 → per serving stays the same; the whole dish doubles.
3. **Left out.** A recipe with "salt to taste", a free-text ingredient and a row without a quantity → «Подробнее» lists them as not counted, each with its reason; the totals count the rest.
4. **Pieces and spoons.** "2 eggs", "1 tbsp oil" → counted through the entry's piece and spoon measures (or the row's own piece weight).
5. **Breakdown.** «Подробнее» → the kcal each counted row contributes, and one line on how it is counted.
6. **Nothing to count.** A recipe where no row can be counted → no section.
7. **English.** The same in the English interface, in full words.

## Out of scope
- Entering nutrition by hand: for a free-text ingredient, or overriding a dictionary entry's values in a recipe. Free-text rows are "not counted".
- Cooking losses, a cooked weight, values per 100 g of the dish.
- Daily norms, % of a daily intake, goals, a food diary; any colour judgement of a dish.
- Nutrition on Cooking, in the book's list, the shopping list, PDF and print; ordering or filtering by calories.
- The server: its dictionary store and API carry no nutrition (serverless is what ships).
- Fibre, sugar, salt, vitamins and any other value.

### T-287 — Nutrition per 100 g in the dictionary format
**Status:** done
**Serves:** scenarios 1, 4 · **Core:** no · **Visible:** no · **Depends on:** —
**Expected files:** `core/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/core/api/DictionaryContract.kt`, `core/src/dictionary/kotlin/app/recipefork/core/dictionary/*`, `core/src/test/kotlin/app/recipefork/core/dictionary/*`, `dictionary/README.md`, `app/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/dictionary/*`, `app/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/data/AppDatabase.kt`, their tests; a handful of entries in one source file as the format's first data (e.g. sugar, butter, wheat flour, egg, water), with the regenerated asset.
**Criteria:**
- An ingredient source line may carry two more trailing columns: `nutrition` — `kcal=<n> protein=<n> fat=<n> carbs=<n>`, all four or none, per 100 g of the raw product, each a finite number ≥ 0 (kcal ≤ 900, each gram value ≤ 100, protein + fat + carbs ≤ 100) — and `nutritionSource` — `fdc:<FoodData Central id>` or `skurikhin:<the table's product name>`, required when `nutrition` is present and refused without it. The generator refuses malformed values, naming `file:line`.
- The asset carries the four values per entry (`IngredientEntryDto.nutrition`, absent when none); the source column stays in the sources only. An entry without nutrition is written exactly as before, so its digest does not change.
- The device's dictionary store keeps the values (a store version bump, re-seeded from the asset like versions 2–4).
- The README documents both columns and the two sources (USDA FoodData Central, public domain; I. M. Skurikhin's tables of Russian food composition).
**Notes:** passed review first time (2026-10-22); nits left: `kcal=-0` and exponent forms pass like the factors cell; the 1e-9 sum allowance is not in the README. A server refresh would drop the device's nutrition while the server carries none (by scope).

### T-288 — The nutrition of a dish
**Status:** done
**Serves:** scenarios 1–6 · **Core:** yes · **Visible:** no · **Depends on:** T-287
**Expected files:** `core/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/core/nutrition/*` (new feature package), `core/src/test/kotlin/app/recipefork/core/nutrition/*`, the module map in `docs/architecture/00-index.md` (the lead updates it at merge).
**Criteria:**
- Given a recipe's ingredients, the scale in force (the card's factor) and the dictionary entries, it returns the whole dish's kcal, protein, fat and carbohydrates and, when a serving count is shown, the values per serving (whole ÷ servings shown); per serving does not change with servings, the whole dish scales with the factor.
- Each row's grams: g, kg, mg, oz, lb exactly; ml, l, fl oz by the entry's `ml` measure; pieces by the row's own piece weight, else the entry's `pc` measure; tsp, tbsp, cup, pinch by the entry's measure for that unit.
- A row is left out with one reason, in this order: free text («нет в словаре»), to taste («по вкусу»), no quantity («без количества»), no measure for its unit («нет меры»), no values on the entry or its parent («нет данных»). The result lists the left-out rows with their reasons.
- A child entry without its own values takes its parent's.
- Totals are summed from exact values and rounded once (kcal to the nearest 10, grams to whole grams); each row's kcal in the breakdown is rounded on its own. A recipe with no countable row gives no result.
**Notes:** one fix round: values past 64 bits were truncated (now whole numbers of any size). Passed the second review; 24 + 19 mutants killed. Nits left: a row the card shows "as written" (off its grid) still counts, scaled; servings ≤ 0 throw — the card passes only a positive count.

### T-292 — Nutrition of seasonings and the rest
**Status:** done
**Serves:** scenarios 1, 3, 4 · **Core:** no · **Visible:** no · **Depends on:** T-287
**Expected files:** `dictionary/seasoning*.tsv`, the regenerated asset.
**Criteria:** see *Data tasks*.

**Data tasks (T-289–T-292).**
- Every top-level entry of the family gets values and a source; a child gets its own values only where it differs from its parent's in a way a cook would notice (wings vs. a whole chicken, 20 % vs. 10 % sour cream), else it inherits.
- Source order (F-1): USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy or Foundation, raw where a raw form exists) by default, cited `fdc:<id>`; Skurikhin's tables for products that are Russian-specific or where USDA's nearest food differs meaningfully (творог, кефир, ряженка, сметана, сало, гречка-ядрица and the like), cited by the table's product name. A value is copied from the cited source, never estimated; an entry neither source covers fairly is left without values (it shows as «нет данных»).
- Water, ice and the like get zeros, cited like any other entry.
- The generator accepts the sources, the shipped-dictionary test passes; the asset is regenerated, never edited.
- Review bar: a value that differs from its cited source, or a cited food that is plainly not the entry, is a `must`; the choice between two fair analogues is a `nit`. The reviewer checks a random sample of at least 30 entries against the cited source.
- The asset is a merge conflict between data tasks by design: the lead takes either side and rebuilds it at merge (`dictionary/README.md`, *Merging*).
