# v0.41 — Paper and ink: the redesign's theme, type and icon
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-22 · **Shipped:** —

First increment of the visual redesign the owner decided on 2026-10-15 (`docs/brand/identity/decisions.md`, `redesign-1-recipe-fork.html`, `redesign-1-fixes.html`, values in `port-1-rodoslovnaya.html` § «Как переносить в Compose», the mark in `mark-2.html` option 2б). On 2026-10-22 the owner asked, before going to sleep, to start the redesign once v0.40 finished and to keep going without them; the plan below and the split into increments (`redesign-roadmap.md`) are the lead's, `assumed`, re-decided at the demo. The redesign is presentation only: no behaviour changes beyond those decisions.md records and spec/ already carries (sync 0a52567).

## Goal
Every screen takes the redesign's look at once — paper-and-ink colours in light and dark, Literata for what is read and Golos Text for what is pressed and counted, the new mark as the launcher and notification icon — while nothing moves.

## Demo scenarios
1. **Light.** Open the book, a card, the editor, the settings → cream paper, olive actions and tabs; titles and reading text in a book serif (Literata); buttons, chips, tabs and timers in Golos Text.
2. **Dark.** Settings → Theme → Dark → a warm dark brown, not grey; olive and beet lighter; everything readable.
3. **Beet is provenance.** Open your version of an imported recipe → the «Основано на рецепте «…»» line is beet; nothing else in the app is beet.
4. **The glyph.** The «Своя версия» button carries the provenance glyph (hollow node, one knee, filled node) instead of the old fork; the same glyph heads the «У вас уже есть своя версия» dialog.
5. **The icon.** Home screen → the two-tine fork with the beet tine, on paper. With themed icons on (Android 13+) → its one-colour version. Start a timer and leave the app → the notification's small icon is the fork's thick drawing.
6. **Largest text.** Largest font size on a narrow phone → Cooking's current step, the card's title and the book hold: no word broken mid-word, nothing clipped.

## Out of scope
- Every layout change of the redesign: book entries as contents rows, collection spines, selection actions at the bottom, the sort chip (v0.42); the drawn provenance line, quantity column, steps in the margin, pencil notes and the graphite colour (v0.43); the Cooking plate, round done marks, timer pills (v0.44); underline fields in the editor and the rest of the inventory (v0.45).
- Engraved empty-state drawings (v0.42), store screenshots and uploading the icon to Play (v0.46).
- The product's name (OQ-016).
