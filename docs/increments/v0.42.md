## Goal
The book reads as a cookbook's table of contents — four to six titles a screen, your versions saying what they were made from, collections as narrow spines, the order and the selection actions in words.

## Demo scenarios
1. **Title and tabs.** Open the app → «Моя книга рецептов» as a title in the book serif under the cart and settings icons; «Мои рецепты» and «Из файлов» as tabs with an olive underline. Scrolling hides the title and brings it back on a scroll up.
2. **Contents rows.** «Мои рецепты» with a dozen recipes → each a row: a 60 dp cover thumbnail, or the title's first letter as a drop cap when there is no cover; the title in the serif; tags under it; hairlines between rows, no cards. At least four rows fit the first screen of a 360 × 780 phone.
3. **Your version.** A version of an imported recipe → its row carries the provenance glyph and «по рецепту «Шакшука»». An imported recipe's row shows «Автор: … (указан в файле, не проверен)» in italics.
4. **Collections.** With three collections → a row of narrow spines (name and count) above the recipes; tapping one opens it.
5. **Order.** The filter row starts with «Все фильтры», then a chip «Сначала новые ▾»; choosing «По названию» changes the chip's words. The search field's hint is no longer cut.
6. **Selection.** Long-press a recipe → top: «Выбрано 1», «Выбрать все», close; bottom: five labelled actions «В коллекцию», «В покупки», «Печать», «PDF», «Удалить».
7. **Empty.** A fresh book → the engraved mark, the same words and one button; an empty shopping list → the engraved cart.

## Out of scope
- The card, Cooking, the editor, the shopping list's rows, collections' own screen, My kitchen, settings (v0.43–v0.45).
- New functions; the order, filters, import and removal behave as now.
