## Goal
A recipe's card reads like a page of a good cookbook: the origin as one beet line with its knee above the title, quantities in a column of their own, step numbers in the margin, your own notes pencilled in the margin.

## Demo scenarios
1. **Origin.** Open your version of an imported recipe → above the title one line «по рецепту «Шакшука» ›» with a hollow node, a knee and a filled node, in beet; tapping the line opens the parent, whose own card names its author. A version whose parent is not in the book → a dashed node, the line is not a button, and under it, small and muted, the stated author («Кухня на Васильевском · автор из файла, не проверен»).
2. **Title.** The title in the book serif, large; the summary («8 ингредиентов · 4 шага · таймеры ≈ 21 мин») in the working face under it; no empty space where there is no cover.
3. **Quantities.** Ingredients: quantities in a column on the left in the working face («2 ст. л.», «800 г»), names in the serif, the preparation muted; ↑ ↓ in the column; group headings in small capitals. At the largest font the column moves above the name, nothing cut.
4. **Steps.** Step numbers large in the margin; quantity references in the working face, following the servings; timers as duration labels.
5. **Pencil notes.** Your own version's step notes stand in the margin in pencil: italics, a dashed rule, a pencil mark, in graphite — distinct from everything else on the page.
6. **Menu.** ⋮ → … «Поделиться PDF», «Экспорт», «Снять отметки», «Удалить» (in the error colour).
7. **Equipment.** An item you have → a filled square; one you lack → an empty square and the words «Нет на кухне»; tapping leads to My kitchen as now.

## Out of scope
- Cooking (v0.44): the current step plate, done circles, timer pills.
- The editor, the shopping list, settings (v0.45).
- Any change to what the card does: forking, servings, fixing an amount, timers behave as now.
