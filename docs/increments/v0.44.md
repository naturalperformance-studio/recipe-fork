# v0.44 — Nutrition per 100 g of the finished dish
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-22 · **Shipped:** —

Asked by the owner on 2026-10-22 after v0.40: nutrition per 100 g. Placed before the paused redesign v0.43 resumes (the owner's choice); the redesign roadmap's later increments move one number up. The spec says nothing on nutrition or a dish's weight; a departure for `/req-forge --sync`.

## Goal
The author writes down what the finished dish weighs, and the card shows its approximate nutrition per 100 g.

## Demo scenarios
1. **The weight.** Editor → under «Порций» the field «Вес готового блюда», grams → type 560 → save.
2. **Per 100 g.** The card's nutrition switch reads «100 г | Порция | Всё блюдо» → «100 г» shows the tiles per 100 g of the finished dish.
3. **Servings.** Change servings 2 → 4 → per 100 g stays the same; «Подробнее» names the weight scaled («1 120 г на 4 порции»).
4. **No weight.** A recipe without a weight → the switch is «Порция | Всё блюдо»; on your own recipe «Подробнее» says how to get per 100 g.
5. **Carried.** Export the recipe and import it; make your version of it → the weight comes along.
6. **Older recipes.** Recipes and files from before → open as before, without a weight.

## Out of scope
- Per 100 g of the raw ingredients; estimating the weight in any way.
- Reading «Выход: 800 г» from pasted text.
- A warning that the ingredients changed after weighing.
- Per 100 g on Cooking, in print and PDF, in the book.
