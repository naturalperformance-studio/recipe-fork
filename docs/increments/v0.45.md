Decided design for every task: `docs/brand/identity/decisions.md` (2026-10-15, items 4 and 9) wins over every mock-up; `docs/brand/identity/redesign-1-recipe-fork.html` § 4.4 (Cooking, four frames) with `docs/brand/identity/redesign-1-fixes.html` § 4 («Отменить» next to a running timer); values in `docs/brand/identity/port-1-rodoslovnaya.html` (§ Compose); `docs/design/decisions-recipe-fork.md`.

## Goal
On Cooking the cook sees at a glance where they are, what is done and what is running, without reading small text with wet hands.

## Demo scenarios
1. **Marks.** Cooking → tap an ingredient or a step → a round mark with a tick, the line struck through and muted; a done step collapses to one struck line.
2. **The current step.** The first unmarked step stands on the olive plate in large Literata with «Текущий шаг · 3 из 4» above it, visible; mark it → the plate moves to the next step.
3. **A running timer.** Start a step's timer → one 56 dp control «04:12 Яйца · идёт», a quieter «Отменить таймер» under it; «Вернуть» after cancelling still works.
4. **Timers at the bottom.** A timer whose step is off screen stands in the bottom bar as a pill; a ringing one first, with the word «Звенит» and a filled «Стоп»; tapping the row brings its step.
5. **Nothing lost.** Fixing an amount («Сколько у вас есть»), the «Этот таймер уже идёт» dialog, the screen staying awake — all work as before.

## Out of scope
- «Всё собрано · показать» (collapsing a fully gathered ingredients or equipment group), unless the build already has it.
- A forced dark page on Cooking: Cooking follows the app's theme as today.
- Step numbers in the margin and dotted rules on Cooking.
- View, the editor, the shopping list, collections, My kitchen, settings (v0.46); the «Фильтры» sheet; one icon family; store graphics (v0.47).
- Any change to what is stored: marks, timers and fixing keep their current data.
