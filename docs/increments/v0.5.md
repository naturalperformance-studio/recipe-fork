# v0.5 — Photos in a recipe
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-02 · **Shipped:** —

Goal, scenarios and out-of-scope below were approved by the user on 2026-10-02.

## Goal
A recipe has a cover and its steps have photos: they show on the card and travel with the recipe to a friend.

## What exists at the start
On `develop` @ cab43d1 (v0.4 shipped). The book has a `media` table (`MediaEntity`: id, owner, kind `PHOTO`/`VIDEO`, attachment cover or step, visibility); the model carries `MediaAttachment.Cover` / `ToStep`; editor insert/move/delete of steps already keep step media with their step (T-068). The importer (T-076, T-079) accepts photos (JPEG/PNG/WebP by leading bytes, ≤ 1 MiB) and writes them to `filesDir/book-pool-media/<uuid>`; export writes an empty `media/` (`RecipeArchive.write`). Nothing in the app attaches, processes or shows a photo. The hand-test build is the serverless APK (`DELIVER_APK=recipe-fork-serverless-debug.apk scripts/deliver.sh`).

## Demo scenarios
1. In the editor, at the title, choose "Cover" → take a photo or pick one from the gallery → the app says it shrank the photo and removed its data (location included) → the cover shows at the top of the card.
2. At a step choose "Photo" and add a picture → on the card it stands at its step; a second photo on that step replaces the first; a photo can be removed; moving the step moves its photo.
3. A file that cannot be read or does not shrink to 1 MB is not attached, and the app says why.
4. Export a recipe with photos and open the file on another device → the photos are there; an own fork keeps them; a fork of a found recipe takes none; deleting a recipe deletes its photo files.
5. The editor: steps are framed like ingredients; the drag handle is a narrow column on the left, the full height of the block, for steps and ingredients alike; the horizontal strip is gone.
6. Import outcomes ("already in the book", refusals) and a failed export are shown in full, never cut off.

## Out of scope
- Video (MEDIA-004, MEDIA-007).
- The 150 MB allowance and anything about sending (MEDIA-010, MEDIA-011, FLOW-004's sending half).
- The setting for holding media on the device (FLOW-011, FLOW-060).
- A left-handed placement of the drag column.
- A full-screen photo viewer.

## Tasks
Order: T-080, T-081 and T-084 start together; T-082 follows T-080 and T-081; T-083 follows T-082.

Standing note for every task: leaving the editor shows the card, and tests reach the book through `compose.backThroughTheCard()`. UI tests use the `junit4.v2` Compose rules, wait for conditions (`PATIENCE_MS`), never on timing, and must pass in a full-suite run, not only alone. If the book schema changes, bump its version and add a Room migration — the user's phone holds recipes. Mode-dependent behaviour is tested in both modes.

### T-080 — Framed steps and a drag column on the left
**Status:** running
**Serves:** scenario 5 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** —
**Expected files:** `app/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/editor/IngredientsSection.kt`, `StepsSection.kt`, `Reorder.kt`; tests under `app/src/test/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/editor/`. Not `StepTextField.kt`; not the title area of `EditorScreen.kt` (T-081).
**Criteria:**
- Each editable step is one visibly bounded block like an ingredient (outlined card), separated from the next; its text, insert-ingredient control, timer, remove and menu sit inside it; a frozen recipe's steps are framed too.
- On both steps and ingredients the drag handle is a narrow column at the block's left edge, the full height of the block, lightly tinted with the handle icon centred; a drag started anywhere in the column moves the block; a touch or scroll started anywhere else never drags. The horizontal strip is gone.
- The column takes no more width than needed for a 48 dp touch target; the free-text name keeps its room at 2.0 font scale (T-073's measuring tests pass); inputs inside a block share one left edge.
- Scrolling the editor by a finger that starts on the inputs, labels or blank parts of a block scrolls, never drags (test).
- The row menu's Move up/down stay; a frozen recipe shows no column.

### T-081 — A cover photo, processed on the device
**Status:** running
**Serves:** scenarios 1, 3 · **Core:** yes · **Depends on:** —
**Expected files:** new `app/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/media/*` (taking or picking a photo, re-encoding, storing, removing); `core/.../recipe/*` only if a media rule belongs there; the title area of `editor/EditorScreen.kt` (or a new `editor/CoverSection.kt`); the top of `card/CardScreen.kt` (or a new `card/Cover.kt`); `recipe/RecipeRepository.kt` / `book/*` for storing; `AndroidManifest.xml` (FileProvider path for the camera's output, if used); new `strings_media.xml` (both); tests under `app/src/test/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/media/`, `.../editor/`, `.../card/`.
**Criteria:**
- At the title the editor offers a cover, described as optional, from the camera or the gallery, asking for no runtime permission (system camera intent, system photo picker) (FLOW-026 (1, 3)).
- A photo is re-encoded on the device to at most 1 MB and at most 1600 px on its longer edge (MEDIA-002), keeping only the orientation needed to show it the right way up — no location, device, time or any other metadata (MEDIA-003); tested by reading the stored file's metadata.
- The author is told the photo was shrunk and its data removed (MEDIA-005).
- A file that cannot be decoded, or does not fit 1 MB after re-encoding, attaches nothing and says why (MEDIA-006, MEDIA-008); a conversion failing half-way leaves no file behind.
- A second cover replaces the first, whose file is deleted (MEDIA-009); the cover can be removed, deleting its file.
- The cover shows at the top of the card, with a text alternative; a recipe without a cover shows nothing in its place.
- The cover survives leaving the editor and reopening the book from disk; a frozen recipe shows its cover read-only.

### T-083 — Photos travel in files and forks
**Status:** todo
**Serves:** scenario 4 · **Core:** yes · **Depends on:** T-082
**Expected files:** `app/.../file/RecipeArchive.kt`, `RecipeExport.kt` (writing media), `core/.../file/*` if the document needs it; `core/.../recipe/Fork.kt`, `card/Forking.kt` (an own fork copies the files); `recipe/RecipeRepository.kt`, `library/*` (deleting a recipe deletes its files); tests under `.../file/`, `.../card/`, `.../library/`, `core/src/test/...`.
**Criteria:**
- Export writes every photo the recipe holds into `media/` as the device holds it, and the document names each (FLOW-063); a file exported with photos and imported on another device shows them at the cover and at their steps.
- An own fork copies its parent's photo files under new ids (FORK-007); a fork of a found recipe carries none (FORK-002).
- Deleting a recipe, or removing a found recipe, deletes the photo files nothing else holds (MEDIA-012, device side); a photo a fork copied survives its parent's deletion.
- A recipe whose photo file is missing on disk still opens, exports without that photo, and says nothing alarming.
