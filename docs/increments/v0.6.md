# v0.6 — A compact editor and the app's own camera
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-02 · **Shipped:** —

Goal, scenarios and out-of-scope below were approved by the user on 2026-10-02.

## Goal
The editor's blocks are compact, and the camera shoots inside the app in the frame the recipe uses.

## What exists at the start
On `develop` @ a921b3d (v0.5 shipped). A step block (`editor/StepsSection.kt`) is an outlined card with a 48 dp drag column on the left and one column of children: the text field beside the text buttons "Remove" and ⋮, then "Insert ingredient", "Timer" and "Photo" (`editor/StepPhoto.kt`), each on a line of its own. An ingredient block (`editor/IngredientsSection.kt`) has the name with a bin icon and ⋮ (stacked when narrow), then quantity and unit. "Take a photo" launches the system camera app (`media/PhotoSources.kt`, `media/CameraFiles.kt`) into a FileProvider file; the result goes through `PhotoAttaching` → `PhotoStore` (re-encode ≤ 1 MB / 1600 px, no metadata). The app holds no runtime permission besides notifications.

## Demo scenarios
1. A step in the editor: its text takes the block's full width; under it one row of icon buttons — insert ingredient, timer, photo, remove, ⋮ — each with a spoken label and a tooltip on long press; the timer form and the photo show below that row only when present. A one-line step is about a third of today's height.
2. An ingredient in the same style: icon buttons, no text buttons.
3. "Take a photo" opens the app's own camera: the viewfinder shows exactly the 4:3 landscape frame that goes into the recipe, with the phone held upright; the shot goes through the same processing as now.
4. The first "Take a photo" asks for the camera permission, never at start; refused, an explanation with a way to the settings, and "Choose from gallery" still works.
5. In the recipe book, each recipe's entry shows its cover as a small picture; an entry without a cover has none, and the list stays readable.

## Out of scope
- Video, flash, zoom, the front camera.
- A left-handed drag column; a full-screen photo viewer.

### T-087 — The app's own camera
**Status:** running
**Serves:** scenarios 3, 4 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** —
**Expected files:** `gradle/libs.versions.toml`, `app/build.gradle.kts` (CameraX); a new `app/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/media/camera/*` (the camera screen); `media/PhotoSources.kt` (launching it instead of the system camera), `media/CameraFiles.kt` if its temp handling changes; `AndroidManifest.xml` (CAMERA permission, `uses-feature` not required); `strings_media.xml` (both); tests under `app/src/test/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/media/`. Not `editor/StepPhoto.kt` or `editor/CoverSection.kt` beyond what launching needs (T-085 changes StepPhoto.kt).
**Criteria:**
- "Take a photo" — for the cover and for a step — opens an in-app camera screen with a live viewfinder framed at 4:3 landscape while the phone is held upright; the shutter takes a photo of exactly that frame (what the viewfinder shows is what is stored), which then goes through `PhotoAttaching` like any photo (≤ 1 MB, ≤ 1600 px, no metadata).
- Back or a close button leaves the camera attaching nothing; the camera is released when the screen leaves.
- The camera permission is requested on the first "Take a photo" and at no earlier moment; refused, the screen explains that the camera needs it, offers the app's settings, and "Choose from gallery" works; refused permanently, the explanation goes straight to the settings.
- A device with no back camera says so and attaches nothing.
- The system-camera launch is removed; no other permission is added; the APK grows by no more than ~2 MB.

### T-088 — The cover on the book's entries
**Status:** done
**Serves:** scenario 5 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** —
**Expected files:** `library/LibraryScreen.kt` (own and marked entries); `media/PhotoImage.kt` only for a thumbnail-sized decode; `book/*` / `recipe/RecipeRepository.kt` if the entry row needs the cover's id; tests under `app/src/test/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/library/`. **Criteria:**

- The picture is decoded off the main thread at thumbnail size; scrolling a long list stays smooth (no full-size decode per entry).

**Notes:** From the user's request while v0.6 was planned. Review round 1 found one `must`: the cover's slot appeared only once decoded, so each title jumped right as thumbnails arrived. Fix round 1 holds the slot whenever the cover file exists (tinted until drawn), decodes to the thumbnail's exact size, and sizes the cache by the device. Re-review passed; merged. Nits left: a cover file arriving after an entry is shown appears only after it scrolls back; the size test does not exercise the scale-down path. Unrelated, seen in review: `AndroidConnectivityTest` (a lost-and-returned connection) failed once by timeout — flaky.
