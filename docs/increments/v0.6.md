# v0.6 — A compact editor and the app's own camera
**Status:** demo
**Planned:** 2026-10-02 · **Shipped:** —

Goal, scenarios and out-of-scope below were approved by the user on 2026-10-02.

## Goal
The editor's blocks are compact, and the camera shoots inside the app in the frame the recipe uses.

## What exists at the start
On `develop` @ a921b3d (v0.5 shipped). A step block (`editor/StepsSection.kt`) is an outlined card with a 48 dp drag column on the left and one column of children: the text field beside the text buttons "Remove" and ⋮, then "Insert ingredient", "Timer" and "Photo" (`editor/StepPhoto.kt`), each on a line of its own. An ingredient block (`editor/IngredientsSection.kt`) has the name with a bin icon and ⋮ (stacked when narrow), then quantity and unit. "Take a photo" launches the system camera app (`media/PhotoSources.kt`, `media/CameraFiles.kt`) into a FileProvider file; the result goes through `PhotoAttaching` → `PhotoStore` (re-encode ≤ 1 MB / 1600 px, no metadata). The app holds no runtime permission besides notifications.

## Demo scenarios
1. A step in the editor: its text takes the block's full width; under it one row of icon buttons — insert ingredient, timer, photo, remove, ⋮ — each with a spoken label and a tooltip on long press; the timer form and the photo show below that row only when present. A one-line step is about a third of today's height.
2. An ingredient in the same style: icon buttons, no text buttons.
3. "Take a photo" opens the app's own camera: the viewfinder shows exactly the 4:3 landscape frame that goes into the recipe, with the phone held upright; the shot goes through the same processing as now.
4. The first "Take a photo" asks for the camera permission, never at start; refused, an explanation with a way to the settings, and "Choose from gallery" still works.
5. In the recipe book, each recipe's entry shows its cover as a small picture; an entry without a cover has none, and the list stays readable.

## Out of scope
- Video, flash, zoom, the front camera.
- A left-handed drag column; a full-screen photo viewer.

## Tasks
Order: T-085, T-087 and T-088 start together; T-086 follows T-085.

Standing note for every task: leaving the editor shows the card, and tests reach the book through `compose.backThroughTheCard()`. UI tests use the `junit4.v2` Compose rules, wait for conditions (`PATIENCE_MS`), never on timing, and must pass in a full-suite run, not only alone. Layout is checked at 360 dp and 2.0 font scale in Russian.

### T-085 — A compact step block
**Status:** done
**Serves:** scenario 1 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** —
**Expected files:** `editor/StepsSection.kt`, `editor/StepPhoto.kt` (the photo control as an icon), a new `editor/BlockActions.kt` (the icon row, reusable by T-086); `strings_editor.xml`, `strings_media.xml` (both); tests under `app/src/test/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/editor/`. Not `StepTextField.kt`'s keyboard logic; not `media/*` (T-087).
**Criteria:**
- The step's text field takes the full width of the block beside the drag column; nothing sits beside it.
- Under the text one row holds icon buttons: insert ingredient, timer, photo, remove, ⋮ — each 48 dp, with a spoken label naming the step ("Insert an ingredient into step 2", …) and a tooltip on long press; no text buttons remain in the step block.
- The timer form and the photo (with its message) show below the row only when the step has them; the timer's and photo's existing behaviour, refusals and tests are unchanged.
- A one-line step block is at most about 40 % of its v0.5 height at 360 dp and 1.0 (measured); at 2.0 font scale every control stays whole and 48 dp, the row wrapping onto a second line if it must.
- A frozen recipe's step block shows no action icons.
**Notes:** From the v0.5 demo feedback. A one-line step measures 38.8 % of its v0.5 height (the v0.5 block is rebuilt in a test fixture). Passed review on the first round — the step field moved from OutlinedTextField to BasicTextField with the outlined decoration, and the review read the keyboard path line by line: value, change, buffer, styling, undo and `@` reach the field as before; the "N." is a decoration prefix, not text; merged. Nits left: the selection-colour provider OutlinedTextField added is gone (the theme supplies the same); the decoration runs the styling once more per change; "Remove step N" is spoken twice.

### T-087 — The app's own camera
**Status:** done
**Serves:** scenarios 3, 4 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** —
**Expected files:** `gradle/libs.versions.toml`, `app/build.gradle.kts` (CameraX); a new `app/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/media/camera/*` (the camera screen); `media/PhotoSources.kt` (launching it instead of the system camera), `media/CameraFiles.kt` if its temp handling changes; `AndroidManifest.xml` (CAMERA permission, `uses-feature` not required); `strings_media.xml` (both); tests under `app/src/test/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/media/`. Not `editor/StepPhoto.kt` or `editor/CoverSection.kt` beyond what launching needs (T-085 changes StepPhoto.kt).
**Criteria:**
- "Take a photo" — for the cover and for a step — opens an in-app camera screen with a live viewfinder framed at 4:3 landscape while the phone is held upright; the shutter takes a photo of exactly that frame (what the viewfinder shows is what is stored), which then goes through `PhotoAttaching` like any photo (≤ 1 MB, ≤ 1600 px, no metadata).
- Back or a close button leaves the camera attaching nothing; the camera is released when the screen leaves.
- The camera permission is requested on the first "Take a photo" and at no earlier moment; refused, the screen explains that the camera needs it, offers the app's settings, and "Choose from gallery" works; refused permanently, the explanation goes straight to the settings.
- A device with no back camera says so and attaches nothing.
- The system-camera launch is removed; no other permission is added; the APK grows by no more than ~2 MB.
**Notes:** CameraX 1.6.2; a real-emulator test (`CameraXFrameTest`, API 29) confirms the stored picture is the 4:3 landscape frame. The APK grew far beyond the ~2 MB criterion (+10.2 MB debug) — taken to the user as F-1: accepted, R8 first in v0.7. Passed review on the first round (crop, rotation, permission timing and camera release each broken once and caught); merged. Nits left: a second tap on "Take a photo" while the system permission dialog is up reads as a refusal and shows the explanation behind it; a 0→180° turn is not picked up (shot upside down); no test of closing during a capture or of recreation mid-permission; stale KDoc about a camera file.

## Forks
### F-1 — The APK grows by ~10 MB with CameraX: accept, or shrink the release build? (answered 2026-10-02)
CameraX 1.6.2 adds +10.2 MB to the debug APK (38.9 → 49.2 MB) and +8.4 MB to an unshrunk release; with R8 and resource shrinking it costs +1.4 MB (a release APK of 4.8 MB in all). The debug APK the user installs is now 47 MiB, close to the Telegram bot's 50 MB limit for delivery. Options: (a) accept as is; (b) a task enabling R8 for release (keep rules for Room, Ktor, serialization) and delivering the release build — overturns the v0.2 `decided` "no release build with R8 for now"; (c) drop camera-view and draw the viewfinder on a TextureView (about +4.4 MB; departs from the ADR). Recommendation: (a) for v0.6, (b) as the first task of v0.7 before the APK outgrows delivery. Nothing waits: T-087 continues under (a). Answer: (a) now, (b) as the first task of v0.7.
