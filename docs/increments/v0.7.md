# v0.7 — The card keeps your household and your place
**Status:** demo
**Planned:** 2026-10-03 · **Shipped:** —

Goal, scenarios and out-of-scope below were approved by the user on 2026-10-03.

## Goal
Every recipe opens at your usual serving count; a card you left — even after the system ended the app — comes back at the same count, fixing and place; and the build the user installs is about 5 MB.

## What exists at the start
On `develop` @ 55b2958 (v0.6 shipped). The card (`card/CardScreen.kt`) opens at the authored count every time and holds its count and fixing in composition state; nothing survives leaving the card (spec index "Known departures": FLOW-017 (6, 7)). `LibrarySession` holds which view or card is shown. The only build type with a config is `debug`; release is unminified; the hand-test artefact is `recipe-fork-serverless-debug.apk` (49 MB), delivered by `DELIVER_APK=… scripts/deliver.sh`. F-1 of v0.6 decided a release build with R8 becomes the hand-test build.

## Demo scenarios
1. In the book set "I usually cook for 2" → a recipe for 4 opens at 2 with its quantities marked as recalculated; setting it back to 4 shows no marks.
2. Set 6 on a card, go to the book, open another recipe, come back → the first still shows 6; remove the app from the recent apps and open it again → the usual count again.
3. Scroll a recipe to its middle, fix an ingredient, start a timer, press Home; come back — also after the system ended the app in the background → the same recipe, count and fixing, the same line at the top, the timer showing the right time left.
4. The build is about 5 MB; export and import, the camera, timers that ring and photos all work in it.

## Out of scope
- Everything in `later.md` (full-screen camera with crop, the book's ⋮ menu, the left-handed column).
- Search and filters in the book (FLOW-035); the server.

**Criteria:**

- With the setting at 2, a recipe authored for 4 opens showing 2 servings and every changed quantity carries its derived marking (FLOW-017 (1)); never set, a recipe opens at its authored count with nothing marked (FLOW-017 (2)).

- A recipe with no serving count ignores the setting.
**Notes:** Review round 1 passed the mutation check on every criterion and found one `must`: a stored value of another type under the setting's key would crash the app at launch (`getString` throws). The lead merged on the developer's evidence that the new tests fail without the fix.

### T-091 — The card survives leaving the app
**Status:** done
**Serves:** scenarios 2, 3 · **Core:** yes · **Depends on:** T-090
**Expected files:** `library/LibrarySession.kt`, `card/CardScreen.kt`, `card/FixAmount.kt` (state that survives), `card/StepsList.kt` / `IngredientsList.kt` (the scroll anchor); tests under `app/src/test/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/card/`, `.../library/`.
**Criteria:**
- A count set on a card is kept for that recipe for the rest of the session: leaving it for the book or another recipe and coming back shows that count (FLOW-017 (7)); a new session — the app removed from the recent apps, or the device restarted — opens every recipe at the usual count again (FLOW-017 (6), FLOW-058). The process ended by the system while away does not end the session (FLOW-058 (2)).
- Leaving the app and returning, or the system ending the process while away, shows the recipe that was open, at the same count, with the same fixed ingredient, and with the same ingredient line or step at the top of the screen (FLOW-012 (1, 3, 5)); a running timer shows the time left by the clock (FLOW-012 (2)).
- The line or step at the top is restored even when the font scale or the orientation changed while away (FLOW-012 (5)).
- Tests use `StateRestorationTester` / activity recreation for process death, and a fresh activity without saved state for a new session.
**Notes:** Held per recipe in the library's SavedStateHandle as short strings (count "n/d", fixing with a hash of its row, the top line as a key and an offset). Review round 1 caught most mutations; two tests could not fail — a pixel restore passed the font/orientation tests (Robolectric did not measure text), and the row guard had none. Fix round 1 moved those tests to NATIVE graphics with wrapping lines, added row-guard tests, fixed a real bug real measurement surfaced (the pin could release on a late scroll value of its own), and capped held cards at 50. Re-review found the cap evicted a card just read but unchanged. With the budget spent, the lead fixed it (reading a held card counts as showing it) with a failing-without test; a fresh review passed; merged. Nits left: the pin's remembered set and the timer-step guard are defensive, not pinned by a test; the same "shown" on `heldAnchor` is untested.
