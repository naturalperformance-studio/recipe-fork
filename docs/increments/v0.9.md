# v0.9 — The phone's own camera, a quieter editor, a share of an ingredient
**Status:** active
**Planned:** 2026-10-03 · **Shipped:** —

The scope was set by the user on 2026-10-03: "everything listed + the camera" — the photo message as a pop-up, a camera like the Claude app's, and the later list (a reference to part of an ingredient, a left-handed drag column). The user went to sleep and asked for the build to be ready by morning; goal and scenarios below are the lead's reading of that.

## Goal
Photos are taken with the phone's own camera and cropped in the app, the editor no longer fills with messages, a step can reference part of an ingredient, and the drag column can sit on the right for a left hand.

## What exists at the start
On `develop` @ e1facd3 (v0.8 shipped). "Take a photo" opens the app's own CameraX camera (`media/camera/*`), full-screen, then a 4:3 crop (`media/crop/*`) that gallery photos share; the app asks for the camera permission on the first shot. A photo's processing message (`media/PhotoNotice.kt`) is a line under the cover or step photo in the editor (`editor/CoverSection.kt`, `editor/StepPhoto.kt`). A step reference (`IngredientReference(ingredientPosition, textOffset)`) always reads the ingredient's whole quantity, scaled (`core/recipe/StepText.kt`, `card/StepText.kt`). The drag column sits at the left of every step and ingredient block (`editor/Reorder.kt`).

## Demo scenarios
1. "Take a photo" opens the phone's own camera app — the one the Claude app opens (the user's screenshots: Samsung's camera with zoom, flash and lens switch) — and the shot goes straight into the app's 4:3 crop; the app no longer asks for the camera permission.
2. Attaching, refusing or removing a photo says so in a pop-up at the bottom of the screen that goes away by itself; nothing is added under the photo.
3. In a step, insert garlic and choose "part": enter 2 of its 4 cloves → the step reads "2 зубчика", another step reads the other 2; at double servings they read 4 and 4; the ingredient list still shows 8.
4. In the book's ⋮ menu, "Drag handle on the right" moves every step's and ingredient's drag column to the right side; it is kept on the device.

## Out of scope
- Video; the server; a camera of the app's own.

### T-098 — The phone's own camera, then the crop
**Status:** running
**Serves:** scenario 1 · **Core:** no · **Depends on:** —
**Expected files:** `media/PhotoSources.kt` (launch the system camera into a FileProvider file, then the crop), `media/camera/*` (removed), `media/crop/*` (fed from the camera's file), `AndroidManifest.xml` (no CAMERA permission; a FileProvider path for the capture), `res/xml/*`, `gradle/libs.versions.toml`, `app/build.gradle.kts` (CameraX removed), `app/proguard-smoke-test.pro`, `strings_media.xml` (both); tests under `app/src/test/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/media/`, `app/src/androidTest/` (CameraXFrameTest replaced, ReleaseSmokeTest adapted).
**Criteria:**
- "Take a photo" (cover and step) launches the system camera (`ACTION_IMAGE_CAPTURE` into a FileProvider URI with a write grant to that capture only); on a result the shot opens the existing 4:3 crop, with "Retake" relaunching the camera; a cancelled camera attaches nothing; the temp file is deleted after the crop or on cancel, and a leftover older than a day is cleared at start.
- The app declares no CAMERA permission and asks for none; CameraX and the in-app camera screen are removed; the camera ADR is superseded (the lead writes it).
- A shot's EXIF orientation is honoured by the crop; the stored photo carries no metadata (MEDIA-003), tested on the stored file.
- No camera app on the device: says so and points to the gallery.
- The release build still passes `scripts/device-test-release.sh`; the release APK size is reported.

### T-099 — A reference to part of an ingredient
**Status:** running
**Serves:** scenario 3 · **Core:** yes · **Depends on:** —
**Expected files:** `core/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/core/recipe/Recipe.kt` (`IngredientReference` gains an optional share), `core/.../recipe/StepText.kt`, `core/.../file/*` (the document carries the share; golden file updated), `book/*` (the `step_reference` table: a column, version bump, destructive fallback acceptable), `editor/StepTextField.kt` / the insert offer (choosing "whole" or "part" and entering the part), `card/StepText.kt` (the share scaled); strings (both); tests under `core/src/test/...`, `app/src/test/kotlin/app/recipefork/app/editor/`, `.../card/`, `.../file/`.
**Criteria:**
- Inserting a reference offers "whole" (as now) or "part"; a part is entered as a quantity in the ingredient's unit on its grid (DOM-017, read like the quantity field, DOM-019) and must not exceed the ingredient's quantity; "to taste" or a row with no quantity offers no part.
- A part reference renders its share at the card's scale with the same rounding grid and ↑/↓ marks as a whole one; the editor shows the authored share.
- Parts are independent of each other: their sum is not checked against the whole (an author may use garlic three ways).
- Editing the ingredient's quantity keeps each part as written (a part larger than the new whole is marked in the editor, not refused); changing its unit type drops parts to whole references with a statement.
- The share survives the book (reopened from disk), a fork, and export/import (format_version stays 1 — no old readers exist; the golden file is updated).
- Timer names derived from a step read a part as the card shows it.
