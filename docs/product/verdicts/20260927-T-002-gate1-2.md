**Failed check:** C3 — the behaviour the requirements state is not determinate for a
computed serving count between ¾ and one.
C1: five IDs named. C2: SCALE-002, SCALE-006, SCALE-008 and SCALE-010 are in
`spec/80-scaling-rules/10-scaling.md`, SCALE-015 in `spec/80-scaling-rules/20-display.md`;
`## Retired IDs` is empty and no `**Blocked by:**` line exists anywhere in `spec/`.
C3 stops: T-002 computes the approximate serving count a fixing produces. SCALE-008
displays a computed count below one as "the nearest of the fractions ¼, ⅓, ½, ⅔, ¾";
SCALE-015 displays "every computed serving count" as the nearest value of its grid, a
tie going up, and the section index's grid for the count is one set holding whole
numbers and the five fractions. Fixing 115 g of chicken authored at 500 g for 4 servings
computes 0.92: ≈¾ by the first, ≈1 by the second; 0.875 is ¾ by the first and, as a tie
going up, 1 by the second. No criterion exercises the range, so the developer would make
the product decision and gate 2 could not detect it (TEAM-008). Under the reading where
SCALE-015's grid below one computed serving is the five fractions alone the task would
pass; the index's single set and the scaling-extremes ADR's "no second grid" support the
other reading equally, so the reading is not the gate's to choose. Not `rejected`: the
entry copies the requirements as they are, and a corrected task would read the same.
The fixing/count interplay SD-003 left open is now decided by SCALE-016 and SCALE-017,
carried by T-030, which depends on T-002; SCALE-006 (3) releases the more recent of two
fixings and is determinate without them.
C4 and C5 were walked and do not change the verdict: every observable behaviour in the
entry is one of the eleven criteria below, each under its own ID; each matches `spec/`
verbatim once whitespace is normalised (SCALE-006 (1) now reads "the factor in force",
after the SD-011 correction); and the split (SCALE-002 (2), SCALE-006 (2), SCALE-008 (3)
→ T-020; SCALE-010 (1, 4) → T-001, (3) → T-029; SCALE-015 (1–2) → T-019, (3, 6) → T-001)
accounts for every criterion of the five requirements and agrees with the entries of
