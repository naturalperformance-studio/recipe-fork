**Failed check:** C3 — the behaviour the requirements state is not determinate where
this task's scale meets the serving count.
C1: four IDs named. C2: all four are in `spec/80-scaling-rules.md`, `## Retired IDs`
is empty, and none carries a `**Blocked by:**` line. C3 stops: T-002 builds the
fixed-ingredient scale in the core on top of T-001's serving-count scale and must
model releasing a fixing (SCALE-006 (3)). SCALE-001 displays quantities from the
requested count whenever the count is changed; SCALE-002, SCALE-006 and the glossary
have the most recently fixed ingredient set the scale and show its fixed amount
"exactly". With chicken fixed at 300 g of an authored 500 g and the count then set
to 6 of an authored 4, one requirement shows 750 g and the other 300 g, and nothing
says which state the reader is in. Releasing the only remaining fixing leaves a
scale no requirement names. The serving count's "nearest" has no tie rule, and 2.5
or 5/12 servings is reachable by fixing an amount. None is exercised by a criterion,
so the developer would make the product decision and gate 2 could not detect it
(TEAM-008). Not `rejected`: the task statement copies the requirements as they are,
and a corrected task would read the same.
C4 and C5 were walked before C3's finding was settled and do not change the
verdict: every observable behaviour in the entry is one of the nine criteria below,
each under its own ID; each matches `spec/80-scaling-rules.md` verbatim once
whitespace is normalised; and the split (SCALE-002 (2), SCALE-006 (2), SCALE-008 (3)
→ T-020; SCALE-010 (1, 3–4) → T-001) accounts for every criterion of the four
requirements.
