**Failed check:** C4 — a behaviour the task must produce is stated by no requirement.

(`server/src/main/resources/nickname/**`). Which words a nickname is made of — their
language and script, and which words or combinations may appear — is visible to every
user, and permanent once an account exists (DOM-001, RET-001). AUTH-018, DOM-001, the
glossary and the data model say only "three generated words", unique and permanent.
The task cannot be built without choosing the words, and no requirement makes that
choice. Class: omission, in the specification, not in the task: this costs T-005 no
return. The entry itself needs no change; once `/req-forge` answers SD-001, it comes
back for gate 1 against the new spec commit.
