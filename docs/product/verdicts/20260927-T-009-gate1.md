   (4): "the screen cannot be left by an ordinary back action"; AUTH-007 (7) and ADR
   20260921-nothing-exists-until-it-is-confirmed presume a person who leaves without

   creation, and where the person then lands are stated nowhere. Each reading is a
   different screen.
2. **The reroll control — SD-010 (ambiguity).** AUTH-018's heading, "may be rerolled
   once, before the account exists", reads either as a single reroll, contradicting
   the statement's "any number of times" and the unbounded criterion 2, or as the
   single moment at which rerolling is possible. Whether the control survives its
   first use is visible on the screen.
3. **The nickname shown — SD-001 (omission, existing; it already names T-009 as
   stopped).** The screen displays the generated nickname in both interface
   languages; which words, in which language and script, it shows is decided
   nowhere, and L10N-001 (1) cannot be judged for it at gate 2 until it is.
