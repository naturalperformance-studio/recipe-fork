**Failed check:** C3 — the task is narrower than the requirements state for the behaviour it builds.
T-021 displays a scaled, possibly rounded quantity inside a step. For such a quantity it
carries only that the reference "carries the same derived marker the list carries
(SCALE-004)" (FLOW-032 (3)) and equals the list "including its rounding and its derived
marker" (SCALE-007 (1)). The specification separates the marker from the direction:
SCALE-004's statement marks a derived quantity "and where rounding moved it away from the
exact computed value, ... show[s] the direction of that move", and SCALE-004 (4) has a
grid-exact quantity "marked as derived but carries no direction". The requirement that
obliges a direction wherever a rounded derived quantity is displayed is CON-004 — statement:
"Where the system displays a quantity it derived ... the system shall mark that quantity as
derived and, when the derivation rounded the value, show the direction of the rounding";
criterion (2): "Given a derived quantity that was rounded, when it is displayed, then the
interface states whether the displayed value is above or below the exact computed value."
A step reference at a scale other than the authored one is a quantity the reader sees that
is not the authored one (glossary, "Derived"), and CON-004 is binding on every section
(`spec/30-constraints.md`). T-021 names neither CON-004 nor its criterion (2); T-019 carries
CON-004 for the ingredient list only.

As sliced, gate 2 cannot tell a right build from a wrong one: a step that shows the marker
and no direction meets every criterion T-021 carries and fails CON-004 (2) unobserved; a
step that states the direction, as CON-004 (2) requires, shows behaviour none of T-021's
criteria asks for and meets the gate-2 sweep as an addition. FLOW-032 (3)'s own purpose
clause — "so the step never states a rounded number as though it were exact" — is not
served by the marker alone, since under SCALE-004 (4) a grid-exact derived number carries
the same marker without a direction.

The reading under which the task would pass — that "the interface states" is met by the
list line of the same ingredient elsewhere on the card — does not hold: CON-004 (2)'s
trigger is the derived quantity itself being displayed, and the card is one scrolling
screen, so the step's number is read where the list's statement is not. Choosing that
reading would be the gate narrowing a constraint.

Route, the Team Lead's: T-021 names CON-004 and copies criterion (2) verbatim (its
carried-elsewhere line then places CON-004 (1) and (3) at T-019), and returns to gate 1.
The direction stated in the step is then the list's, whatever `/req-forge` decides on
SD-012.
