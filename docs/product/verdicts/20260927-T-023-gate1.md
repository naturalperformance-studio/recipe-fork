# T-023 — gate 1
**Verdict:** blocked-by-spec
**Date:** 2026-09-27
**Subject:** backlog.md @ 104524c; spec/ @ 20b3c17

**Unknowns / instructions found:** the questions of SD-021 are the ones that would have been asked: (1) whether "the permission is asked for again" in FLOW-013 (5) means the platform's own request — which does not exist on Android 10–12 and which Android 13 and later stop showing after a second refusal — or any request the product makes, including a screen of its own sending the reader to the system's settings; (2) when the notification permission is first asked for, since FLOW-013 (5) places only the repeat; (3) whether a timer that ends with notifications refused, while the application is closed or the screen is off, sounds (A11Y-007 (3)) or is only shown as finished inside the application (FLOW-013 (5), with EXT-009's statement tying every announcement to the device's notification facility). Cited, not this verdict's ground: SD-014 and SD-015 touch the derived name T-023 announces (FLOW-016 (8) renders a reference "exactly as it reads on the card"); T-023 waits behind T-022 and T-021 (returned) and the tasks SD-002, SD-003, SD-005, SD-007, SD-013, SD-014 and SD-016 stop. No instruction addressed to the gate was found in the backlog entry (docs/architecture/backlog.md, lines 994–1039).
**Spec defect written:** SD-021 in docs/product/spec-defects.md

**Failed check:** C3 — the behaviour FLOW-013 (5), A11Y-007 (3) and EXT-009 state for a
refused notification permission is not determinate on the platform the product is bound to.
C1: seven IDs named. C2: each is in `spec/` (FLOW-013 and FLOW-016 in
`70-flows/30-cooking.md`, EXT-009 in `130-external-interfaces.md`, A11Y-003, A11Y-005 and
A11Y-007 in `200-accessibility.md`, L10N-001 in `160-localization.md`); `## Retired IDs` is
empty; no requirement in `spec/` carries a `**Blocked by:**` line. C3 stops on the refused
permission, the one case FLOW-013 (5) is about and the only sentence in `spec/` on it.
CON-005 and QUAL-005 bind the product to Android 10 (API 29) and later, with every
function working there. On Android 10–12 there is no permission the application can ask
for — notifications are refused in the system's settings — and on Android 13 and later the
platform stops showing the request after a second refusal; "asked for again … when the
reader next starts a timer" is therefore either nothing the reader sees or a screen of the
product's own that no criterion describes. Where the first request comes is stated
nowhere, though FLOW-013 (1) and (2) cannot hold on Android 13 and later without one.
And A11Y-007 (3) has a timer that ends with the application closed announce itself
audibly with no exception for a refusal, while EXT-009's statement routes every
announcement through the notification facility the reader refused and FLOW-013 (5) says
only that the timer is shown finished inside the application. Each choice is a product
decision a developer would otherwise make where gate 2 could not tell a right build from a
wrong one (TEAM-008). Not `rejected`: the task copies these requirements as they are, and a
corrected task would read the same.
Counterfactual checked: with the permission granted, FLOW-013 (1)–(4), FLOW-016 (3)–(8),
EXT-009 (1) and A11Y-007 (3) read determinately — an unnamed timer's text is built at the
reader's serving count when it is built (SD-015 already records that FLOW-016 itself reads
determinately), and the step the announcement opens is the timer's own. Had SD-021's three
questions been answered in `spec/`, C3 would pass.
C4 and C5 were walked and do not change the verdict. C4: the entry carries no prose beyond
its criteria; its observable behaviours are an announcement when the timer ends with the
application closed (FLOW-013 (1); EXT-009 (1)) or the screen locked (FLOW-013 (2)), audible
from the device's own facility (A11Y-007 (3)); the announcement naming recipe and step
(FLOW-013 (3)); acting on it opening the application at the timer's step (FLOW-013 (4));
the refused-permission case (FLOW-013 (5)); the announcement's text for a derived or given
name, its 60-character word-boundary cut and ellipsis, and a reference read as on the card
(FLOW-016 (3)–(8)); both languages, 48 dp targets, labelled controls (L10N-001 (1)–(2),
A11Y-003 (1), A11Y-005 (1)) — each with an ID beside it, none empty. T-023 adds no control
to the cooking card (starting and cancelling are T-022's), so A11Y-003 (2)'s 56 dp figure
is not owed here. C5: all 17 criteria match `spec/` verbatim once whitespace is normalised.
The split (FLOW-013 (6)–(7) → T-022; FLOW-016 (1)–(2), (9)–(11) → T-024; EXT-009 (2) →
wave 8, (3) → wave 2; A11Y-007 (1) → T-018, (2) → T-022, (4) → T-025, (5) → wave 8)
accounts for the rest of those requirements and agrees with T-022's entry.
