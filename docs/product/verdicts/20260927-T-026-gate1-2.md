T-026 is "Writing a recipe asks for an account first", and every criterion it carries about the offer fires "when they use a control that needs one" (ACT-001 (3), AUTH-001 (2), FLOW-003 (6)). For a person with no account, `spec/` states exactly one control that writes a recipe: FLOW-042 (3) — "Given a catalogue holding no recipe the reader may see, when it is opened with no query and no filter, then it states that there is nothing in it yet and offers to write a recipe." The entry does not name FLOW-042, and the backlog places FLOW-042 (3) in wave 2 (coverage map, line 1587); wave 1 has no catalogue. The other write control, FLOW-042 (4)'s offer in the own-recipes view, is "Given an account that has written nothing", and FLOW-034 (7), carried in wave 1 by T-007, shows a device holding no account "the view of marked recipes alone and no switch". So in the wave this task is sliced into, a person with no account is shown no control that writes a recipe. The task can then be built in only two ways, and neither is the behaviour its requirements state: it adds a write control for account-less devices somewhere in `library/**` or `ui/nav/**` — a control no named requirement states, which is C4's finding — or it adds none, and its offer criteria have no trigger a gate-2 walk could fire. The counterfactual reading under which it passes — that a wave-1 screen already shows an account-less person a write control — does not hold, for the three reasons just given.

Also found, for the re-slice: AUTH-001 (2), carried here verbatim, ends "for a muted account, as AUTH-004 (5) states", and on this task's trigger that is AUTH-004 (5)'s first half ("when it was created because the person asked to write … then that action proceeds on the device at once"). Gate 2 of this task will walk AUTH-001 (2) through that reference either way; the entry does not say that the write half of AUTH-004 (5) is observed here.

What would clear it is the Team Lead's to choose, and both are slicing, not product decisions: the entry carries FLOW-042 (3) and moves to the wave that builds the catalogue, or its trigger is otherwise tied to a control a named, live requirement states for a person with no account.
