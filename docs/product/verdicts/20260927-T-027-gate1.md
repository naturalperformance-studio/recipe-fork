`**Blocked by:**` line. C3 stops on FLOW-008. The task builds the server's handling of
every late edit a device sends, so it must decide which edits are "divergent". The
statement makes divergence an edit on two devices ("edits one recipe on two devices …
so that the local version and the version the server holds have diverged"); criterion
(4) glosses its absence as "device B's version is the one the server already holds",
which makes any difference a divergence. The two readings part on one reachable case:
B's offline edit arrives for a recipe that was submitted and became `pending`,
`link_shared` or `published` without its content changing. Under the gloss, criterion
(3) turns B's edit into a `private` fork; under the statement FLOW-008 does not fire,
CON-003 (1) rejects the edit, and no requirement says what becomes of B's change or
what the owner is told. Both satisfy CON-003 (1), so gate 2 could not tell which was
built, and the developer would be making the product decision (TEAM-008). Not
`rejected`: the task copies the requirements as they are, and a corrected task would
read the same.

For the re-gate, and not a verdict on them: the seven criteria were compared with
`spec/` after whitespace normalisation and all seven match verbatim; the title's "keeps
an account's drafts" is the server holding the version FLOW-008 (1, 4) and ACT-002 (1)
presuppose, not a behaviour of its own.
