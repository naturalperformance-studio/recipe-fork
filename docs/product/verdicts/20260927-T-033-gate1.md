Unknowns, recorded as the gate's readings for gate 2 and not as grounds of this verdict: (1) **Whether a timer that ends with notifications refused is heard while the application is open.** FLOW-013 (5) says only that the timer "is shown as finished inside the application" and exempts the closed and locked cases, and the explanation of (10) speaks only of those two. The ADR `20260927-the-card-survives-the-process.md` rejects "Sound the timer by another facility when notifications are refused", and EXT-009's statement drives every announcement from the device's notification facility. The gate reads this to mean that, with notifications refused, the finished state is shown and nothing sounds, whether the application is in the foreground or not. At gate 2, a sound or vibration produced by some other path when notifications are refused would be an addition. (2) **Leaving the explanation.** FLOW-013 (9) has the explanation shown "before the reader returns to the recipe", so a way back to the recipe is part of the explanation. The only other control the specification names is the one that opens the settings ((10), (13)). A further control, such as "don't show again", would be an addition at the gate-2 sweep, and it would break (10), which shows the explanation on every timer start while notifications are not permitted. (3) **Dismissing the platform's request without answering it.** The gate reads this as a refusal under (9), because the ADR `20260927-a-refusal-is-explained-at-once.md` states that "every reader who ends up without notifications has been told so at the timer where it happened". (4) **Target sizes.** A11Y-003 (2) lists the cooking card's controls as "the timer controls and the serving control". The settings control and the way back in the explanation are measured against A11Y-003 (1)'s 48 dp. Any timer control on the card that this task adds or changes is measured against 56 dp. (5) For the Team Lead, not a gate finding: T-033 depends on T-023, and through that chain it waits behind SD-028, SD-029, SD-030, SD-031 and SD-032. All five are open, and each names T-033 in its `Stopped:` line as waiting through dependencies. None of them names a requirement this task carries as defective. They are cited here, not restated. This verdict releases the task statement only. It does not release the task past those defects.
