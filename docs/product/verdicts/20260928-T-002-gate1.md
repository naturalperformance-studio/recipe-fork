**Failed check:** C3 — two criteria of SCALE-008 carried by the entry cannot both hold
with SCALE-002's statement, so the behaviour the task is to build is not determinate.
SCALE-002: the factor is "that amount divided by the authored amount of the same
ingredient", applied to the whole recipe, and "the resulting serving count" is
displayed. SCALE-015 (4), in the same entry, walks that arithmetic: 250 g fixed against
an authored 400 g for 4 servings is 2.5 servings, shown ≈3.
- SCALE-008 (7), as replaced at spec/ @ 1168954: "Given a recipe authored for 4 servings
  with 400 g of chicken, when the reader fixes 350 g of it (a computed 0.875 servings,
  exactly between ¾ and 1), then the serving count shows an approximate 1 serving." By
  SCALE-002, 350 g ÷ 400 g = 0.875 is the factor, and the resulting count is
  4 × 0.875 = 3.5 servings: a tie between 3 and 4 in the serving-count set, shown ≈4 by
  SCALE-015 and SCALE-008 (4). The criterion's parenthesis gives the factor as the count,
  and its *Then* (≈1) is met only by an implementation that does not follow SCALE-002.
  The correction note under SCALE-008 ("It now reaches the same 0.875 servings with
  amounts on the grid; the behaviour is unchanged") repeats the same figure.
- SCALE-008 (8): "Given the same recipe, when the reader fixes 100 g of chicken (a
  computed 0.8 servings), then the serving count shows an approximate ¾ serving." Before
  1168954 "the same recipe" could only be the 500 g recipe of criterion (6), and
  100 ÷ 500 × 4 = 0.8. Now the nearest antecedent is criterion (7)'s 400 g recipe, where
  100 ÷ 400 × 4 = 1.0 serving, shown ≈1 and not ≈¾. Under the other reading, (6)'s 500 g,
  the criterion holds as written.
A test asserting (7) as written fails against a SCALE-002-conformant core, and one
asserting the arithmetic fails (7)'s *Then*; gate 2 could mark neither met. Choosing
which half of (7) is meant, and which recipe (8) names, is the product decision
(TEAM-008), and C5 obliges the entry to copy these words, so rejecting T-002 would return
it unchanged.
Counterfactual walked: the task would pass C3 if (7)'s recipe, amount, computed count
and *Then* agreed with one another under SCALE-002, and (8) named one recipe under which
its own figures hold; neither is so at spec/ @ 21b80b2.
