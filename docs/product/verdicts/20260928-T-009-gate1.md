on it at gate 2, it goes to the defect ledger then.

person with no account active", and AUTH-026 (3) shows the pool "whenever no account

reach this task. Whether the recipe book's statement and offer also appear on the

AUTH-001 (6) and by the FLOW-003 criterion that T-026 carries (backlog.md line 1773).

criterion in this task observes. How that is handled before T-026 is a slicing
question for the Team Lead.

recipe book's files". T-022's expected files include `AppContainer.kt` but none of
`library/**`. That is ordering and belongs to the Team Lead; it is not a ground of
this verdict.

**Failed check:** C3. The task's behaviour is narrower than a criterion it carries,
and the trigger that criterion needs is unreachable on this branch.
LEG-004 (1) (backlog.md line 1429): "Given account creation (AUTH-007), when the
account is created, then the terms are presented and accepted there — on its first
step, before any key is shown (AUTH-024) — and the accepted version is recorded with
the account." Its *When* is the account being created, and its *Then* ends in the
accepted version being recorded with that account. T-009 builds the first of the
three steps and nothing after it. Under AUTH-024 (4) (spec/45-auth/20-account-key.md
line 104), creation completes only when the third step is confirmed. That step is
T-010, which depends on T-032, which depends on T-009. So on T-009's branch no
account can be created through the product, and the criterion's *Then* cannot be
observed. Gate 2 would then have two options, and both fail:
- A test that records the version without a created account observes something
  other than this *Then*. Under AUTH-007 (7), nothing of an unfinished attempt is
  kept anywhere, so that recording would itself be behaviour the spec does not
  state.
- A build that completes creation from the first step would show behaviour that
  AUTH-024 (4) and AUTH-007 do not state.

The earlier gate on this task recorded the same point as an unknown and left it to
gate 2 (docs/product/verdicts/20260927-T-009-gate1-2.md, unknown (2)). The re-slice
of 2026-09-28 gave AUTH-024 (1) a trigger inside this task: the offer of FLOW-051
(7). It did not do the same for LEG-004 (1). Since then, the gate has held that a
criterion whose trigger cannot be set up on the task's own branch fails C3
(docs/product/verdicts/20260928-T-008-gate1.md). That verdict was `blocked-by-spec`
only because no correct form of that task existed. Here a correct form exists.
The part of LEG-004 (1) that T-009 can observe is that the terms are presented and
accepted on the first step, before any key is shown. AUTH-024 (1) and (2) already
carry exactly that in this entry. The whole of LEG-004 (1) can be observed in the
task where creation completes. Where it goes is slicing, and it is the Team Lead's.
This is not a spec defect: `spec/` states the behaviour in full, and only its
placement in the backlog cannot be gated.

Counterfactual: T-009 would pass if its branch could create an account through the
product as `spec/` states, or if the carried text of LEG-004 (1) could be observed
before creation. Neither is true, for the reasons above.
