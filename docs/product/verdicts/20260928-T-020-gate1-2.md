**Failed check:** C3. The fixing control this task adds must turn what the reader types
into an amount. How it reads the typed text is determined by no requirement.
C1: nine IDs named. C2: SCALE-002, -006, -008 and -011 are in
`spec/80-scaling-rules/10-scaling.md`, SCALE-009 is in `spec/80-scaling-rules/20-display.md`,
A11Y-003, A11Y-005 and A11Y-007 are in `spec/200-accessibility.md`, and L10N-001 is in
`spec/160-localization.md`. All are live.
C3 stops on the control. The entry's title and its note ("the fixing control this task
adds") place the control in T-020. SCALE-002's statement computes the factor from "the
available amount" the reader fixes, so the control must produce an amount from what was
entered before any of this task's criteria can be reached. SCALE-018 is the only
requirement that says a fixed amount is typed. Its criteria enter `0,3 кг` and
`109,375 г`, and it defers the value to DOM-017. L10N-009 fixes how a number is
*displayed* in each language, and nothing states how a *typed* one is read (SD-037
item 1). T-020 carries L10N-001 (1), so the control exists in both interfaces. Removing
SCALE-018 from the entry removes the grid bound. The reading stays, because it belongs
to the control. Paths still reachable on T-020's card:
- English interface, a recipe authored for 4 servings with 500 g of chicken; the reader
  fixes `1,500 kg`. Under English grouping it is 1500 kg (a factor of 3000). Under a
  decimal comma it is 1.5 kg (a factor of 3). Both lie on the grid, so the bound in T-039
  does not tell them apart either. The card shows two different recipes.
- Russian interface, the same recipe; the reader fixes `0.3 кг`. The requirements do not
  say whether this is 300 g or not a number.
A developer of T-020 would settle these in the control's parser, and gate 2 could mark a
test of `1,500 kg` or `0.3 кг` neither met nor not met (TEAM-008).
The split also creates a second gap. With SCALE-018 out, the entry states no rule for
what the control accepts until T-039 lands. Whatever the developer picks is a decision
nobody made. Accepting only whole numbers of the authored unit is narrower than SCALE-018
(1). Accepting anything is wider than its "shall accept nothing else". Either behaviour
could be seen on `develop`, and neither maps to a requirement this entry names.
Counterfactual walked. The task would pass C3 if its control read no typed text, or if a
requirement T-020 names stated how a typed amount is read. Neither holds. Not `rejected`:
no rewording of the entry passes. Putting SCALE-018 back brings back
docs/product/verdicts/20260928-T-020-gate1.md. Stating an interim input rule in the entry
would be a product rule written by the Team Lead, a C4 addition. Either way the task
would come back unchanged and spend one of its returns on a defect that is not its own.
`blocked-by-spec` is not a return; returns stay at 0 of 2.
Stopped: T-020 (the fixing control's reading of a typed amount), and T-039 (SCALE-018),
which its own note already holds back until SD-037 is answered. T-021, T-022, T-023,
T-034, T-033, T-024 and T-025 wait behind T-020 through the backlog's dependencies.
Whether they wait is the Team Lead's call. T-035 is already stopped by the same entry.
