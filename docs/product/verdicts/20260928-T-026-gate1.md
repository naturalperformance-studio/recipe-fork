**Failed check:** C3 — on the task's own trigger, what happens to the recipe being sent is not determinate when the person declines AUTH-026's offer.
T-026's trigger is a recipe written with no account active, so a recipe in the pool (AUTH-026, FLOW-051), whose owner chooses to send it. FLOW-059 (1) says account creation is offered and "the recipe is sent once the account exists, becoming that account's". Creating the account makes it active on a device whose pool holds at least this recipe. So AUTH-026 (1) shows the offer to move the whole pool, and T-036, which is gated, builds exactly that offer on creation.
- If the offer is accepted, the whole pool becomes the account's, the recipe is the account's (ACT-002 (2)), and FLOW-059 (1) holds.
- If the offer is declined, AUTH-026 (3) says "nothing from the pool is in" the account's book. ACT-002's statement gives a pool recipe "no owner until the pool is moved into an account" and "no other means of transferring ownership". ACT-002 (2) says a pool recipe "can be sent only by" the account the pool moved into. FLOW-059 (1), AUTH-001 (2) and ACT-001 (3), all carried here, say the send proceeds and the recipe becomes the account's.
Whoever builds the task would decide which holds: the recipe is moved alone, the send stops, or the offer is not shown on this trigger. Gate 2 could not tell a right outcome from a wrong one (TEAM-008). Choosing among them is the product decision, and none is chosen here (PO-006). Route: the user runs `/req-forge` on SD-040. Stopped: T-026.

Counterfactual: the entry would pass C3 if `spec/` stated what a declined pool offer does to the recipe whose sending caused the account to be created. It does not state it in FLOW-059, AUTH-026, ACT-002, FLOW-051, the glossary entry "Pool", or either ADR of 2026-09-28.
