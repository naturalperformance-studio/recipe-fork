**Where the behaviour is produced.** `core/src/main/kotlin/app/recipefork/core/scaling/ScaledRecipe.kt` holds the fixings in the order they were made, one per ingredient. The factor in force is the last fixing's amount over its authored amount, both in the line's base unit. With nothing fixed, the factor is the requested count's. Every line is displayed through `Scaling.display` from that exact factor. A fixed ingredient other than the latest carries a `Shortfall` when its exact requirement at the factor in force exceeds its fixed amount. The serving count is the requested one, not approximate, while nothing is fixed. Otherwise it is the factor times the authored count, taken by `RoundingGrid.SERVING_COUNT` and marked approximate. That grid (`RoundingGrid.kt`) uses ¼, ⅓, ½, ⅔, ¾ below one and whole numbers from one up, with the nearest value chosen, a tie going to the larger, and a floor of ¼.

**Sweep for observable behaviour no criterion asked for.** Nothing in the diff is visible to a user, because no screen, string, stored field or control is added. Five things were checked because each could surface later:
- A requested count is shown as asked and not approximate while nothing is fixed. That is SCALE-008 (3) and SCALE-011 (4).
- Releasing the last fixing returns to the requested count. That is SCALE-017, carried by T-030.
- A fixing in kilograms is read as grams. That is SCALE-018 (1).
- The core refuses a fixing of zero or below, one in a unit of another dimension or another household unit, and one on a `taste_unmeasured` ingredient. Those are SCALE-018 (3), (5), (6) and (7). The control that holds or refuses the amount is T-020's.
- Re-fixing replaces the earlier fixing and becomes the latest. That is SCALE-006, as argued in unknown 4(b).

None is an addition.
