**Failed check:** C3 — on the task's own trigger, when the account is created muted it is not determinate whether the pool recipe whose sending caused the creation becomes that account's.
T-026 carries AUTH-004 (5): "Given a muted account, when it was created because the person asked to send a recipe or to rate, then AUTH-006 states at that moment that the action cannot proceed and why." So the task's trigger can create a muted account, and on that path the recipe is not sent. The account is still created while the pool holds that recipe, so AUTH-026 (1) shows the pool offer. The task's carried criteria then read two ways:
- The recipe becomes the account's. ACT-002's statement gives it an owner when "the recipe's own sending creates an account". AUTH-026 (3) keeps "a recipe whose sending created the account" in the book. AUTH-026 (4) has the offer state that "this recipe is already in the account whatever the answer".
- The recipe stays in the pool. FLOW-059 (1) ties "becoming that account's" to "the recipe is sent", and ACT-002 (3)'s Given is "a recipe in the pool sent". Under the glossary, "Sent" means sent to the server, which a muted account never does (AUTH-004). The ADR's stated reason is that the act "send this recipe" is "carried out as asked". The ADR does not mention a muted account.

What a user sees differs between the two. If the offer is declined, the recipe is either in the muted account's book or in the pool. The offer either tells a muted account that the recipe "is already in the account" or does not. The same question arises for a full account whose sending the server refuses after creation, because FLOW-003 says "nothing changes on the device". Whoever builds the task would choose between the readings, and gate 2 could not tell a right outcome from a wrong one (TEAM-008). Choosing between them is the product decision, and the gate makes no choice here (PO-006). Route: the user runs `/req-forge` on SD-042. Stopped: T-026.

Counterfactual: the entry would pass C3 if `spec/` stated whether the recipe becomes the new account's when that account sends nothing. The gate looked for such a statement in FLOW-059, ACT-002, AUTH-026, AUTH-004, AUTH-005, AUTH-006, FLOW-051, the actors table, the glossary entries "Pool", "Sent" and "Muted account", and the ADRs of 2026-09-28 and 2026-09-29, and found none.
