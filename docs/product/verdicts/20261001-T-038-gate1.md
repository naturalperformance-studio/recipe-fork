The ADR `20261001-a-sending-with-no-revision-is-made-from-the-first.md` rejects exactly that outcome for a first sending ("the owner gets a copy of their own recipe and a notice about an ordinary edit") and states its no-cost reasoning for a first sending only. It is for the gates of T-041, T-042 and T-046, where the server chooses the answer. The user may want to look at it in `/req-forge` before then.
