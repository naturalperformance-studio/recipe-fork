**Failed check:** C3. The entry has the check select a scrolling container and measure it "across its scroll direction only" (backlog.md:504). Under one reading of A11Y-003 that is narrower than the requirement. Under the other it is exactly the requirement. `spec/` supports both readings:
- *Reading 1: 48 by 48, like every interactive element.* The statement says: "The system shall give every interactive element a touch target of at least 48 by 48 density-independent pixels". The definition counts "any container that scrolls" as an interactive element (spec/200-accessibility.md:69–75). Criterion 1 measures each interactive element at "at least 48 by 48 dp, whatever the element looks like" (lines 78–79). Criterion 5 sets the direction across the scroll but lifts nothing. Under this reading, a list that scrolls down, 200 dp wide and 40 dp tall, fails, and the entry's "only" lets it pass.
- *Reading 2: across the scroll direction only.* The ADR's Decision says a container that scrolls "is measured across its scroll direction". The Verification line says "include every scrolling container across its scroll direction" (lines 97–99). Criterion 5 would add nothing if criterion 1 already applied to such a container in both directions. Under this reading, the same list passes, and the entry is faithful.

The check this task changes is the one that carries A11Y-003 (1) onto every screen (backlog.md:484, 505), and its self-tests have to fix one reading. A developer cannot write the scrolling-container rule without choosing, and gate 2 could not call either choice right or wrong (TEAM-008).

This is not `rejected`. Under reading 2 the task would pass as written. Under reading 1 it would pass without "only". No word in `spec/` says which reading holds, and choosing between them is the product decision. Returning the entry would spend one of its two returns (TL-011) on a question the Team Lead cannot answer either. Class: ambiguity. Route: `/req-forge`.
