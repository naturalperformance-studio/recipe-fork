# Vision audit — 2026-10-04

Produced by `/req-forge --audit` over the whole of `spec/` at commit `ce0e85a`
(synced through v0.21; plans of v0.22–v0.24 read). Nothing in the vision was
changed by the audit. This file is a worklist, not a source: requirements stay in
their sections, and an item here is closed by editing the section it names.

## 1. Contradictions

1. **The book's view: kept vs reset.** FLOW-034 (5) keeps the view last left on the
   device across sessions; FLOW-058 (1–3, 5) and its verification reset it to the
   default in a new session; ADR `20260927-a-session-is-ended-by-the-reader`
   rejects "every choice is kept for good". → Drop the view from FLOW-058; mark the
   ADR narrowed.
2. **One timer per step still in live text** (against DOM-024): FLOW-027 Source
   ("One timer per step, because…") and its verification ("try to attach a
   second"); GUIDE-007 (4) "the timer it already had"; FLOW-021 "a step whose timer
   is already running"; GUIDE-004 item 1 assumes one wait per step. → Pluralise,
   delete the stale rationale.
3. **FLOW-053 still argues for confirmation.** The statement is immediate deletion
   with undo, offered on the card; its Source and the 2026-09-27 correction still
   justify a statement before deletion and keeping deletion off the card bar.
   → Delete both passages.
4. **The note vs "three fields".** FLOW-023: "exactly three fields… no fourth
   field"; DOM-023 adds a 60-character note. → "three fields plus an optional note".
5. **FORK-001 starting state.** Statement: "a new private recipe"; criterion 7 and
   ST-004: `local`. → `local`.
6. **FLOW-007 hides the parent from its owner.** FLOW-007 (1, 3): the fork never
   names a parent; FORK-005 (5), 2026-10-02: the owner sees it in their own book.
   → "to anyone but its owner".
7. **FLOW-008 (8) narrower than FLOW-067 (3).** Read literally, FLOW-008 forks a
   case FLOW-067 says is not forked. → FLOW-008 defers to FLOW-067 (or vanishes
   with the collapse in 2.2).
8. **Ingredient offer order.** ADR `20260927-the-ingredient-field` puts leaves
   before the parent; `20261004-synonyms-and-the-exact-match-first` puts the exact
   match first and names only the exact-label ADR as superseded. Inside FLOW-057
   the "exactly the label" rule suppresses free text on a label only, a criterion
   also on a synonym. → Pointer on the older ADR; "label or synonym" in FLOW-057.
9. **Where synonyms apply.** DOM-021: a synonym finds the entry wherever the label
   would; FLOW-035's list of what the book's query matches omits synonyms. → Pick
   one.
10. **Language filter.** L10N-005 requires it in the book "as in the catalogue
    (FLOW-035)"; FLOW-035 has none. → Add it or narrow L10N-005.
11. **MEDIA-010 predates device-first.** It refuses media when added (criteria 1,
    6, 7); MEDIA-011 (4): adding on the device needs no allowance. → Restate as a
    refusal of the sending.
12. **Done marks.** MVP-002 lists "a record of what is done" as decided against;
    FLOW-068 adds done marks. → Drop that phrase from MVP-002.
13. **MVP-001 superseded but live.** Still "shall implement every requirement in
    the closed sections" and a "ready for handoff" banner; the index row describes
    MVP as the first-release boundary; LEG intro cites the banner and "define before
    MVP"; GOAL-003 and CON-005 still say "MVP". → Retire MVP-001, keep MVP-002 as
    the decided-against list and MVP-003 as a store-release checklist, reword.
14. **Banner vs ledger.** Banner: "0 next, 40 later". Ledger: 25 resolved, 15 open;
    OQ-016, OQ-029, OQ-030 still "define before MVP", ten entries "define after
    MVP". → "0 next, 15 later"; reclassify legacy entries as `later`.
15. **Data model vs reporting and the pool without an account.** Report.`reporter`,
    Favourite and Block are typed `Account`; ABUSE-002 and the no-account pool say
    otherwise; the glossary "Report" says "one account". → `Account or none`.
16. **`piece_weight`.** DOM: "used to keep scaling smooth", inherited by FORK-001;
    DOM and SCALE elsewhere: "no weight is ever… used in scaling"; no requirement
    sets it. → Drop the field and "held internally in grams".
17. **Replacement of a never-published recipe.** FORK-008 allows declaring a
    replacement of a frozen `link_shared` recipe; FORK-009 names it as parent;
    FORK-005 never names a never-published parent. → Limit FORK-008 to a recipe
    that has been published, or state the case in FORK-009.
18. **Serverless and import vs moderation and forking.** ADR
    `20260920-forking-published-only`: "every other fork is rejected" — serverless
    forks of found recipes contradict it. ADR `20260928-serverless-mode-and-recipe-files`:
    "everything… passes through moderation" — a TikTok import publishes at once

19. **ADR statuses behind the index.** Index notes supersession, the file does not:

## 3. Missing core

1. **The safety flag never reaches device-held forks.** MOD-008 and ADR
   `danger-travels-down-the-chain` flag every descendant at any depth; since
   device-first a fork not yet sent, or made later from a cached copy, is unknown
   to the server and nothing flags it when sent. Proposal: on any sending, the
   server sets the flag if any published ancestor was removed for `health_risk`
   and not cleared.
2. **No "an edit is never lost" invariant.** It lives only in rationale. Missing:
   on the device, what the editor showed as entered survives a crash or a full
   device; and the server holds no copy of what was never sent — losing the device
   loses it.
3. **Device-first and serverless survival are not in GOAL or CON**, though the
   index calls them defining; they live in ADRs, FLOW-002 and FLOW-061.
4. **No goal states what success or "done" means**, though ANL-008 cites the
   owner's measure (forks made by others).
5. **One-person operation is not a constraint**, though MOD-013, ABUSE-001, OBS and
   QUAL-010 rest on it. Related: losing the only moderator leaves `reported_hidden`
   with no exit.
6. **State-machine holes.** The muted account is not in the account machine
   (only `active`, `publishing_banned`); the submit guard omits FLOW-006 and
   ABUSE-001; a ban arriving while a submission is `pending`; submitting a recipe
   that holds a `removed` dictionary entry (DOM-009); which wins when an entry's
   divisibility is set after the author answered.
7. **FORK-001's inheritance list is incomplete:** declared language (L10N-004 says
   inherited, FORK does not); `safety_flag` and `replaces_parent` on a fork; a fork
   of a recipe file always loses media and description (FLOW-065) even for the
   author's own recipe from another device.
8. **Delete with undo** has no rule for process death, restart or a lost
   connection while the notice shows.
9. **In-app account deletion** has no requirement of its own (where, what proof),
   though RET-004/005 and EXT-005 assume it and the store requires it.
10. **Licence basis for forking authorless recipes** (imports, found files):
    LEG-002 rests on the author's grant. `later`.
11. **Small gaps.** No limit on a group name; the note's 60 characters are not in
    DOM-012; a running timer across a device restart; where done marks live and
    what happens to them on deletion or an account switch.
