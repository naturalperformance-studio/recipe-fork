# A retry restores the control and performs nothing

**Status:** accepted, completes AUTH-005 and AUTH-006

**Context:** A muted account reaches AUTH-005 through the retry control on the
statement that stands where a withheld control would be (AUTH-006). AUTH-005's
first criterion retried from the statement in place of the fork control, which
the same day's decision had removed by giving a muted account its fork back
(AUTH-004 (3)), and nothing said what a successful retry does to the action the
statement stood for. The re-check of SD-019 found it open. Two of the withheld
actions cannot simply "proceed": a rating has no value until like or dislike is
chosen, and a submission is irreversible and must first pass GUIDE-001.

**Decision:** A retry that makes the account full puts every withheld control back
in place of its statement and performs no withheld action. The person uses the
control as any full account does.

**Consequences:** One more tap after a successful retry. In exchange no rating is
guessed and no submission bypasses the statement that makes it irreversible, and
one rule covers every withheld control — media, submission, private link, rating.

**Alternatives considered:**
- *The action proceeds at once.* Rejected: undefined for a rating, and for a
  submission it would either skip GUIDE-001 or need that statement inserted after
  the retry as a special case.
